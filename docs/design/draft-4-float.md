# DRAFT 4: Float

An 11-sector "patient compounder" idle city whose loyalty balance is delivered monthly as fixed-ratio stock rewards through licensed rails (USDG-to-Robinhood-Wallet self-directed into Stock Tokens in the EU/ROW, broker-journaled ETF fractions in the US), with a consumptive FLOAT utility token living on Robinhood Chain that is deliberately never redeemable for, or multiplied into, stock.

# Float — architecture proposal (Robinhood Chain-native lens)

Date of record 2026-09-01. Facts cited as F-numbers (brief) or `file.md #n` (research finding), `file.md S1/S2-missed` (skeptic missed facts). Anything requiring Robinhood cooperation is tagged **PARTNER** with a **SOLO** fallback.

## 1. Concept and theme

**Name:** Float. **Token:** FLOAT (ERC-20, Robinhood Chain, chain ID 4663). **Tagline:** "Build the city. Collect the dividend. Never trade."

**Player fantasy: the patient compounder.** You run a holding company that owns a city laid out as the 11 GICS sectors, each a district: Main Street (Staples), the Mall (Discretionary), the Yards (Industrials), Bank Row (Financials), the Campus (Tech), the Clinic (Health), the Field (Energy), the Quarry (Materials), the Grid (Utilities), the Blocks (Real Estate), the Tower (Comms). Every business is fictional and every number is in-game Cash. Growth comes from reinvestment (compounding), diversification (sector synergies) and going public (prestige). There is no buy/sell at a moving price, no chart, no ticker feed, no timing decision anywhere in the game.

**How it ties to real stocks without encouraging trading (F7, legal-eu.md #18-19):** Massachusetts fined Robinhood over confetti, scratch-off stock lotteries, tap games and referral stock (F7); the SEC's DEP list names games, streaks, prizes and free stock (legal-us.md #9). Float has none of the flagged patterns: no chance with value (C1), no celebration tied to any real transaction, no referral rewards with value (Apple 3.1.5(v), legal-us.md #31), no notifications about real assets, no leaderboards of real holdings, no prices of real securities in the client. Real stocks appear exactly once: on **Dividend Day**, the first trading day of each month, the loyalty balance a player earned is delivered as a fixed-ratio reward by a licensed party (section 4). The game teaches "hold and reinvest"; it never rewards frequency of anything financial. Copy follows Robinhood Chain ToS (robinhood.md S1-missed): we say "Stock Tokens", never "tokenized stocks"; our brand is primary; "Built on Robinhood Chain" is the only chain mark.

**Thin client over verifiable state, where it helps.** The simulation is a normal server-authoritative game (Cloudflare Durable Objects running the same deterministic TypeScript sim as the client, break_eternity.js numerics). Only what benefits from public verifiability lives on Robinhood Chain: season results (Merkle roots), FLOAT emission and claims, FLOAT sinks, sponsor-drop escrow and distribution lists, monthly payout roots, and team/treasury vesting via Sablier (deployed on 4663, tech.md #35). No PII on-chain (EDPB v2.0, F17); the Seat registry stores only an opaque id and a wallet address.

## 2. Core loop and progression

**Currencies (five, one job each):** Cash (soft, closed-loop, never withdrawable); Shareholders (prestige 1); Index Weight (prestige 2); Gold Bars (hard, IAP, never expire per Apple 3.1.1); Dividend Ledger (real, cents) and FLOAT (on-chain) — the last two never affect Cash production.

**Generators:** 44 (11 sectors x 4). `cost = base * r^owned`, `production = rate * owned * multipliers` (idle-design.md #1). r rises by sector: 1.07, 1.08, 1.09, 1.10, 1.11, 1.12, 1.13, 1.14, 1.15, 1.15, 1.15. Sector unlocks at lifetime Cash 0, 1e3, 1e5, 1e7, 1e9, 1e12, 1e15, 1e18, 1e21, 1e24, 1e27. Milestones: x2 profit at 25, 50, 100, 200, 300, 400, then every 100; x2 speed at 25/50/100 (bumps, idle-design.md #7). First generator "Corner Store": base cost 4, r 1.07, 1.67/s (AdCap constants). Bulk buy x10/x100/max via the closed-form cost.

**Automation and upgrades:** Managers (one per generator; first free at 1:00, then 10x the generator's base cost). Sector Synergy: each 100 owned in a sector gives +0.5% to its two neighbours, keeping old districts relevant (Derivative-Clicker pattern, idle-design.md #6).

**Prestige 1 "Go Public":** `Shareholders = floor(150 * sqrt(lifetime_cash / 1e15)) - claimed`; +2% profit each (50 = x2); doubling needs 4x lifetime (idle-design.md #4 and skeptic). Shareholder shop: Night Desk (offline cap 8h -> 12 -> 18 -> 24h), Instant Managers, DRIP (+10% all), Synergy II.

**Prestige 2 "Index Inclusion"** at lifetime 1e60: `IndexWeight = floor(cbrt(lifetime / 1e60))`, each x1.5 all production (multiplicative), resets Shareholders. Cube root gives long runs (~8x per doubling) so the monthly Ledger cadence, not the sim, is the pacing partner.

**Offline:** 8h at 100% base, 24h max via Night Desk (a time cap bounds replay at ~30k actions/24h, idle-design.md #13). "Overnight Report" on return with one rewarded x2 (max 3/day).

**Session shape:** 3-5 min, four sessions/day: collect -> x2 ad -> spend all -> 1-2 milestone bumps -> check event -> set boost -> leave.

**First five minutes:** 0:00 tap Corner Store x5; 0:30 second store; 1:00 free Manager; 1:30 Bakery unlock; 2:30 first x2 milestone; 3:30 Mall teaser at $1,000; 4:00 pick a cosmetic 4-letter ticker; 4:30 first rewarded offer ("x2 Cash for 4h"); 5:00 Prospectus popup explains Go Public. No interstitial in session 1; first at session 2 after 90s; cap 2/session (idle-design.md #24).

**Day 1:** four sectors; first Go Public in the evening (~150-200 Shareholders, x4-5). The Ledger tab is revealed only after the first Go Public and only to self-declared 18+ (game first, on-ramp second, C6). **Day 7:** 2-3 Go Publics; Campus and Clinic; joined a Fund (guild, preset stickers, no free text — keeps us outside DSA "online platform" scope, legal-eu.md #20); first Earnings Season; first weekly "Earnings Call" accrual; Season Pass offer at the third sector unlock (conversion moment, idle-design.md #27). **Day 30:** first Dividend Day; base players mostly roll over (<$1), Pass holders ~$1.

**Walls:** 1e9 (Campus) around day 1; 1e15 first Go Public; 1e30 mid-game; 1e60 Index. "Pay or wait", never ad-gated essentials.

**LiveOps:** weekly IPO Friday (community goal -> cosmetic); monthly Earnings Season (7-day sector spotlight with event generators and event Shareholders; the sponsor-drop slot); monthly Dividend Day; 30-day Season Ladder (prestige sprint, cosmetic prizes, FLOAT ticket); quarterly Index Rebalance (content + balance). Backend LiveOps from day one (AdCap lesson, idle-design.md #20).

**Monetization:** Starter x3 permanent ($2.99), time skips, Gold Bars, ad-removal $3.99/30d, Season Pass $4.99/30d (event track + Ledger accrual boost). Rewarded 2-6/DAU opt-in; interstitials ≤2/session. No gacha, no loot boxes, no paid random items (keeps Belgium clean and PEGI below 16, legal-eu.md #25, S1-missed PEGI).

**KPI gates:** D1 ≥45%, D7 ≥18%, D30 ≥7% (mobile medians 22/4/0.75, idle-design.md #15; idle titles report 60%+ D1, #17). Net ARPDAU ≥$0.045 EU, ≥$0.08 US.

**Anti-cheat:** monotonic clock + server timestamps; server replays the input log from the last checkpoint (every 10 min and at every claim); affordability and rate checks; nothing of value is credited from client state (idle-design.md #31-32).

## 3. Token model (Q1)

**Legal shape.** US: FLOAT is a *digital tool* (membership, ticket, credential) under the SEC-CFTC release (F10, legal-us.md #19): it has no yield, no revenue share, no stock claim, no governance over economics, and consumptive utility from day one. Because it is paid *for gameplay*, the airdrop interpretation does not cover it (legal-us.md #20), so its non-security status rests on the absence of any investment contract: no sale, no marketing of appreciation, no buyback, no liquidity provision, no listing. The game issues but never redeems FLOAT for fiat or crypto, so it is not a FinCEN "administrator" (F11, legal-us.md #27). NY is permanently geofenced from on-chain claims (BitLicense outside-market test, legal-us.md #28). EU: MiCA utility token under Art 4(3) "existing utility" (F13, legal-eu.md #2): the game operates before any claim; we never draft a white paper (Art 4(8)), never seek admission to trading (Art 4(4)), and Art 4(5) lets us run claim/sink contracts without CASP status (legal-eu.md S1-missed). UK: pre-unlock FLOAT is non-transferable and redeem-only, outside "qualifying cryptoasset" (legal-eu.md #33); post-unlock UK persons are excluded from claims.

**Transferability decision (Q1): deployed at launch, transfer-locked until a published Unlock Gate, then a plain ERC-20 that third parties may pool on Robinhood Chain DEXs; the studio never lists, never seeds liquidity, never buys back, never sells.** Locked mode allows only player -> sink contracts and Ledger -> player claims. Unlock Gate (all required, measured publicly): ≥100,000 verified Seats; ≥6 months of live ops; ≥50% of trailing-90-day emission consumed by sinks; verified-Seat D30 ≥8%; counsel confirms the MiCA Art 4(3) and SEC-CFTC digital-tool postures unchanged; 30-day notice. If the gate is never met, FLOAT stays a soulbound on-chain ledger of standing — still real utility, still on Robinhood Chain.

**Supply and emission:** 1,000,000,000 FLOAT hard cap (OZ 5.7 ERC20Capped + Permit, immutable), MINTER_ROLE held by an Emission contract behind a 3-of-5 multisig with 7-day timelock. Allocation: 55% Play (10-year schedule, year-1 12% of Play, then −2 pp/yr to a 3% floor); 15% Team (Sablier, 4y linear, 1y cliff); 15% Treasury/LiveOps (timelocked, published budget); 10% Robinhood Chain ecosystem grants (integrations, Arbitrum Open House co-sponsorship, F3); 5% Founding Seats (retro to soft-launch players by a rule published before soft launch). No investor allocation; there is no sale ("sell the game first", F18). Year-1 daily ceiling ≈ 180,800 FLOAT, but tokens are **minted only as earned**: each verified Seat earns fixed amounts per discrete milestone (Closing Bell 10, first Go Public of the day 10, Ladder objective 10, Fund contribution 10; max 40/day), never scaled by score or purchases (Google fixed-ratio rule, legal-us.md #32). Unearned ceiling is never minted, so emission scales *down* with real activity (the Axie/SLP failure inverted, p2e-lessons.md #5). Per-action amounts are fixed per season and published 30 days ahead; if Seats exceed the ceiling the Treasury tops up within the season and the next season's amounts drop — never retroactive (Hamster lesson, precedents.md #31).

**Utility and sinks (all consumptive):** Fund charter (burn 500); Seat tier lock (lock 100/1,000/10,000 -> frame, banner, reserved ticker length); ticker names (burn 50-5,000 by length); Season Ladder ticket (burn 20); Time Capsule (burn 10 + mint an ERC-1155 record of a prestige run, a digital collectible); Earnings Season theme vote (lock 1, returned); web-client boosts (burn). On iOS none of this runs inside the app (Apple 3.1.1 bars crypto-unlocked functionality, precedents.md S1-missed); the iOS app shows balances and accrual only; claims and spends happen on the web companion or in Robinhood Wallet. Android declares Financial features and never advertises earnings (idle-design.md S1-missed).

**Where it lives, custody, UX.** Contracts on 4663: FLOAT; SeatRegistry (opaque seatId -> address, upgradeable, timelocked); Emission + Merkle distributor (per-season roots); Sinks; SponsorEscrow (USDG); PayoutLedger (Dividend Day roots). Gas sponsored via Alchemy Gas Manager or ZeroDev, both documented on Robinhood Chain (robinhood.md S1-missed AA; 8% overhead, tech.md #23), funded in ETH. Custody is the player's: Robinhood Wallet via WalletConnect (supported on Robinhood Chain, tech.md #21) as the default; an embedded 7702 wallet (Privy/Dynamic) with key export as fallback, provisioned only for claiming players to keep MAU fees small (Privy free tier is 0-499 MAU, tech.md S1-missed). Audit: boutique tier $15-25k, 10-14 weeks (tech.md #36). Chain risk: the sequencer can filter addresses (tech.md #5); every contract is chain-agnostic and state is server-held, so a filtered contract is redeployed on Arbitrum One with re-rooted Merkle claims; we monitor the 7-day Security Council timelock notices (robinhood.md S1-missed governance).

**What stops it becoming Hamster Kombat (F18):** (1) real value is paid in dollars from realized revenue, never in FLOAT; (2) no TGE, listing, liquidity, market maker or sale — there is no price for the team to promote; (3) per-human caps through Seats, not accounts; (4) emission tracks verified activity downward; (5) rules published a season ahead and never changed retroactively; (6) team/treasury streamed on-chain via Sablier; (7) unlock only after demonstrated sink demand; (8) FLOAT carries no rights to revenue, stock, yield or economic governance.

## 4. Stock reward path by jurisdiction (Q2)

Every player has a **Dividend Ledger**: a dollar-denominated loyalty balance accrued at fixed, published ratios (section 7). It is cents, not stock, until Dividend Day, when it is delivered at execution price. Between accrual and delivery there is no chance and no market exposure. The player chooses from "the Float 12": a broad-market ETF plus 11 sector ETFs (third-party securities bought on the open market, F8, F21). Delivery is monthly; minimum balance $1 (EU/ROW) or $5 (US, journal cost).

**US — fractional shares via broker.** SOLO: Alpaca Broker API. The player opens a brokerage account inside a partner flow (broker CIP: name, DOB, address, TIN, 18+; legal-us.md #33); the game funds a firm account that holds Float 12 inventory; on Dividend Day the broker journals shares to the player (JNLS journals are documented precisely for "reward your users", economics.md S1-missed Alpaca). Alpaca Securities LLC is also the custodian named in Robinhood's own Stock Token product spec (robinhood.md S1-missed product), a useful alignment for a later PARTNER handoff. The game never holds or transfers securities (C3). Funder: the Dividend Pool (section 7). Player sees: "Your $6.40 dividend was delivered as 0.0121 shares of [ETF]" with the broker's confirmation. PARTNER: Robinhood signs a flat marketing-fee agreement (no per-transaction compensation, FINRA 2040 caveat, legal-us.md #6) and delivers the sign-up stock itself under its own promotion terms; there is no Robinhood API to push our balances into Robinhood accounts (F4), so PARTNER means Robinhood credits, we pay a fee. Legal basis: third-party listed stock delivered by a registered broker-dealer into a KYC'd account (F8); deterministic ratios remove chance (F9); annual caps keep every player under the $2,000 1099-MISC threshold (F12).

**EU/EEA and other eligible countries (Stock Tokens).** SOLO (launch): on Dividend Day the game transfers the balance as USDG on Robinhood Chain to the player's self-custodied wallet (Robinhood Wallet recommended; USDG is a Robinhood Chain core token, tech.md S1 #1; MiCA status of the specific EMT to be confirmed before launch, EURC as alternate). Paying our own reward obligation in an EMT is not a crypto-asset service; the game never touches a security. The player then, self-directed, swaps USDG for any Stock Token on Uniswap/1inch through Robinhood Wallet's dapp browser (venues per robinhood.md #3; wallet features per #19). This copies the Cash App "stock gift" form — a restricted cash credit the recipient self-directs — which is the standard legal shape for "free stock" (precedents.md S2-missed Cash App). The Locker tab shows Stock Token holdings read from the RHJ registry and ERC-8056 `uiMultiplier` (robinhood.md S1-missed API; tech.md #9), with no prices, P&L or notifications. We deliberately do not prefill swap links or present named tokens: Robinhood's own materials do the offering (RHJ site terms restrict offers to Authorised Offerors, robinhood.md S1-missed). SOLO-CONDITIONAL (Phase 2b): a Voucher contract that routes the USDG into the player-chosen Stock Token in one call, only if Lithuanian counsel clears that a self-directed on-chain redemption via a decentralized venue is not MiFID execution/placing by the game (open question F14). PARTNER (preferred end state): Robinhood Europe UAB runs the promotion as licensed distributor — onboarded, appropriateness-tested clients, 180-day hold, its own terms (F5, legal-eu.md #10) — crediting Stock Tokens funded by the game's payment, plus a flat marketing fee per funded EU account. Classic Stock Tokens are never used: they are non-transferable MiFID derivatives exposed to the CFD inducement ban (F14, legal-eu.md #14-15, S1-missed Description of Services). Legal basis: no chance (deterministic), no stake (free to play), cash-value prize allowed under DE/NL/AT law when those two hold (F15); Robinhood's 18+ and EEA residency gates mirrored at Seat verification (legal-eu.md S1-missed Customer Agreement).

**Excluded countries (UK, CA, CH, UAE, sanctioned).** Stock Tokens are restricted there (F1, robinhood.md #7); UK bans "free crypto" incentives (F16). The Ledger accrues as Gold Bars at a published fixed ratio instead; no real-value reward, no token claim (CH: FLOAT claim allowed post-unlock). Sanctioned jurisdictions: no accounts.

## 5. Token-to-stock relationship (Q3)

**None, by design.** FLOAT is never redeemable for stock, never priced against stock, and holding or locking FLOAT never raises any Dividend Ledger ratio, cap, or sponsor-drop allocation. Any such link makes token purchases a claim on securities (custodial receipt or security-based swap, F10, legal-us.md #23), breaks C2, and violates Google's "not exponentiated by game performance" rule once value is attached. The only shared elements are the Seat (one identity gates both) and the on-chain PayoutLedger roots that let anyone audit both flows. FLOAT is the game's social economy; the Ledger is the game's loyalty program funded from revenue. Two separate systems is the boring, legal, fundable answer, and it is also what keeps FLOAT from becoming a pseudo-security the moment a price appears.

## 6. Identity, sybil and fraud (Q4)

**Primitive: the Seat** — one per verified human, 18+, jurisdiction-tagged, bound to exactly one wallet (SIWE signature via WalletConnect from Robinhood Wallet or the embedded wallet), with wallet changes requiring re-signature and a 7-day cooldown. Off-chain record (GDPR/AMLR, F17); on-chain only seatId -> address. There is no Robinhood OAuth (F4) and a WalletConnect signature proves wallet control, not KYC, so PARTNER = a Robinhood attestation ("verified, 18+, jurisdiction X"); SOLO = our KYC vendor (Sumsub Basic $1.35, Compliance $1.85 with AML, economics.md #22) in EU/ROW, and the broker's CIP in the US (broker returns an account id, never PII). Dedup: vendor face/document dedup across our applicant pool; in the US the broker's one-account-per-TIN. World ID was considered and rejected for now (Router not on 4663, Orb coverage limits; tech.md #34).

**When:** no Seat is required to play, accrue, or see the Ledger. Seat verification happens at the first real-value event (first Dividend Day delivery or first FLOAT claim), like Mistplay/ZBD (F19, precedents.md #26). Unverified accruals expire after 180 days of inactivity (published).

**Withdrawal gating:** verified Seat; 7-day pending window after each Dividend Day root (reversal on fraud); $1/$5 minimum; daily/season/year caps (section 7); geo checks (IP + KYC country) against the RHJ restricted list (robinhood.md S1-missed sanctions list) and the Robinhood Chain VPN prohibition.

**Device and behaviour:** Play Integrity (10k/day default quota, so only at claim/withdraw, tech.md #29) and App Attest; server replay of input logs; rewarded-ad SSV callbacks from AdMob/Unity/MAX/LevelPlay, idempotent on transaction id — an ad view that has no verified callback accrues nothing (tech.md #32); Fingerprint Smart Signals for emulator, device-farm and cloning (device farms are up 72% YoY, tech.md #31); behavioural clustering (synchronized logins, identical tap coordinates, many Seats paying to one exchange address — the Hamster playbook, precedents.md #31). Expect ~half of Android finance-style installs to be fake pre-KYC (economics.md #21); the Seat moves the fraud cost to the attacker: a real ID, liveness and a unique face for at most ~$15-30/year of extractable value.

**Cheaters:** published policy; pending balances forfeited, Seat suspended, future emission excluded (on-chain FLOAT already claimed cannot be clawed); human-reviewed appeal within 72h (the opaque-ban trust cost, p2e-lessons.md #33). We never reward referrals with value, removing the referral-farm vector entirely.

**KYC cost handling:** paid only for players reaching a first delivery (perhaps 5-10% of installs), budgeted at $2.50 per Seat in the Dividend Pool; US KYC is broker-borne.

## 7. Funding stack and payout rule (Q5)

**Revenue lines:** rewarded video (SSV-verified), interstitials, IAP, Season Pass and ad-removal subscriptions, sponsor drop fees, broker acquisition bounties.

**Published payout rule.** Each day, `Dividend Pool = 30% x net rewarded-video revenue + 15% x net IAP/subscription revenue + 100% x sponsor fees designated to drops + 50% x broker bounties actually received (paid only to the converting player as a Graduation Bonus)`. This is ~19% of net revenue in the examples below, inside ZBD's 10-20%-of-ads guidance once IAP is added and under Mode's audited ~25-33% bound (F19, economics.md #19 skeptic). Accrual ratios per geo tier are set each season so that expected accrual at 100% vesting ≤ pool; a coverage ratio (pool/accrual) is published monthly; if coverage <1.0 for 14 days, next-season ratios fall — accrued cents are always honoured (C1, C4). FLOAT never appears in the rule, so a zero token price changes nothing.

**Accrual ratios (EU tier / US tier):** verified rewarded view $0.0020 / $0.0035 (max 4/day); daily Closing Bell $0.003 / $0.006; weekly Earnings Call $0.02 / $0.04; Season Pass +$0.02 / +$0.04 per day (the loyalty benefit tied to a purchase, strengthening the Google posture, legal-us.md #32); Listing Bonus $2 one-time at Seat verification; Graduation Bonus (US only) = 50% of any bounty received, cap $10, disclosed as a material connection (legal-us.md #29). ROW tier-3 geos run at 0.5x EU (eCPMs $1-5, idle-design.md #23), like Bitcoin Miner's per-country caps (precedents.md S2 #16).

**Caps per Seat:** $0.03/day base, $0.06 with Pass (US $0.06/$0.10); $2 per 30-day season base, $4 with Pass (US $4/$6); $50/year plus one-time bonuses ≤$12 plus sponsor drops ≤$60 -> hard annual ceiling $150, far below the $2,000 1099-MISC line (F12), the NL EUR 449 gaming-tax line (moot: deterministic) and Ireland's EUR 3,000 gift exemption (legal-eu.md #41, #43).

**Sponsor drops (F21):** a sponsor escrows USDG (EU/ROW) or funds the broker firm account (US) in SponsorEscrow; the drop is a fixed amount per verified Seat completing a published objective during Earnings Season; if oversubscribed, allocation is by Seat seniority ("book-building"), deterministic and pre-published, never by chance. Default asset is the sponsor's *sector ETF*; a sponsor's own stock only under issuer counsel within Rule 10b-18 limits and outside Reg M restricted periods (Landa precedent, economics.md #30), and never a private company (F5). PARTNER option: Robinhood's Partner Stock Program, which already lets companies gift shares to customers' Robinhood accounts with issuer 1099 duties (precedents.md S2-missed), same Reg M caveat.

**Broker bounty use:** US SOLO = public affiliate programs ($20-50 Robinhood, $20-70 Webull-class, F6/F20, economics.md #13) structured as flat marketing fees; half to the player, half funds delivery and KYC. EU: none at launch (MiFID introducer questions, legal-eu.md #16). The model must close with bounty = 0, and it does.

## 9. Launch sequencing (Q7)

## 10. Why this beats the obvious alternatives, and what to cut first

*Pure points, no token* loses the ask's second leg and Robinhood Chain composability; Float keeps the token real (on 4663, verifiable emission, sinks) without the failure modes. *Tradeable token at launch with seeded liquidity* is the NOT/HMSTR/PIXEL path (F18): our unlock gate, zero treasury liquidity and revenue-denominated payouts are the difference. *Token redeemable for stock* is a custodial receipt or swap (C2, legal-us.md #23) — illegal for retail. *Classic Stock Tokens as rewards* are non-transferable derivatives under a likely inducement ban (F14). *US-first* has no on-chain rail (F2) and the richest fraud (economics.md #21); non-US-first on Robinhood Chain uses rails that exist today. *Robinhood-dependent* designs die if the partnership never lands; here Robinhood Wallet and USDG are public rails and Alpaca is public, so PARTNER only upgrades UX and economics. The exciting part survives: real Stock Tokens in the player's own Robinhood Wallet, a real on-chain token, sponsor drops with an on-chain audit trail, and an idle game tuned to genre-best retention.

**Cut order if forced:** (1) the Unlock Gate — FLOAT stays soulbound on-chain forever (still utility, zero market risk); (2) the US rail — EU/ROW only, no broker integration; (3) sponsor drops; (4) the web companion — Android-only token claims; (5) Season Ladder and Funds; (6) last, the Dividend Ledger itself, leaving a good idle game with a locked token — which is the honest floor of this product.

## Decisions
- **Q1. Is the token transferable/tradeable at launch, later, or never? How does it live on Robinhood Chain and what stops it becoming Hamster Kombat?**: Deployed on Robinhood Chain (4663) at launch as a capped, immutable ERC-20 in transfer-locked mode (only player->sink and Ledger->player claims), becoming a plain transferable ERC-20 only after a published, measurable Unlock Gate (>=100k verified Seats, >=6 months live, >=50% of trailing-90-day emission consumed by sinks, verified D30 >=8%, counsel re-confirmation, 30-day notice). The studio never lists, seeds liquidity, market-makes, buys back or sells FLOAT; US on-chain claims are off at launch (NY never); UK excluded post-unlock. _(Keeps the token genuinely on Robinhood Chain and composable (verifiable emission, Sablier vesting, sinks) while removing every Hamster Kombat ingredient (F18): real value is paid in dollars from revenue, emission is minted only as earned by verified humans and scales down with activity, rules are published a season ahead and never retroactive, and there is no price for the team to promote. Fits SEC-CFTC 'digital tool' (F10), MiCA Art 4(3)/(4)/(5) (F13), FinCEN non-administrator status (F11), Apple 3.1.1 (utility off-app on iOS) and Google fixed-ratio rules.)_
- **Q2. What is the path from gameplay to holding a Robinhood Stock Token (EU) and a fractional share (US)? Who funds, who delivers, what does the player see?**: A dollar-denominated Dividend Ledger accrues at fixed published ratios and is delivered monthly on Dividend Day. EU/EEA and eligible ROW, SOLO: the game pays the balance as USDG on Robinhood Chain to the player's self-custodied Robinhood Wallet, and the player self-directs the swap into any Stock Token via the wallet's dapp browser (Uniswap/1inch); SOLO-CONDITIONAL: a one-call Voucher contract if Lithuanian counsel clears it; PARTNER: Robinhood Europe UAB credits Stock Tokens under its own promotion terms for a fee. US, SOLO: Alpaca Broker API account opened by the player, game-funded firm inventory of 'Float 12' ETFs journaled (JNLS) to the player; PARTNER: Robinhood flat-fee promotion delivering its own sign-up stock. Excluded countries get Gold Bars at a fixed ratio. _(Only licensed parties ever hold or move securities (C3, F8); the EU path copies the Cash App 'restricted cash credit, self-directed purchase' template and avoids the open Prospectus/MiFID distribution question (F14) while Robinhood's own materials do the offering; Classic tokens are excluded as non-transferable derivatives under a likely inducement ban; the US has no on-chain Robinhood rail (F2) so a broker-dealer journal is the only lawful route; deterministic delivery removes chance (F9, F15) and monthly cadence with minimums keeps delivery cost and tax exposure low.)_
- **Q3. What is the relationship between the token and the stock rewards, given C2?**: None. FLOAT is never redeemable for stock, never priced against stock, and holding/locking FLOAT never raises any Ledger ratio, cap or sponsor-drop allocation. The only shared elements are the Seat identity and the public on-chain PayoutLedger roots used for auditing both flows. _(Any value link turns FLOAT purchases into a claim on securities (custodial receipt or security-based swap, F10/legal-us.md #23), breaks C2, and violates Google's 'not exponentiated by game performance' rule once value attaches; two separate systems is the boring, legal, fundable choice and the strongest anti-pseudo-security posture once a FLOAT price exists.)_
- **Q4. What is the identity/verification primitive without Robinhood OAuth, and how are duplicates prevented?**: The Seat: one per verified human, 18+, jurisdiction-tagged, bound to one wallet by SIWE signature (Robinhood Wallet via WalletConnect or embedded wallet), verified only at the first real-value event. SOLO: Sumsub KYC with face/document dedup in EU/ROW and the partner broker's CIP in the US (account id only, no PII); PARTNER: a Robinhood attestation API if offered. Withdrawals gated by Seat, 7-day pending window, minimums, daily/season/year caps, geo and sanctions screening; device (Play Integrity/App Attest/Fingerprint) and behavioural clustering are secondary signals; ad accrual only on verified SSV callbacks; no value for referrals; published cheater policy with 72h human appeal. _(C5 requires a verified-human binding, not device signals; there is no Robinhood OAuth or public attestation (F4); KYC-at-first-cashout is the proven reward-app pattern (F19) and moves fraud cost to the attacker (real ID + unique face for at most ~$15-30/year); brokerage KYC cannot be repurposed so an attestation/account-id is the GDPR/AMLR-safe primitive (F17); World ID rejected for now (no Router on 4663, coverage).)_

## Top risks
- Lithuanian counsel concludes that paying USDG to wallets with an in-game nudge toward Stock Tokens is a MiFID distribution/inducement or that the chosen EMT lacks MiCA status — mitigation: EU rewards fall back to EUR/gift-card payouts until the PARTNER path lands; no prefilled swap links at launch.
- Robinhood Chain sequencer filtering or ToS changes block or restrict the game's contracts (single sequencer, ArbOS 61 filtering) — mitigation: chain-agnostic contracts, server-held state, Arbitrum One redeploy with re-rooted claims.
- Google Play interprets real-value rewards for engagement as non-compliant loyalty (rumored July 2026 tightening) or Apple rejects the Ledger/Locker UI under 3.1.1/3.1.5 — mitigation: Ledger accrual tied to Season Pass purchase as the primary loyalty framing; all claims/spends on the web companion.
- Retention below Gate 1 (the idle game is not fun on its own) — mitigation: no real-value features ship until D1>=40%/D7>=15%; the reward layer is additive, never the hook.
- Sybil/device-farm fraud exceeds the model (half of Android finance installs fake; KYC document fraud) — mitigation: Seat-bound payouts, 7-day pending windows, reversal, per-Seat caps that make attack ROI negative.
- After the Unlock Gate, a third-party DEX price emerges and press/players frame FLOAT as an investment, re-triggering SEC investment-contract or MiCA marketing exposure — mitigation: unlock only after sink-demand evidence, strict comms policy, no liquidity or MM ever, option to keep FLOAT soulbound indefinitely.
- US broker rail economics unknown (Alpaca journal/account pricing unpublished) — mitigation: $5 US minimum, monthly batching, raise minimum or switch to cash-to-broker deposit if per-journal cost exceeds $1.50.
- FINRA 2040/finder risk on affiliate bounties or MiFID introducer status in the EU — mitigation: flat marketing fees only, no per-transaction compensation, EU bounty off at launch, drop the Graduation Bonus if counsel objects.
- Tax characterisation surprises (1099-NEC vs MISC; FR 60% gift rate; LT prize frequency rule) — mitigation: annual ceiling $150, monthly cadence, per-country accrual toggles, recipient-responsibility disclosures mirroring Robinhood's own terms.
- Sponsor drops become the dominant payout and a sponsor's own-stock request creates Reg M/10b-18/Section 5 exposure — mitigation: sector ETFs by default, issuer counsel and blackout calendars for own-stock, never private companies.
