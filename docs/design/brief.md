# Design brief: idle clicker game with a utility token on Robinhood and tokenized-stock rewards

Date of record: 2026-09-01. All facts below come from a verified research package in `research/` (read `research/_digest.md` first; per-topic files `research/robinhood.md`, `research/legal-us.md`, `research/legal-eu.md`, `research/precedents.md`, `research/idle-design.md`, `research/p2e-lessons.md`, `research/tech.md`, `research/economics.md` hold ~35 sourced findings each with skeptic verdicts).

## The ask (verbatim from the user)
"lets spec an idle clicker game with a token utility on robinhood where you can earn tokenized stocks. make no mistake"

Interpretation: a full product spec for (1) an idle/clicker game that is genuinely fun and retentive on its own; (2) a utility token whose utility and life is anchored in Robinhood's ecosystem (Robinhood Chain, Robinhood Wallet, and potentially Robinhood listing); (3) a mechanism by which players end up holding Robinhood tokenized stocks (on-chain "Stock Tokens" where they exist; fractional shares in a Robinhood brokerage account where they do not, i.e. the US) as a consequence of playing. "Make no mistake" means: no hand-waving on legality, economics, bots, or on what Robinhood actually offers today. Be explicit about assumptions and about what requires a Robinhood partnership versus what can be built unilaterally.

## Load-bearing facts (verified unless marked)
Robinhood products
1. Two distinct Robinhood tokenized-stock products exist. "Classic Stock Tokens" (since 2025-06-30): OTC derivative contracts issued by Robinhood Europe UAB (Bank of Lithuania), in-app only for 30 EU/EEA countries, settled on Arbitrum One, 24/5, 2,000+ tokens, dividends paid in cash in-app, KID risk class 7/7, no self-custody. On-chain "Stock Tokens" (since 2026-07-01 with Robinhood Chain mainnet): tokenized DEBT securities issued by Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Ltd under a Jersey base prospectus, plain 18-decimal ERC-20s with ERC-8056 uiMultiplier absorbing dividends/splits (no cash dividends), freely transferable to any EVM wallet, 24/7 on DEXs (Uniswap, Rialto, Lighter, Arcus, 1inch), Chainlink feed per token, mint/burn only by KYB'd Authorised Participants, offered in 120+ countries, explicitly NOT to US, Canada, UK, Switzerland, UAE persons. 190+ tokens by Aug 2026.
2. No Robinhood tokenized stock is available to US customers. The SEC "innovation exemption" for tokenized equities was delayed (May 26, 2026); the Aug 2026 Regulation Crypto Assets proposal does not cover tokenized equities. US players can only receive fractional shares into a brokerage account at an SEC-registered broker-dealer.
3. Robinhood Chain: Arbitrum Orbit L2, chain ID 4663, ETH gas, ~250 ms blocks, ~$0.001 fees, permissionless contract deployment, ERC-4337 and EIP-7702 live, Blockscout explorer, single Robinhood-operated sequencer with address hash-list transaction filtering (a contract address could in principle be filtered), canonical bridge to Ethereum (7-day exit), third-party bridges from Arbitrum One. Sablier already deployed. Developer docs at docs.robinhood.com/chain; $1M buildathon funding via Arbitrum Open House 2026.
4. Robinhood Wallet (self-custody, Robinhood Non-Custodial Ltd, Cayman) supports Robinhood Chain and Arbitrum and WalletConnect; funding via Robinhood Connect (no public developer docs) or Sardine. There is NO public equities API, NO third-party OAuth/"Sign in with Robinhood", and no documented partner path for third parties to deliver Stock Tokens to Robinhood-KYC'd users. Any feature needing Robinhood account attestation or in-account delivery requires a partnership.
5. Robinhood's own token giveaway terms (2026-06-24) hand tokens only to onboarded, appropriateness-tested clients with a 180-day hold, "not available to the general public". The 2025 OpenAI/SpaceX token giveaway (~150k EU users, ~$1M) drew OpenAI's disavowal and a Bank of Lithuania information request.
6. Robinhood's referral program changed 2026-09-01 to fixed tiered bonuses paid in stock from 26 large caps ($50 for 1 referral up to $10,000 for 200, $15k/yr cap), replacing the lottery-style $5-$200 reward (~99% paid $5). Referees "may receive a separate sign up stock reward". Public affiliate CPAs for brokers run $20-$70 per funded account; Robinhood's 2025 marketing spend implies up to ~$222 per net funded add (upper bound). Robinhood has 28.4M funded customers, $1.31B quarterly revenue.
7. Massachusetts fined Robinhood $7.5M (settled Jan 2024) over gamification: confetti, scratch-off free-stock lotteries, tap-to-climb games, referral stock rewards; Robinhood undertook to drop games-of-chance features. SEC's 2021 "digital engagement practices" concern named "games, streaks, prizes, free stock". Any design must survive that lens.

Legal
8. US: "free stock" given for registering, supplying data, or referring is a "sale for value" (Securities Act 2(a)(3), 1999 SEC no-action letters): an issuer cannot give away its OWN securities for gameplay; giving third-party listed stock bought on the open market via a broker-dealer avoids Section 5. Every live stock-reward program (Stash Stock-Back, Acorns Earn, Bits of Stock, Grifin, broker referral promos) delivers via a registered broker-dealer (often Apex) into a KYC'd brokerage account (effectively 18+), with the sponsor funding the shares.
9. US gambling: prize + chance + consideration; several states count "considerable time or effort" as consideration, so RANDOMIZED rewards for gameplay are the highest-risk design. Deterministic, fixed-ratio rewards remove chance. Google Play requires in-game loyalty rewards to be fixed-ratio and non-chance and never multiplied by game performance; Apple 3.1.5(v) bars crypto apps from paying currency for downloading apps/social posting, requires loot-box odds disclosure, and permits incentives for in-app actions. NY/FL sweepstakes registration/bonding above $5,000. Washington "thing of value" cases (Big Fish $155M, DoubleDown $415M) mean even non-cashable chips can be gambling.
10. US token law: SEC-CFTC joint interpretive release (effective 2026-03-23) classifies digital commodities, digital collectibles (expressly including in-game items), and digital tools (memberships, tickets, credentials) as non-securities unless sold under an investment contract; its airdrop interpretation covers ONLY distributions with no consideration, so tokens paid "in exchange for" gameplay are outside it. A token redeemable for stock at a fixed rate is a security (custodial receipt) or a security-based swap that cannot be sold to retail. Proposed Regulation Crypto Assets (comments due 2026-10-20) adds a $5M/4-yr startup exemption and $75M/12-mo exemption. CLARITY Act: House passed 2025-07-17, Senate cloture vote reported for 2026-09-15 (needs 60 votes). GENIUS Act is law (no yield to stablecoin holders).
11. US money transmission: an administrator that issues AND redeems convertible value is a money transmitter; closed-loop, non-convertible in-game currency is not; an on-chain transferable token convertible to stock or cash is. NY BitLicense excludes units usable only inside a game with no outside market.
12. US tax: prizes reportable on 1099-MISC at FMV at >= $2,000/yr (from 2026); rewards for services on 1099-NEC. COPPA compliance date 2026-04-22; brokerage KYC is 18+.
13. EU: the game token is most naturally a MiCA "utility token"; Art 4(3) exempts an offer of a utility token for a good/service that already exists or operates from Title II (white paper), but "offered for free" does NOT apply if the offeror gets personal data or any benefit. Anyone providing exchange/custody/transfer of the token as a service needs CASP authorisation (grandfathering ended 2026-07-01). Financial instruments (the stock tokens) sit under MiFID II, not MiCA.
14. EU: ESMA (2026-02-24) says cash-settled derivatives giving exposure to an underlying "would likely fall in scope" of national CFD product-intervention measures, which PROHIBIT monetary and non-monetary benefits that incentivise retail trading. Inference: a third party handing out Classic Stock Tokens (derivatives) as rewards is exposed to that ban; Robinhood, not the game, must be the regulated distributor. Whether a third party may deliver the Jersey on-chain Stock Tokens (transferable debt securities) to EU retail wallets without triggering Prospectus Regulation/MiFID distribution rules is an OPEN question needing Lithuanian counsel.
15. EU gambling: Belgium treats paid random rewards with monetary-value prizes as illegal gambling; Austria/Netherlands say loot boxes with no cash-out in skill games are not gambling; Germany requires a stake and a monetary prize. A reward with real cash value satisfies "prize" everywhere, so the levers are: no stake and/or deterministic allocation. Free-entry promotional draws are lawful but regulated (NL, IT).
16. UK: crypto promotions regime bans "free crypto" incentives; Robinhood Stock Tokens are not offered to UK persons; UK is out of scope for real-asset rewards at launch.
17. GDPR/AML: keep personal data off-chain (EDPB v2.0, 2026-07-07); brokerage KYC data cannot be repurposed for the game; an attestation ("this user is verified, 18+, jurisdiction X") without data sharing is the workable primitive. Tax: giver may owe (Germany 30% flat option, NL 37.8% gaming tax on chance prizes > EUR 449, France 60% on third-party gifts); Robinhood's terms push tax to recipients.

Precedents and economics
18. Every engagement-minted token with no external funding source collapsed 95-99.9% (NOT, HMSTR, CATI, DOGS, SLP, GST, PIXEL, BIGTIME, PRIME, ILV, GUN, SONIC). Bot share was 40-70% of "users"; Hamster banned 2.3M pre-airdrop and lost ~1M users/day post-TGE; retroactive changes to allocation rules triggered boycotts. Survivors: separate soft currency from tradeable token, gate withdrawals, tie payouts to revenue (Off The Grid 30% of gross revenue buyback; Parallel prize pools from royalties), hard per-player daily caps, sell the game first (Big Time $100M revenue, no token sales).
19. Reward-app payout benchmarks: ZBD guidance 10-20% of ad revenue for the reward budget; Mode Mobile at most ~25% of net revenue; Mistplay $0.50-$3/hr, $550/yr cap, 2h/day/game, KYC selfie at first redemption; Bitcoin Miner dynamic cap ~330 sats/day (~$0.25). Coinbase ended Learning Rewards after $150M. Sweatcoin free users earn under $1/yr.
20. Ad economics: US rewarded-video eCPM ~$15-20, UK ~$10.65, continental EU ~$5-9 blended, Lithuania tier-2/3. Idle games run ~8 rewarded ads/DAU/day (Idle Miner Tycoon; 60% of revenue from ads), practitioner guidance 2-6/DAU. A US-heavy idle title at 3 rewarded/DAU and $12 eCPM makes ~$0.036 ad ARPDAU. Payer conversion ~2-5%. Ad-side money is cents per player-day; the broker acquisition bounty ($20-$70 CPA, more via partnership) is the dominant funding source per converted player.
21. Stock-reward programs: Stash Stock-Back 1% (subscription/interchange funded); Acorns Earn and Bits of Stock are brand-funded (~0.5% back), invested 60-120 days later to absorb returns; Bumped (brand stock loyalty) died when brands stopped funding; Grifin survives because user-funded. Bumped's study: +40% spend at rewarded brands, +100% after $5-$10 stock grants. Issuers sponsoring giveaways of their OWN stock hit Reg M/Rule 10b-18/Section 5 problems (Landa 2023); sponsors should fund third-party stock or ETFs.
22. Fraud: affiliate/incentivized traffic shows 36x the fraud rate of self-reporting networks; ~22% of non-organic installs flagged; ~half of Android finance-app installs fake. KYC costs $1.35-$4 all-in per check.
23. Idle design canon: cost = base * r^owned (r 1.07-1.15), milestone x2 at 25/50/100 owned, prestige currency via sqrt (AdCap angels) or cbrt (Cookie Clicker) of lifetime earnings, offline caps 2-24h, 1e308 layer walls, break_infinity.js. Benchmarks: mobile median D1 ~22-26%, D7 ~4-10%, D30 ~1-4%; idle titles report D1 60%+; ~3.9 sessions/day of ~3-10 min. Ad frequency: first interstitial after 60-90s, 1-2 per session, 2-6 rewarded views per DAU with 40-70% opt-in. Monetization that holds: permanent early x3 multiplier, rewarded 2x/time-skip boosts, ad-removal subscription, event battle pass. Anti-cheat: monotonic clock + server-side plausibility checks; deterministic server replay is cheap for idle games.
24. Tech: wallet onboarding is commoditized (Privy free to 50k MAU, EIP-7702 sponsorship; Dynamic; Turnkey); paymaster support for chain 4663 unconfirmed; Chainlink push feeds exist per Stock Token; Play Integrity free (10k/day default quota), App Attest; rewarded-ad server-side verification callbacks exist for AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin MAX, LevelPlay; ERC-20 audit $5k-$25k, 8-14 weeks. US-eligible on-chain stock delivery exists only via permissioned issuers (Dinari, Superstate, Securitize), not Robinhood.

## Hard constraints every design must satisfy
- C1. No chance in anything with cash value. Stock rewards and token rewards are deterministic, fixed-ratio, pre-published, and never retroactively changed.
- C2. The token is never redeemable for stock at a fixed rate and is never marketed as an investment. It must fit the SEC-CFTC "digital tool/collectible" categories and the MiCA "utility token" category.
- C3. Securities reach players only through a licensed party (Robinhood, or a fallback broker-dealer) into a KYC'd, 18+ account/wallet in an eligible jurisdiction; the game itself never holds or transfers securities to players unless counsel clears it for a specific jurisdiction.
- C4. Real-value payouts are budgeted from realized revenue (ads, IAP, sponsor fees, broker acquisition bounties), capped per account per day/season/year, and gated behind verification at first withdrawal. Publish the budget rule.
- C5. Sybil resistance must not rely on device signals alone; the binding primitive is a verified human/brokerage identity attestation, one game account per verified identity.
- C6. The game must be a good idle game with no real-value hook: D1/D7 targets, prestige loop, LiveOps, and an honest "rewards are cents to a few dollars, the on-ramp is the point" positioning.
- C7. App-store compliant on both stores (fixed-ratio loyalty rewards, no crypto-for-tasks outside the app, odds disclosure not needed because no chance).
- C8. Jurisdiction-aware from day one: US (fractional shares only, via partner broker), EU/EEA (on-chain Stock Tokens via Robinhood or DEX swap; Classic tokens excluded as derivative inducements), UK/CA/CH/UAE (no real-asset rewards), everywhere else (on-chain Stock Tokens where Robinhood offers them).
- C9. Everything requiring Robinhood cooperation is labelled "PARTNER" with a unilateral fallback labelled "SOLO".

## Open design questions the architecture must decide
- Q1. Is the token transferable/tradeable at launch, later, or never? If tradeable, how does it live on Robinhood Chain and what stops it from becoming a Hamster Kombat?
- Q2. What exactly is the path from gameplay to holding a Robinhood Stock Token (EU) and to holding a fractional share (US)? Who funds it, who delivers it, what does the player see?
- Q3. What is the relationship between the token and the stock rewards, given C2? (e.g. staking/burning the token raises a deterministic reward multiplier; token is spent on boosts instead of ads; token gates sponsored drops; or no relationship at all)
- Q4. What is the identity/verification primitive without a Robinhood OAuth (WalletConnect signature from Robinhood Wallet? own KYC at first withdrawal? partner attestation?) and how are duplicates prevented?
- Q5. What is the funding stack and the payout rule (percent of which revenue lines; per-account caps; how sponsor drops work; how the Robinhood acquisition bounty is used)?
- Q6. What is the game's fantasy/theme and how does it tie thematically to compounding, sectors, IPOs and real stocks without encouraging trading (ESMA/SEC gamification lens)?
- Q7. Launch sequencing: which jurisdiction first, points-before-token, when TGE, what needs counsel sign-off.

## Evaluation criteria for judging designs (score each 1-10)
- Legal viability in the US and in the EU as of Sept 2026 (given facts 8-17)
- Economic sustainability (payouts funded from real margin, survives zero token price, survives loss of Robinhood partnership)
- Sybil and fraud resistance
- Player value and fun as a standalone idle game (retention mechanics, no dead time, respect for the player)
- Fidelity to the ask (token utility genuinely on Robinhood; players genuinely end up with tokenized stocks)
- Feasibility with a small team in 2026 (what is buildable SOLO vs needs PARTNER)
- Clarity and completeness (decisions made, not deferred)
