### Recommended hybrid

Build on Draft 5 (Compounders). It has the most honest player contract (the mandatory positioning line), the cleanest loop (Reinvest is the only verb, the daily Cycle is no-streak and nothing is lost by skipping), the most buildable sequencing (game-only soft launch, then US on settled broker rails per F8, then the token as a feature update, then Ireland-first EU), and the only token that genuinely sits in Robinhood Wallet without being tradeable (bound transfer). Fix four defects before grafting anything: (1) delete Float packs; (2) take DRIP off the value path entirely: no Tier I sponsor gating, no Tier II pending shortening, no settlement burn, no DRIP boosts in the Season Pass (adopt Draft 2's zero-linkage rule; the Q3 answer becomes 'none', which also removes the C2/F10 exposure Drafts 3 and 5 share); (3) keep only an opaque seatId-to-address map on-chain and all identity linkage off-chain (Draft 4's SeatRegistry, F17); (4) stop funding the Welcome Grant from an incentivized Robinhood affiliate funnel into a second brokerage account: in SOLO pay a fixed $3 grant from IAP margin (Draft 1's fallback) at the delivering broker, and raise it to $5-10 only under a PARTNER flat fee where the bounty-paying broker is the delivering broker. Bounties become upside, never a player-visible promise.

Graft from Draft 3 the payout engine: Pool(S+1) funded from the prior season's banked revenue, rate clamped 0.5x-1.25x and published seven days ahead, per-account caps that follow the Season Pass entitlement (which is also the strongest 'subordinate to a qualifying monetary transaction' framing for Google, F9), and the monthly Earnings Call publishing pool, points, rate, mint/burn and enforcement counts. Graft from Draft 2 the game feel: Reinvest auto-buy as the automation tier, a spendable Float shop, the Prestige Exponent upgrade as the long-tail goal, walls placed at tiers 4/7/10, first IPO inside about three elapsed hours (not Draft 5's 8-12h or Draft 3's day-2 +10%), the starter pack at the third sector unlock, max one interstitial per session, the $5 unverified cap, 14-active-day tenure, 35-day vest on IAP-derived credit, and the economy-simulation gate at three geo mixes and 2x the verified rate. Broad ETF default, no single names in copy.
