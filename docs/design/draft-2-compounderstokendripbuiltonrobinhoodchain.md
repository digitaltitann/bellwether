# DRAFT 2: Compounders (token: DRIP) — "Built on Robinhood Chain"

A cozy idle game about patience and compounding, built on Robinhood Chain, where verified adult players earn a non-tradeable membership token (DRIP) held in Robinhood Wallet and small, deterministic, revenue-funded fractional-share (US) or Stock Token (EU/EEA) rewards delivered only by a licensed party.

# Compounders — architecture proposal (2026-09-01)

Framing: written as Robinhood's growth PM would build it, then split into **PARTNER** (needs Robinhood to act or build) and **SOLO** (a third-party studio alone). Every Robinhood capability cited is one the research confirms; where none exists (no OAuth, no equities API, no third-party Stock Token delivery path: F4; robinhood #21), the PARTNER item says "Robinhood would have to build this". Copy rule from day one: Robinhood's on-chain product is called "Stock Tokens", the network "Robinhood Chain", the mark usage "Built on Robinhood Chain", and the studio's brand is primary (Robinhood Chain ToS, robinhood missed-facts).

Mandatory positioning line (store listing, first run, and FTC earnings-claim substantiation): "Compounders is a game about patience. Member rewards are cents to a few dollars a year, paid in real fractional shares or Stock Tokens by a licensed partner. The point is a first, free, boring, diversified holding — not trading."

## 1. Concept and theme

**Name**: Compounders. **Token**: DRIP (Dividend Re-Investment Points) — the name mirrors what Robinhood's Stock Tokens actually do: dividends auto-reinvest via the ERC-8056 multiplier (F1; robinhood #5).

**Player fantasy**: you run a tiny founder's company in a hand-drawn city split into 12 sector districts (Staples, Discretionary, Tech, Health, Financials, Industrials, Energy, Materials, Utilities, Real Estate, Comms, plus an "Index" meta-district). Businesses throw off Cashflow; the only verb is **Reinvest**. Upgrades are **Moats**, automation is **Board Members**, prestige is an **IPO** (you take the company public, reset, keep permanent **Float**), and the second layer is **Index Inclusion**. The lesson the loop teaches, mechanically, is time × rate: the longer you leave things alone, the more they compound. That is deliberately the opposite of what the SEC's DEP concern (F7; legal-us #9) and ESMA's gamification statements (legal-eu #18) worry about: trading frequency.

**How real stocks appear without encouraging trading**:
- No real tickers, prices, P&L, charts, buy/sell verbs, market simulation, streaks, confetti, or money leaderboards anywhere in the loop (all named in the Massachusetts settlement, robinhood #28; SEC 2021 DEP list, legal-us #9). Leagues rank Float (a prestige number), prizes are cosmetic.
- Real securities exist in exactly one place: the **Vault**, a separate screen outside the loop, showing holdings delivered by the licensed partner. The reward menu is pre-published and diversified: default is an S&P 500 ETF (VOO/SPY), with an optional pick from the same 26 large caps ("2 largest S&P 500 companies in the top 13 sectors") Robinhood uses for its own Tiered Referrals (F6; robinhood #23). Robinhood's own EU giveaway used NVDA or VOO (legal-eu #10). Vault copy carries "no security is recommended; this is a promotional loyalty reward" and never links to a trade ticket.
- Sector districts are flavor ("this district makes the things you use every day"), not stock picks. IPO is a story beat about patience ("your company is worth more because you waited"), not an event to trade.
- Rewards are auto-held (US: partner broker's 3-trading-day sell lock and 30-day withdrawal lock, mirroring Robinhood's promo terms, robinhood #23; EU PARTNER: Robinhood's 180-day hold, F5).

## 2. Core loop and progression

**Currencies**: Cashflow (soft, per run, resets at IPO); Float (prestige 1, permanent); Index Points (prestige 2, permanent); DRIP (on-chain membership token, no cash value, never affects the economy of the loop); Stock Credit (USD/EUR cents in a separate off-chain ledger, only from money events, section 7). Rule: **time and performance earn DRIP; money events earn Stock Credit; nothing random touches either** (C1).

**Generators** (12 sector businesses per world): cost_k(n) = 4 × 11^(k−1) × r_k^n, output_k = 6^(k−1)/s per unit, r_k = 1.07 + 0.005(k−1) (1.07 → 1.125; canonical band, idle #1, #2). First-unit payback of tier k is 4 × (11/6)^(k−1) s (tier 6 ≈ 83 s, tier 9 ≈ 8.5 min, tier 12 ≈ 52 min), so walls arrive naturally ("a natural energy system", idle #7). Milestones: ×2 output at 25/50/100/200/300/400 owned, ×2 speed at 25/50/100 (AdCap, idle #1). Bulk-buy ×1/×10/×max with the closed-form max-affordable formula (idle #1).

**Automation**: Board Member per generator (auto-collect, buyable at 10× the generator's base cost); a CFO (auto-buys cheapest Moat) unlocks after the first IPO; a "Reinvest All" button from minute one. Tapping matters only in the first five minutes.

**Prestige**: IPO grants Float = floor(150 × sqrt(L/1e15)) − Float already claimed, L = lifetime Cashflow this world; +2% production per Float (square root, ~4× earnings to double, fast early resets: idle #4). First IPO is recommended at L ≈ 1e13 → 15 Float (+30%), reachable in 8–12 elapsed hours. Worlds: Main Street; **Wall Street** unlocks at L = 1e14 (AdCap's Moon pacing, idle #20; day 5–7); **Global** at 1e22, each with its own Float constant (per-world tuning, idle skeptic #4). Layer 2, **Index Inclusion**: IP = floor(cbrt(total Float earned across worlds / 1,000)), +1% all-worlds output and +1 h offline cap per IP (cube root, slow, idle #3). Numbers use break_infinity.js / BreakInfinity.cs (idle #29).

**Offline**: 8 h cap at full rate (inside the 2–24 h envelope, idle #13), +1 h per Index Point up to 12 h, 24 h with Compounders Plus. "Double offline earnings" rewarded ad on return is the top placement (idle #14, #28). Server-authoritative: monotonic elapsedRealtime on device, server checkpoint (state + server timestamp), deterministic replay of the input log before any DRIP or Stock Credit is written (idle #31, #32; tech #28).

**Session shape**: 3–4 sessions/day × 5–8 min ("log in, spend, log out": idle #7). Daily **Compounding Cycle** (collect offline → reinvest all → one Moat) is the DRIP-bearing ritual; there is no streak and nothing is lost by skipping a day.

**Ads** (all opt-in except interstitials): Bull Run ×2 for 4 h; Double Offline; Fast Forward 4 h; Bulk Discount −10% on next 10 buys. 8 rewarded/day hard cap, first 6 count for Stock Credit, 2-minute cooldown, S2S verified (tech #32). No interstitials in session one; then first after 90 s, ≤2 per session, ≥3 min apart (idle #24). Targets: opt-in ≥ 50%, 3 rewarded/DAU.

**IAP (no loot boxes anywhere, so no odds disclosure and PEGI stays 12: legal-eu missed-facts)**: Founder's Pack $4.99 permanent ×3 offered at the Wall Street unlock (third-content-unlock conversion moment, idle #27; "extremely compelling", idle #7); Compounders Plus $3.99/30 days (ad-free, 24 h offline, second CFO; Cats & Soup pricing, idle #19); Season Pass $9.99/90 days (cosmetics, event generators, Float boosts, DRIP boosts — never Stock Credit); Float packs (instant prestige). Purchased currency never expires (Apple 3.1.1).

**First five minutes**: 0:00 tap Reinvest at the Corner Bakery; 0:45 second business; 2:00 tutorial gifts a Board Member; 3:30 third district unlocks; 4:30 first Moat; 5:00 the IPO button appears greyed with its target, and a quiet ticker shows "+10 DRIP: Day-1 Cycle complete". No wallet, no KYC, no mention of money in the first session.

**Day 1**: three sessions, first IPO at the end of the day (+30%), Wall Street teased. **Day 7**: Wall Street unlocked, Founder's Pack shown, first Sector Week event, Vault revealed with the honest positioning line and the geo-specific reward status; players in reward geos see Stock Credit accrue in "pending" from their first verified ad.

**LiveOps**: 90-day seasons (frozen DRIP rules published 30 days before); weekly Sector Week (one district ×3 plus event-only generators — Idle Miner Tycoon's event mines doubled weekend revenue, idle #17); monthly Earnings Season (7-day side economy, cosmetic prizes); seasonal sponsor drops (section 7). Weekly content sprints with 1/5/20/100% staged rollout (idle #17).

**Targets and kill criteria**: D1 ≥ 40%, D7 ≥ 18%, D30 ≥ 7% (above the 22/4/0.8 mobile medians, idle #15; idle titles report 60%+ D1, idle #17). Gate 0 (section 9) requires D1 ≥ 35% with no rewards and no token; if the idle game is not fun on its own, nothing else ships (C6).

## 3. Token model (DRIP)

**What it legally is**. US: a **digital tool** (membership/credential) under the SEC-CFTC interpretive release, with its cosmetic skins as **digital collectibles** (F10; legal-us #19). It is never sold, carries no yield, no buyback, no price, no profit marketing, and the studio makes no "essential managerial efforts" promises, so no investment contract attaches; we do not rely on the airdrop interpretation, which excludes tokens paid for gameplay (legal-us #20). Because it is non-convertible and has no outside market, it stays outside NY's virtual-currency definition (legal-us #28) and the operator is not a FinCEN administrator/exchanger (legal-us #27). EU: a MiCA **utility token** exempt under Art 4(3) on the "existing utility" limb (the game is live ≥ 4 months before the token exists; we do not claim "free", since sign-up data defeats that: F13; legal-eu #2), never admitted to any trading platform (Art 4(4)), no voluntary white paper (Art 4(8)); Art 4(5) then means holding and moving it needs no CASP licence (legal-eu missed-facts). UK: non-transferable except with the issuer/limited network, so outside the "qualifying cryptoasset" promotions regime (legal-eu #33); never marketed as crypto in the UK.

**Q1 decision — "points, then bound token, open transfer only conditionally and maybe never"**:
- Phase A (launch): Drip Points, off-chain ledger, no token.
- Phase B (TGE, no hype event, shipped as a feature update): DRIP on Robinhood Chain (chain 4663, F3; tech #2). Immutable OpenZeppelin 5.7 ERC20Capped (cap 10B) + ERC20Permit; `_update` consults a **BoundRegistry**: transfers allowed only between game contracts and wallets bound to the same verified identity hash. So a player can move DRIP from the embedded wallet into **Robinhood Wallet** (WalletConnect on Robinhood Chain is confirmed, F4; tech #21), but not to anyone else.
- Phase C (open P2P transfer): only if all published conditions hold — US and EU counsel opinions that transferability does not change the classification, 12 consecutive months of realized net revenue, ≥100k verified humans, and a sybil audit — and even then the studio never creates a pool, pays a market maker, lists, quotes a price, or buys back. This may never happen and the token policy says so.

**Supply and emission** (published, frozen per season, never retroactive: C1; Hamster's rule change triggered the boycott, precedents #31): per-account fixed grants — 10 DRIP per Compounding Cycle, 25 per IPO (max 4/day), 100 per Sector Week, season-pass milestones; caps 200/day and 12,000/season per identity. Season ceilings 200M × 0.95^n (max 4B, 40% of cap); if a ceiling is reached, emission halts for the season. 20% LiveOps/sponsor drops, 10% treasury locked 36 months in Sablier (deployed on 4663, tech #35), 30% never minted without a published policy change. Team liquid allocation: zero. No emission for referrals, installs, or social posts (Apple 3.1.5(v), legal-us #31).

**Utility list and sinks** (DRIP gates, never prices):
1. **Compounder Tiers** (lock 1k/5k/20k DRIP, no yield, unlock any time): Tier I unlocks sponsor-drop eligibility; Tier II shortens the Stock Credit pending window from 14 to 7 days; Tier III adds cosmetic slots and a Vault theme. Tiers never change accrual rates.
2. **Collectibles** (burn): district skins, mascots, ticker plates as ERC-1155 on Robinhood Chain, used on the web dapp ("Exchange Floor") and reflected cosmetically in-app.
3. **League entry** (burn 200): seasonal leagues ranked by Float; cosmetic prizes only.
4. **Settlement fee** (burn 50 per Stock Credit withdrawal).
5. **Votes** (snapshot-weighted): next Sector Week theme.
6. PARTNER: DRIP tiers surface as Robinhood Wallet quests/badges (Lighter's 2× points inside Robinhood Wallet is the precedent, robinhood #15). SOLO: none.
On iOS all DRIP utility is exercised on the web dapp or in Robinhood Wallet, not in-app, because Apple 3.1.1 bars crypto from unlocking in-app content (precedents missed-facts); Google Play gets a Financial Features declaration and no "earn" marketing (legal-eu missed-facts).

**Where it lives / custody UX**: an embedded EIP-7702 wallet (Privy/Dynamic, both listed for the chain, tech missed-facts) is created silently at account creation; gas sponsored via Alchemy Gas Manager/ZeroDev on 4663, funded in ETH at ~$0.001/tx (F3). "Link Robinhood Wallet" = WalletConnect + SIWE, both addresses bound to one identity. Claims are seasonal Merkle drops (own distributor; Sablier's $1.99 per claim is too high for micro-claims, tech #35). Periphery (distributor, registry, tier lock, collectibles) sits behind a 3-of-5 multisig with a 7-day timelock; boutique audit $8–25k, 8–14 weeks (tech #36). Fallback chain if the sequencer's ArbOS 61 filtering ever rejects our contracts: Arbitrum One (tech #5, #8).

**What stops it becoming Hamster Kombat** (F18): nothing to dump (no listing, no price, no buyback); emission fixed per action and per identity, declining per season, published before play; on-chain claims require a verified human (section 6); no referral emission; real value flows through a separate revenue-funded ledger that is indifferent to DRIP price (which does not exist); and the game must pass a fun gate with no rewards at all.

## 4. Stock reward path by jurisdiction (Q2)

Common core: **Stock Credit** is a USD/EUR-cent ledger entry, not a security. It only becomes an asset when a licensed party converts it (C3). The game never holds, buys, or transfers securities.

**United States — fractional shares only** (F2; no Robinhood Stock Tokens for US persons, robinhood #7).
- PARTNER (native version): Robinhood extends its existing promotional stock-reward mechanics (reward stock bought in an omnibus account and allocated, as Robinhood's referral program and Webull's F-1 describe: F6; economics #12) into the player's Robinhood brokerage account, on receipt of a signed Compounders attestation ("verified member, earned $X"). Robinhood would have to build the attestation endpoint; the Partner Stock Program covers only a company's own equity (precedents skeptic-2 missed-facts) and is not usable. Player sees the grant in Robinhood with the standard 3-day sell / 30-day withdrawal locks.
- SOLO: a white-label brokerage account ("Compounders Vault, brokerage services by Alpaca Securities LLC" or Apex via Bits of Stock, F8; legal-us #2, #4) opened with the broker's CIP at first withdrawal (18+, SSN). Delivery via Alpaca's documented firm-to-user JNLS share journals or JNLC cash journals with self-directed purchase (economics skeptic #24; the Cash App "fiat used solely for a self-directed purchase" form, precedents skeptic-2 missed-facts). Default investment VOO after 30 days if the player does not pick from the 26-name menu. Funded by the studio's published budget (section 7).
- Why legal: third-party listed stock bought on the open market through a broker-dealer avoids Section 5 (F8; legal-us #1); deterministic fixed-ratio value removes chance (F9); the broker's retail communications go through FINRA 2210 principal approval (legal-us #8).

**EU/EEA — on-chain Stock Tokens** (Jersey tokenised debt securities, passported prospectus; F1; robinhood #3, legal-eu missed-facts). Classic Stock Tokens are excluded: they are MiFID derivatives, non-transferable, and giving them away risks the CFD inducement ban (F14; legal-eu #14, #15, missed-facts).
- PARTNER: Robinhood Europe UAB (MiFID + CASP licences, Bank of Lithuania, legal-eu #8) runs a "Compounders Stock Token Grant" under its own June-2026 giveaway framework (F5; legal-eu #10): onboarded, appropriateness-tested, 18+ clients (legal-eu skeptic #37); tokens (default SPY/VOO Stock Token) delivered into the client's Robinhood Wallet or account with the 180-day hold; the studio pays Robinhood Europe a flat marketing fee, not per token. Robinhood is the regulated distributor; the game is a marketing partner whose copy Robinhood approves (MiFID Art 24; legal-eu #16).
- SOLO: Stock Credit is paid as EURC (Robinhood's own dividend match paid in EURC, robinhood missed-facts) by a MiCA-licensed EMI/CASP payout partner (ZBD holds NL EMI + MiCAR licences and does light KYC at first cash-out, precedents #18; p2e #31) into the player's Robinhood Wallet on Robinhood Chain. The player, if they choose, swaps EURC for a Stock Token on Uniswap inside Robinhood Wallet (Stock Tokens are plain ERC-20s tradable there, robinhood #4). The game never touches Stock Tokens and its copy never presents the securities' terms, because the RHJ prospectus consents only to offers by Authorised Offerors and an "offer to the public" is any communication with sufficient information to decide (robinhood missed-facts; legal-eu missed-facts). Whether even this steering is a MiFID/Prospectus act is the open question F14 flags; it is a Phase 3 counsel gate.

**Excluded countries** (UK, Canada, Switzerland, UAE, RHJ prohibited list; F1, F16; robinhood #7, #32): game and DRIP only; Stock Credit never accrues, and the Vault says so at install. Rest of world where Robinhood offers Stock Tokens: no accrual until a licensed rail is live per country (Phase 4, PARTNER via Robinhood's distributing entity).

## 5. Token-to-stock relationship (Q3)

DRIP is never redeemable for stock or cash, at any rate, fixed or floating (C2; a fixed-rate redeemable token is a custodial receipt or security-based swap, legal-us #23). DRIP does not scale accrual: Stock Credit rates are fixed ratios to money events regardless of Float, DRIP balance, or game performance (Google Play's fixed-ratio, not-exponentiated-by-performance rule, legal-us #32). The only relationships are eligibility and friction: Tier I gates sponsor drops (each drop grants the same fixed amount to every eligible completer), Tier II shortens pending, and each withdrawal burns 50 DRIP. Membership credential in, no price out.

## 6. Identity, sybil and fraud (Q4)

**Primitive**: a verified-human attestation from the licensed counterparty that will hold the asset — one identity hash per human, no PII to the game (F17; brokerage KYC cannot be repurposed, legal-eu #39). The attestation is `{verified, age18, jurisdiction, identity_hash = H(partner_customer_id ‖ salt)}`. PARTNER: Robinhood issues it (there is no OAuth today, F4; Robinhood would have to build it). SOLO: the white-label broker (US) or EMI/CASP (EU) issues it from its own KYC ($1.35–1.89 list, ~$4 all-in, F22; economics #22). World ID (cloud verify, nullifier per action, ~18M Orb-verified, tech #34) is an optional accelerator that raises DRIP caps, never a substitute for the asset-side attestation. GDPR: linkage record off-chain, DPIA before deploy (legal-eu #38).

**Tiers**: T0 play (email/Apple/Google sign-in). T1 on-chain DRIP claim: Play Integrity MEETS_DEVICE_INTEGRITY / App Attest at claim time only (10k/day default quota, tech #29, #30), device intelligence (Fingerprint/Sardine: emulator, VM, fleet patterns, tech #31), WalletConnect signature for Robinhood Wallet linking. T2 Stock Credit withdrawal (min $5/€5, 14-day pending): the partner attestation above. One game account per identity_hash; a second account's pending credit is forfeited and its DRIP frozen. Stock Credit accrues in "pending" before T2 but is capped at $5 pending so unverified accounts create no liability.

**Ad SSV**: credit only on the network's server-to-server callback (AdMob ECDSA, Unity HMAC, MAX, LevelPlay; tech #32), idempotent on transaction ID, 6/day, 2-min cooldown, plus a progression-sequence check (Playrix cut fraudulent rewarded credits 12.5% that way, p2e #36).

**Behavioural signals**: synchronized logins, same-pixel taps, identical action timing across accounts, VPN masking (also banned by Robinhood Chain ToS), many accounts to one bound wallet — the Hamster/Pixels playbook (precedents #31; p2e #18). Expect ~22% of non-organic installs and ~40% of affiliate traffic to be fraudulent and half of Android finance installs to be fake pre-KYC (F22; p2e #35; economics #21); the KYC gate moves the cost to the attacker.

**Cheaters**: published policy, forfeiture of pending credit and DRIP, permanent loss of the real-value rail, a human-reviewed appeal within 14 days (opaque bans are the top trust complaint, p2e #33). Delivered assets are not clawed back except for fraud proven inside the broker's 30-day hold (Stash reserves the same right, legal-us #2). Cost handling: KYC is paid once, only for players who reach the $5 threshold; in the broker-funnel path the broker already KYCs at its own cost.

## 7. Funding stack and payout rule (Q5)

**Revenue lines**: rewarded ads (~60% of idle revenue, idle #17), interstitials, IAP and subscriptions, sponsor platform fees (20% of drop escrow), and broker acquisition bounties (public affiliate CPAs $20–70 per funded account; Robinhood $20–50 per qualifying action; F20; economics #13).

**Published payout rule (the "Compounders Reserve Rule")**: the reward budget for a period is 20% of net rewarded-ad revenue (ZBD guidance 10–20%, F19; p2e #31), 8% of gross IAP/subscription revenue, a fixed $5 Welcome Grant per funded broker account (≈14% of a $35 CPA), and 100% pass-through of sponsor escrow. Per-event rates are fixed and published per season and country: $0.003 per verified rewarded ad (US; €0.0015 EU), $0.40 per Plus month, 8% of each IAP, $5 per funded broker account. If trailing-30-day accruals exceed 80% of trailing-30-day budget, next season's rates are lowered with 30 days' notice; accrued credit is always honored (never retroactive, C1). Caps per identity: $0.50/day, $40/season, $150/year US; €0.30/day, €25/season, €100/year EU (under the US $2,000 1099-MISC threshold, F12; under Ireland's €3,000 gift exemption, legal-eu #43; deterministic, so outside NL gaming tax, legal-eu #41).

**Broker bounty use**: SOLO uses the public affiliate programs (Robinhood via its affiliate network, Webull as fallback), which pay per qualifying account opening, not per trade or asset — the structure counsel must bless under FINRA 2040 (legal-us #6). The game is barred from Robinhood's retail referral program as a "Robinhood-paid creator" (robinhood #23). PARTNER: a flat monthly marketing fee (cleanest under 2040) plus Robinhood co-funding the Welcome Grant as its own "separate sign-up stock reward" (F6). The bounty funds the Welcome Grant, the broker onboarding, and margin.

**Sponsor drops** (F21): a sponsor escrows cash with the broker partner; every Tier-I verified player who completes the sponsored Sector Week during the window receives the same fixed amount (e.g., $1 of VOO) until escrow is exhausted, with the quota published up front (no chance, no race-based variation in amount). Sponsors see aggregates only. Reg M caveat: sponsors may fund third-party stock or ETFs only; a public company wanting its own shares granted is refused by default and, if ever allowed, requires broker-executed 10b-18 open-market purchases paused during restricted periods and counsel sign-off (Landa was terminated, economics #30).

**Worked example — 1,000 DAU, US, per day.** Assumptions: 3 verified rewarded views/DAU at $15 eCPM (F20; economics #1, #2); interstitial/banner $0.010 ARPDAU; IAP + subs $0.025 ARPDAU (2–5% payers, hybrid split, idle #26); 1,000 DAU ≈ 80 installs/day (DAU/installs ≈ 12 at D1 40/D7 18/D30 7); 1% of installs → funded broker account at $35 CPA.

## 9. Launch sequencing (Q7)

- **Phase 0 (Sep–Dec 2026)**: engage US securities/gaming/FinCEN/tax counsel and Lithuanian MiCA/MiFID counsel; build the game and server-authoritative backend; soft launch game-only (Drip Points, no rewards, no token) in Canada and the Philippines. **Gate 0**: D1 ≥ 35%, D7 ≥ 12%, crash-free ≥ 99.5%, ad ARPDAU ≥ $0.03 in a tier-1 cohort, opt-in ≥ 45%. Fail → no token, no rewards, fix the game.
- **Phase 1 (Jan 2027, US)**: Stock Credit live on the SOLO rail, affiliate broker funnel, Welcome Grant. Counsel sign-offs: third-party-stock promotional structure (Section 5, F8), no-chance state gaming memo (time-as-consideration states, F9), Rule 2040 affiliate structure and FINRA 2210 co-branded copy, closed-loop points (FinCEN/NY), tax characterisation (1099-MISC vs NEC), COPPA/age gates; Apple review and Google Financial Features declaration. **Gate 1** (60 days): withdrawal fraud < 5%, duplicate-identity rate < 2%, payouts within ±20% of the Reserve Rule, KYC pass ≥ 85%, zero store strikes, funded-account conversion ≥ 0.7% of installs.
- **Phase 2 (Q2 2027)**: DRIP on Robinhood Chain in bound-transfer mode; Robinhood Wallet linking; Exchange Floor dapp; Arbitrum Open House buildathon entry for grants (F3; robinhood #10). Sign-offs: US digital-tool opinion, EU Art 4(3)/4(5) opinion, contract audit, DPIA. **Gate 2**: ≥ 30% of verified players link a wallet, claim gas < $0.01/claim, no sequencer rejections ("Transaction rejected by chain policy", tech missed-facts).
- **Phase 3 (Q3 2027, EU/EEA)**: Ireland first (English, $12 eCPM, €3,000 gift exemption), then NL, DE, LT, FR; Belgium excluded. Sign-offs: Lithuanian opinion on whether steering to Stock Tokens is an offer/RTO/placing (F14), EMI partner contract, UCPD copy review ("free" wording, legal-eu #21), NL/IT promotional-game non-applicability memos, per-country tax notes. PARTNER talks with Robinhood Europe run in parallel; if signed, the EURC SOLO rail becomes the fallback. **Gate 3**: same fraud/payout metrics, plus complaint rate < 0.5% of withdrawals.
- **Phase 4 (2028)**: rest-of-world Stock Token countries (PARTNER); open-transfer review against the published conditions; US on-chain delivery only if an SEC exemption arrives or a FINRA-registered on-chain rail (Dinari, whose US terms conflict internally; Superstate, small-caps only) clears counsel (tech #15, #16, missed-facts).
- Watch list that can change the analysis: CLARITY cloture 2026-09-15, Regulation Crypto Assets comments 2026-10-20 (F10), RIS application ~2029, Digital Fairness Act proposal (legal-eu missed-facts).

## 10. Why this beats the obvious alternatives, and what gets cut first

- Token redeemable for stock at a fixed rate: a custodial receipt or security-based swap, unsellable to retail (legal-us #23). Rejected outright.
- Pay stock directly for play time or performance: violates Google's fixed-ratio rule and, with any chance leak, becomes prize + chance + "considerable time" consideration in strict states (F9); also unfunded by revenue, the SLP/HMSTR failure signature (p2e #1, #12).
- Tradeable token at launch with revenue buybacks: every comparable fell 95–99% (F18); it makes the studio an administrator/exchanger and CASP, drags in UK promotions rules, and buybacks are "closer to a symbolic gesture" (p2e #29). Rejected; revenue goes to real stock instead.
- Game vault hands out Stock Tokens in the EU: unlicensed distribution against a prospectus that consents only to Authorised Offerors (robinhood missed-facts; F14). Rejected in favor of Robinhood-as-distributor or player self-swap.
- Robinhood-only, no SOLO: no partner API exists (F4), so the product would be hostage to a deal. Every PARTNER item here has a SOLO twin.
- No token at all: cleaner, but loses the ask's Robinhood Wallet/Chain anchor; it is the first thing to cut, and the design is built so cutting it costs nothing.

**Cut order if forced**: (1) open-transfer Phase C (already conditional); (2) sponsor drops; (3) the EU SOLO EURC rail (EU becomes PARTNER-only or game-only); (4) on-chain DRIP (keep Drip Points); (5) ad-funded Stock Credit (keep only the broker-funded Welcome Grant). What remains at the bottom is a good idle game with a broker affiliate funnel that still pays real fractional shares through a licensed party — which is the only version of this product that has ever survived (precedents #1–#8).

## Decisions
- **Q1. Is the token transferable/tradeable at launch, later, or never? If tradeable, how does it live on Robinhood Chain and what stops it becoming a Hamster Kombat?**: Points at launch (off-chain Drip Points); at TGE (Phase 2) DRIP is an immutable ERC20Capped on Robinhood Chain (4663) in bound-transfer mode: transfers only between game contracts and wallets bound to one verified identity, which includes the player's Robinhood Wallet via WalletConnect. Open P2P transfer is conditional on published triggers (US+EU counsel opinions, 12 months of realized net revenue, >=100k verified humans, sybil audit) and may never happen; the studio never lists, seeds a pool, pays a market maker, quotes a price, or buys back. Supply cap 10B; per-action fixed emission (10/Cycle, 25/IPO, 100/Sector Week), caps 200/day and 12,000/season per identity, season ceilings 200M x 0.95^n, team liquid allocation zero, no referral emission. _(Bound transfer keeps DRIP a SEC-CFTC digital tool (F10; legal-us #19) outside NY BitLicense and FinCEN administrator status (legal-us #27, #28), a MiCA Art 4(3) utility token custodiable without a CASP under Art 4(5) (legal-eu #2, missed-facts), and outside the UK qualifying-cryptoasset regime (legal-eu #33), while still living in Robinhood Wallet on Robinhood Chain (F3, F4). Every engagement-minted tradeable token fell 95-99% (F18; p2e summary); with no price, no listing and no buyback there is nothing to dump, and pre-published frozen rules avoid the Hamster allocation-change boycott (precedents #31).)_
- **Q2. What exactly is the path from gameplay to holding a Robinhood Stock Token (EU) and a fractional share (US)? Who funds, who delivers, what does the player see?**: Gameplay never earns securities; money events (verified rewarded ads, IAP/subscriptions, funded broker account, sponsor drops) accrue Stock Credit in cents on an off-chain ledger funded by the studio's published Reserve Rule. US: at first withdrawal (>= $5, 18+, broker CIP) the credit is delivered as fractional shares of VOO (default) or one of Robinhood's 26 large caps; PARTNER = Robinhood allocates reward stock into the player's Robinhood brokerage account on a signed attestation (Robinhood must build the endpoint); SOLO = Alpaca (JNLS/JNLC journals) or Apex white-label brokerage account. EU/EEA: PARTNER = Robinhood Europe UAB runs a Stock Token grant under its June-2026 giveaway framework into onboarded, appropriateness-tested clients' Robinhood Wallets with a 180-day hold, studio pays a flat marketing fee; SOLO = EURC paid by a MiCA-licensed EMI/CASP into the player's Robinhood Wallet, player self-swaps to a Stock Token on Uniswap on Robinhood Chain; the game never holds Stock Tokens and never presents their terms. Classic Stock Tokens excluded. Excluded countries (UK/CA/CH/UAE/sanctions list) get no accrual. _(No Robinhood tokenized stock exists for US persons (F2; robinhood #7), so US must be broker-delivered open-market third-party stock, the only model in use and the one that avoids Section 5 (F8; legal-us #1, #2). Robinhood's own June-2026 giveaway terms are the template (F5; legal-eu #10). Alpaca's Journals are documented for share rewards (economics skeptic #24).)_
- **Q3. What is the relationship between the token and the stock rewards, given C2?**: DRIP gates, never prices. It is never redeemable for stock or cash at any rate and never changes Stock Credit accrual rates (which are fixed ratios to money events). Its only links to the stock rail are eligibility and friction: Tier I lock (1k DRIP) gates sponsor-drop eligibility (every eligible completer gets the same fixed amount), Tier II (5k) shortens the pending window from 14 to 7 days, and each withdrawal burns 50 DRIP. _(A token redeemable for stock is a custodial receipt or security-based swap unsellable to retail (legal-us #23), and a token that scales stock rewards would make the reward 'exponentiated by game performance', breaking Google Play's fixed-ratio rule (legal-us #32) and inviting chance/consideration analysis (F9). A membership credential that only gates eligibility fits the SEC-CFTC 'digital tool' category (F10) and MiCA utility token (F13) while still giving the token real use in the Robinhood ecosystem.)_
- **Q4. What is the identity/verification primitive without a Robinhood OAuth and how are duplicates prevented?**: A verified-human attestation from the licensed counterparty that will hold the asset: {verified, age18, jurisdiction, identity_hash = H(partner_customer_id || salt)}, no PII to the game; one game account per identity_hash, duplicates forfeit pending credit and freeze DRIP. PARTNER = Robinhood issues it (must be built). SOLO = the white-label broker's CIP (US) or the EMI/CASP's KYC (EU) at first withdrawal (>= $5/EUR 5, 14-day pending, $5 pending cap pre-verification). Ad credit only on S2S callbacks with idempotent transaction IDs, 6/day, 2-min cooldown, progression-sequence checks. Published cheat policy with 14-day human appeal. _(No Robinhood OAuth or partner attestation exists (F4; robinhood #21); brokerage KYC data cannot be repurposed but an attestation can (F17; legal-eu #39). KYC at first withdrawal is the Mistplay/ZBD pattern (F19; precedents #26; p2e #31) and confines the $1.35-4 cost (F22) to players who actually earned $5. ## Top risks
- EU counsel concludes that even paying EURC and letting players self-swap into Stock Tokens is an 'offer to the public', reception/transmission or placing under the Prospectus Regulation/MiFID II (F14; robinhood missed-facts on Authorised Offerors) — EU collapses to PARTNER-only (Robinhood Europe as distributor) or game-only.
- Google Play treats Stock Credit for ad views as a loyalty reward not 'subordinate to a qualifying monetary transaction' or as real-money gaming, or Apple objects to fractional-share rewards under 3.1.5 — Android/iOS rewards shrink to IAP-ratio and broker Welcome Grant only (legal-us #31, #32; precedents open question on Apple).
- Rule 2040 / Exchange Act 15(a): per-funded-account affiliate CPA is found to be transaction-based compensation making the studio an unregistered finder — restructure to flat fees, cutting the dominant funding line (legal-us #6).
- Robinhood never partners: no attestation, no in-account delivery, no EU grant — the product runs entirely on SOLO rails (Alpaca/Apex, EMI/CASP), which works economically (stress case 23% rail cost) but weakens 'genuinely on Robinhood' fidelity to Wallet/Chain only (F4).
- The idle game is not fun enough (D1 < 35% at Gate 0); rewards cannot rescue retention and would only attract extractive farmers (p2e #2, #15) — the whole stack is moot without the game.
- Fraud: physical device farms and half-fake Android finance installs (economics #21; tech #31) pollute UA and ad metrics before KYC; a KYC-dedup failure at the partner would allow multi-accounting on the real-value rail (C5).
- Any chance element leaking into anything with cash value (a random event touching Stock Credit or sponsor drops) triggers prize + chance + 'considerable time' consideration in strict states and Belgium/NL/IT promo regimes (F9, F15) — requires an engineering-level invariant, not a policy.
- Robinhood Chain sequencer filtering or ToS enforcement (terminology, VPN, commercial-use consent) against the game's contracts or copy — mitigated by Arbitrum One fallback and factual-mark-only copy (tech #5; robinhood missed-facts).
- Regulatory shift changes the token analysis: CLARITY cloture 2026-09-15, Regulation Crypto Assets (comments 2026-10-20), MiCA/ESMA views on DEX 'admission to trading', RIS inducement rules (~2029), Digital Fairness Act addictive-design rules (F10; legal-eu missed-facts).
- Gamification enforcement against a broker-co-branded game under the Massachusetts fiduciary standard or SEC DEP scrutiny if any celebration, streak, or nudge toward specific securities slips into the product (F7; precedents skeptic-2 missed-facts on Robinhood v. Galvin).
