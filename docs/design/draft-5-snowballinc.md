# DRAFT 5: Snowball Inc.

A server-authoritative idle tycoon about compounding in which verified adult players convert capped, revenue-funded loyalty credit into fractional index-fund holdings via a licensed broker (US) or a self-directed EURC credit on Robinhood Chain (EU/EEA), with a non-transferable membership token on Robinhood Chain that never touches the stock.

# Snowball Inc. — architecture proposal (CFO / tokenomics lens)

Design principle: every real-value payout is funded from realized, banked revenue of the prior season; nothing is minted against engagement; the token is never in the payout formula; the game is fun with the reward tab hidden. Anything requiring Robinhood cooperation is tagged PARTNER with a SOLO fallback (C9).

## 1. Concept and theme

**Name:** Snowball Inc. **Token:** DRIFT (a snowball is made of drift). **Player fantasy:** you inherit a lemonade stand and grow it into a compounding conglomerate across the real economy's sectors. Every generator is a business in one of 12 sectors (Consumer Staples lemonade stand, Utilities water tower, Materials quarry, Industrials rail yard, Energy wind farm, Real Estate towers, Health Care clinic, Financials credit union, Consumer Discretionary theme park, Communications satellite, Technology chip fab, Space logistics). The theme teaches compounding by feel, not by charts: reinvesting output into more capacity is the only lever, milestone doublers are literally "economies of scale," and the prestige layer is an **IPO**: you take the company public, cash out to "Founder Shares," and start the next venture with a permanent edge. The second prestige layer is a **Holding Company** that owns your IPO'd firms.

How it ties to real stocks without encouraging trading (F7, ESMA gamification lens, legal-eu #18-19): the only real asset the player ever receives is a broad index fund (VOO in the US; the SPY or VOO Stock Token on Robinhood Chain in the EU). No ticker selection, no price charts, no buy/sell buttons in the app, no celebrations tied to any trade, no streaks with cash prizes, no leaderboards for rewards (in-game cash leaderboards only). The message is "you now own a slice of everything, hold it," which is the opposite of the Massachusetts complaint pattern (F7) and of the SEC 2021 DEP list (legal-us #9). Sponsors appear as generator skins ("Sector Week presented by X") and fund index-fund grants, never their own stock (F21). Copy rules from Robinhood Chain ToS (robinhood.md missed facts): say "Stock Tokens" and "Built on Robinhood Chain," never "tokenized stocks," and keep the Snowball brand primary.

## 2. Core loop and progression

**Currencies (four, deliberately separated; p2e-lessons #10, #18):**
- *Cash* (soft, per run, resets at IPO).
- *Founder Shares* (prestige 1, permanent, off-chain).
- *Blueprints* (hard currency, IAP only, never expire per Apple 3.1.1; also earned slowly from milestones).
- *Ledger Points* (LP): the reward-accrual points. Off-chain ledger entry, "promotional, no cash value until redeemed," fixed-ratio accrual (C1, C7), 18+ tab. Not tradeable, not a token.
- *DRIFT* is not a game currency; it is a membership credential (section 3).

**Generators and costs (F23, idle-design #1-2):** cost_next = base × r^owned with r = 1.07 for sectors 1-4, 1.10 for 5-8, 1.12 for 9-12; base cost per tier ≈ 11× the previous, base output ≈ 6.5× (Cookie Clicker ratio). Production = base × owned × multipliers. Milestone "scale" doublers ×2 at 25/50/100/200/300/400/500 owned (AdCap), plus a ×7.77 "golden ticket" per sector from a Season Pass track (cosmetic-free progression reward). Bulk-buy ×1/×10/×100/max. Numbers on break_infinity.js (idle-design #29); the server uses the same deterministic library.

**Automation:** managers (buy once per sector) collect automatically; "Analysts" (prestige shop) auto-buy the cheapest generator; "CFO" (Holding Company layer) auto-prestiges.

**Prestige 1 (IPO):** Founder Shares gained = 150 × sqrt(lifetime_run_earnings / 1e15) − already_claimed (AdCap square-root, needs ~4× earnings to double, idle-design #4-5). Each share +2% profit. First IPO reachable at ~$1e12 lifetime (≈5 shares) around hour 5-6 of play (end of day 2 for a typical player). Runs shorten as shares stack; expect 8-12 IPOs in week one for engaged players.

**Prestige 2 (Holding Company):** unlocks at 1e60 cumulative earnings across IPOs; grants Compound Points, multiplicative ×1.05 each (Egg Inc prophecy-egg style, idle-design #12), and resets Founder Shares. Layer 3 (Index) reserved for the 1e308 wall (idle-design #10), month 6+ content.

**Offline:** server computes offline yield from its own timestamp (never device wall clock, idle-design #31): 8h cap at full rate, +2h per Silo research level to 12h, 24h with the Season Pass. "Double offline earnings" rewarded ad at return.

**Session shape:** 3-5 sessions/day of 3-8 minutes: collect, spend all cash, hit a milestone bump, optionally watch one 2× boost (10 min), leave. First interstitial after 90 s, max 2 per session, 3-min spacing (idle-design #24). Rewarded placements: 2× income (10 min), 4h time-skip, double offline, "rush IPO" (skip the last 10% of a run). Target 3-4 rewarded views per DAU at 55% opt-in (F20, F23).

**First 5 minutes:** tap the lemonade stand (manual clicks), buy stands to 10 by 0:45, first manager at 1:00 (automation moment), first ×2 at 25 owned by 3:00, unlock Utilities at 4:00, first "scale" popup explains compounding in one sentence. No ad before 90 s, no rewards tab visible.

**Day-1 beats:** 4 sectors, 2 managers, first 2× boost, first Blueprint from a milestone, starter pack offer (permanent ×3, $2.99) at the third sector unlock (idle-design #27). Rewards tab appears at the end of session 2 for players who self-declare 18+, with the honest line: "Most players earn under $1 a month; the point is owning a slice of the market."

**Day-7 beats:** 3+ IPOs, Analysts, first Sector Week event (weekend, 2× event revenue like Kolibri's event mines, idle-design #17), first Holding Company tease, and ~15% of D7 survivors crossing the $5 LP threshold that triggers the brokerage/KYC prompt.

**Targets (C6):** D1 45-55%, D7 20-25%, D30 8-10% (idle titles beat the 22/4/0.75 mobile medians, F23; we set targets below Kolibri's 63-81% D1). Kill criterion: D1 < 35% in soft launch means the core is not fun, and no reward layer ships until it is.

**LiveOps:** 30-day Seasons with a $4.99 Season Pass (event track, 24h offline, cosmetics, no reward multiplier), weekend Sector Weeks, a monthly public "Earnings Call" that publishes the payout pool, points issued, rate, mint/burn ratio and fraud actions. Monetization that holds (F23): permanent multipliers, ad-removal $3.99/30d, Blueprint packs $1.99-$19.99, Season Pass. No loot boxes, no gacha, no chance anywhere, even cosmetic (keeps Belgium, PEGI 16 default and Play odds rules out of scope, legal-eu #25, missed facts).

## 3. Token model (Q1)

**What DRIFT legally is.** US: a "digital tool" under the SEC-CFTC interpretive release (F10; legal-us #19): a membership/credential that performs a practical function, carries no yield, no revenue rights, no redemption right, and is never marketed as an investment (C2). The airdrop safe harbor does not apply because DRIFT is earned (legal-us #20), so its non-security status rests on the digital-tool category plus the absence of any investment-contract promise. EU: a MiCA utility token (F13) giving access to a service that already operates (the Clubhouse and Season Pass), relying on the Art 4(3) "existing utility" exemption from Title II; because it is non-transferable and never admitted to trading, Art 4(5) means no CASP licence is needed to hold or move it for players (legal-eu missed facts). UK: a redeem-only, non-transferable token is outside "qualifying cryptoasset" (F16), but we still do not promote it there.

**Transferability decision (Q1): non-transferable at launch and for at least 18 months; transferability is an optional Phase 4 exercised only if (a) CLARITY is law or Regulation Crypto Assets is final, (b) US and Lithuanian counsel sign off, (c) a MiCA Title II white paper is filed, and (d) a Robinhood PARTNER listing path exists (F4, robinhood.md #31). Default outcome: it stays a soulbound pass forever, and the product loses nothing.** A tradeable engagement token is the single common factor in every collapse in F18; we refuse to be number 15.

**Contract and chain.** Immutable ERC-20 (OpenZeppelin 5.7 ERC20Capped + AccessControl MINTER_ROLE), cap 1,000,000,000, 18 decimals, `_update` override allowing only mint, burn, and transfers to or from the Clubhouse and Redeemer contracts (soulbound). Deployed on Robinhood Chain mainnet (chain 4663, ETH gas, ~$0.001 fees, permissionless deployment, F3), verified on Blockscout. Periphery (Clubhouse, Redeemer, Sablier-based sponsor-drop vesting; Sablier is already on 4663, F3) is upgradeable behind a 3-of-5 multisig with a 7-day timelock. Audit budget $10-20k, 8-14 weeks (F24). Sequencer risk: Robinhood can hash-list-filter a contract address (F3, tech #5); mitigation is a pre-audited mirror deployment on Arbitrum One and an off-chain ledger of record so a filtered chain never blocks gameplay or payouts.

**Supply and emission ("points first, token later" at both product and player level).** No sale, no team/investor allocation (Big Time precedent, p2e-lessons #21), no TGE event. Emission is a function of revenue, not headcount (p2e-lessons #1, #5, #25): each season the minter is authorised to mint 1 DRIFT per $0.01 of the previous season's net revenue, distributed pro rata to verified players' fixed-ratio Ledger Points, capped at 5,000 DRIFT per account per season, plus 100 DRIFT per Season Pass purchase (IAP-receipt verified). DRIFT accrues as a pending off-chain balance and is minted on-chain only after the player's identity attestation (section 6), so bots never touch the chain. Published target: burns ≥ 50% of the season's mint, reported in the Earnings Call; if the mint/burn ratio exceeds 2:1 for two seasons, next season's mint halves (Axie's 4-6× ratio was the failure signal, p2e-lessons #3).

**Utility and sinks (all deterministic):**
1. Clubhouse membership credential (web dapp on Robinhood Chain): sponsor-drop enrolment requires holding ≥ 500 DRIFT (a gate, not a burn, so no "stake").
2. Burn 200 DRIFT for a 4h time-skip or 2× boost instead of watching an ad, on Android and web only.
3. Burn for cosmetic generator skins and IPO badges (digital collectibles) in the Clubhouse; on iOS, cosmetics are unlocked by IAP receipts, never by token balance, because Apple 3.1.1 bars crypto-based unlocks (precedents missed facts).
4. One-account-one-vote polls on next Sector Week theme (balance-independent, so holding more confers nothing).
5. Season Pass holders' redemptions batch weekly instead of monthly (a convenience, not value).

**Custody and wallet UX.** Each player silently gets an ERC-4337 smart account (EntryPoint v0.7 at 0x0000000071727De22E5E9d8BAf0edAc6f37da032 on 4663; Dynamic or Privy embedded wallets; Alchemy Gas Manager sponsorship at an 8% fee on ~$0.001 gas, tech #22-23 and robinhood.md missed facts). Players never hold ETH. "Link Robinhood Wallet" via WalletConnect deep link (F4, tech #21) registers a Robinhood Wallet address as the display and EU credit destination. Personal data stays off-chain; on-chain there is only the smart-account address and a season-badge nullifier (F17, EDPB v2.0).

**What stops it becoming Hamster Kombat (F18):** no engagement mint; no tradeability; no unlock cliff (there is nothing to unlock); rules published 7 days before each season and immutable within it (Hamster's late PPH-to-keys switch caused the boycott, p2e-lessons #11); three-ledger separation (points, token, stock) so a zero token price changes no payout; hard per-account caps; mint only to verified humans; and if Phase 4 ever happens, a 10%-of-net-revenue buyback paced at 2-5% of daily volume (economics #32) with the game explicitly designed to keep working at price zero.

## 4. Stock reward path by jurisdiction (Q2)

**United States: fractional VOO through a registered broker-dealer.**
- SOLO: Snowball Rewards LLC (the marketing affiliate) signs with Alpaca Securities (Broker API; JNLS security journals are documented for exactly "reward your users" flows, economics missed facts) or Apex via a Bits of Stock-style white label (legal-us #4). Player crosses $5 pending credit, opens a KYC'd 18+ brokerage account inside the app (CIP: name, DOB, address, SSN; legal-us #33). Snowball Rewards pre-funds a firm account, buys VOO at the close each day against claimed credits (Webull's claim-then-buy template, economics missed facts), and the broker journals fractional shares (5-decimal, Apex) into the player's account; sale proceeds are non-withdrawable for 30 days (Robinhood/Grifin template). The player sees "5,000 LP → $5.00 → 0.0087 VOO in your Snowball Brokerage account (by Alpaca)" and a read-only holdings card. Alternative SOLO form if the broker prefers it: a restricted cash credit that self-directs into VOO and is forfeited if unused within 60 days (Cash App's legal form, precedents skeptic 2).
- PARTNER: Robinhood buys VOO on the open market and credits the player's Robinhood brokerage account; Robinhood's referee sign-up stock reward (F6) serves as the ignition grant; Robinhood Connect application ID for wallet funding. No API or partner program exists today (F4), so this is a Phase 3 pitch.
- Why legal: third-party listed securities bought on the open market and delivered by a broker-dealer into a KYC'd account avoid Section 5 (F8); deterministic fixed-ratio credit removes chance (F9); compensation to the game is a flat marketing CPA never tied to trades or balances (Rule 2040 / 15(a), legal-us #6, counsel sign-off item); no Robinhood Stock Token ever reaches a US person (F2).

**EU/EEA: on-chain Stock Tokens, with Robinhood or a licensed party as the only distributor.**
- PARTNER (preferred, mirrors Robinhood's own practice): Robinhood Europe UAB runs the "Snowball Season Grant" under its own promotion terms, exactly like its Share Token Giveaway (VOO/NVDA tokens for no consideration, onboarded and appropriateness-tested clients only, 180-day hold; legal-eu #10). The player completes Robinhood EU onboarding (18+, EEA resident, legal-eu missed facts), Robinhood credits SPY/VOO Stock Tokens to the player's Robinhood Wallet, and Snowball pays RHEU a flat fee per verified redemption as a marketing partner with copy approved under MiFID II Art 24. Classic Stock Tokens are excluded entirely: they are cash-settled derivatives inside the CFD inducement ban and are non-transferable anyway (F14, legal-eu #14-15, missed facts).
- SOLO: the reward is a EURC credit (a MiCA e-money token; Robinhood Europe itself pays its Dividend Match in EURC capped at 1,000 EURC, robinhood.md missed facts) delivered by an EU-authorised EMI/CASP partner (ZBD holds MiCAR and EMI licences in the Netherlands, precedents #18) to the player's linked Robinhood Wallet on Robinhood Chain. The player may then, self-directed inside Robinhood Wallet, swap EURC for the SPY or VOO Stock Token on Uniswap (F1: 24/7 DEX, freely transferable Jersey debt securities). The game never holds or transfers a security (C3), never says "free stock" (UCPD Annex I item 20, legal-eu #21), and shows a neutral "hold / withdraw / swap" screen with no token recommendation. Whether even that neutral mention is a "promotion" of a prospectus offer is the open question in F14 and must be cleared by Lithuanian counsel before Phase 3; if not cleared, the SOLO screen drops the swap mention and the player simply receives EURC.
- Why legal: the Jersey tokens are MiFID transferable securities under an FMA-approved, passported prospectus whose consent covers only licensed Authorised Offerors (robinhood.md missed facts), so the game must never be the offeror; F14 puts Robinhood, not the game, in the distributor seat; deterministic allocation keeps NL/BE/IT gambling and promo-draw rules out (F15).

**Other eligible countries (Robinhood's 120+ list minus restricted):** the EU SOLO template, country by country, only after local counsel clears prize/promo, tax and e-money rules; Phase 3 launches with EEA only. **Excluded (UK, CA, CH, UAE, sanctioned list: Cuba, Belarus, Iran, North Korea, Russia, Syria, Ukraine, South Sudan, Sudan, Myanmar, Venezuela; F1, tech missed facts):** full game, LP redeemable only for Blueprints or gift cards via licensed rails where lawful, no real-asset rewards, no token promotion in the UK (F16). US persons are screened out of the Stock Token path by residency KYC, IP/geo, self-certification and Reg S wording.

## 5. Token-to-stock relationship (Q3)

DRIFT has no conversion, no fixed rate, no price link and no multiplier on any stock reward (C2; a token redeemable for stock is a custodial receipt or security-based swap, F10). The only relationships are gates and sinks: holding DRIFT is the membership condition for sponsor-drop enrolment, and burning DRIFT replaces an ad view for boosts on Android/web. Reward caps follow the Season Pass IAP entitlement, not token balance, so Google Play's "fixed ratio, not exponentiated by performance" rule (F9) and Apple 3.1.1 hold. Three ledgers (LP, DRIFT, VOO/Stock Token), three custodians (game, chain, broker/wallet), zero arithmetic between them.

## 6. Identity, sybil and fraud (Q4)

**Binding primitive (C5):** a Verified Person Attestation (VPA) issued by the licensed delivering party at first withdrawal: {hash(partner_customer_id), jurisdiction, is_18plus, issued_at}, signed, no PII to the game (F17). US: the broker's CIP produces it; EU SOLO: the EMI/CASP partner's KYC (Sumsub Basic $1.35 + AML ~$0.50, F22, tech #33); PARTNER: Robinhood onboarding. One VPA maps to exactly one game account (unique index on the hash); a second account presenting the same VPA is merged into the oldest and its pending credit forfeited. Brokerage KYC data is never repurposed for the game (AMLR retention, legal-eu #39).

**Pre-withdrawal signals (device and behaviour, never sufficient alone):** Play Integrity (PLAY_RECOGNIZED, LICENSED, MEETS_DEVICE_INTEGRITY; 10k/day default quota means we request only at claim and withdraw, tech #29) and App Attest; server-authoritative simulation with deterministic replay of the client input log from the last checkpoint (F23), so impossible progress per elapsed second is rejected; rewarded-ad server-side verification callbacks (AdMob ECDSA, Unity HMAC, AppLovin MAX, LevelPlay) with idempotent transaction IDs, and LP credited only on a verified callback (F24, tech #32); a 3-minute rewarded cooldown and 10/day hard cap; Hamster-style behavioural clustering (same-pixel taps, synchronised logins, shared ASN/IP, 400-accounts-one-address patterns, precedents #31) and Sardine-class fleet detection for physical device farms (+72% YoY, tech #31).

**Withdrawal gating (C4):** $5 minimum; 14-day settlement window (Acorns holds 60-120 days, Mistplay uses pending windows); first withdrawal requires the VPA; VPN/proxy/emulator blocks withdrawal, never gameplay; per-account daily/season/year caps; velocity rules on claim-to-withdraw timing.

**Cheaters:** pending credit forfeited, rewards tab disabled, game still playable; a 72-hour human appeal path and monthly published enforcement counts (opaque bans right before payout are the top trust complaint, p2e-lessons #33); legitimate players never see retroactive rule changes (C1).

**KYC cost handling:** US CIP cost sits inside the broker CPA economics; EU checks (~$2 all-in) are paid only when pending credit is ≥ $5, so KYC never exceeds 40% of a first payout and is amortised across the $30 annual cap; budget comes from the non-pool half of CPAs and general margin. Expect ~50% of Android finance-style installs to be fake pre-KYC (F22); the funnel, not the payout layer, absorbs them.

## 7. Funding stack and payout rule (Q5)

**Revenue lines:** (1) rewarded video (SSV-verified), (2) interstitials, (3) IAP (starter pack, Blueprints, ad-removal, Season Pass), (4) broker acquisition CPAs (affiliate $20-$50 per funded account SOLO, F6/economics #13; bespoke PARTNER at $50+, the value Robinhood itself now assigns to one funded referral, F6), (5) sponsor escrow.

**The Snowball Rule (published in-app and in the Earnings Call):**
Pool(S+1) = 20% × net ad revenue(S) + 10% × net IAP revenue(S) + 50% × broker CPAs cash-received(S) + 100% × sponsor escrow earmarked for drops in S+1. "Net" means after network and store fees; "received" means banked, never accrued. The season LP rate is set 7 days before S+1: rate(S+1) = clamp(Pool(S+1) / projected LP(S+1), 0.5 × rate(S), 1.25 × rate(S)), fixed for the whole season. Accrual is fixed-ratio: 10 LP per verified rewarded ad, 5 LP per $1 of IAP, nothing for referrals, installs or social posts (Apple 3.1.5(v), F9). The token is not a term in the formula; the CPA term can be zero.

**Per-account caps:** $0.15/day and $3/season and $30/year (base); $0.25/day, $5/season, $50/year with the Season Pass entitlement; plus one lifetime $5 "Ignition Grant" of VOO or EURC paid from the CPA share, pre-published as contingent on a CPA actually being received (if the broker channel closes, the grant pauses next season, not mid-season). Maximum legal annual value ≈ $55, an order of magnitude under the $2,000 1099-MISC threshold (F12), under Mistplay's $550 (F19), and in the spirit of Stash's $10/month bonus cap (precedents skeptic 2).

**Sponsor drops (F21):** sponsors buy a Sector Week skin plus a fixed grant, e.g. "$0.50 of VOO to the first 10,000 verified players who complete Sector Week," funded 100% into escrow before the event, deterministic and pre-published. Rewards are always a third-party index or sector ETF, never the sponsor's own stock (Reg M Rules 101/102, Rule 10b-18, Section 5; Landa 2023 terminated, economics #30). Pitch data: Bumped's +40% spend and +100% after $5-$10 grants (F21). Sponsor money is additive; the base pool never depends on it (Bumped died when brands stopped paying, precedents #3). EU drops run only through the PARTNER path or as EURC credit.

**Broker bounty use:** half of every CPA funds the Ignition Grant and the pool; half covers KYC, broker journal fees, 1099 administration and margin. Robinhood's new Tiered Referrals is for individual US residents and excludes paid creators (F6), so the game uses affiliate CPAs SOLO and negotiates a flat-fee marketing agreement PARTNER; counsel confirms the agreement is not transaction-based (Rule 2040).

**Worked example, US-heavy cohort, per 1,000 DAU, one 30-day season (assumptions from F20-F22):**
- Ads: 3.0 rewarded/DAU/day at $12 eCPM = $36/day; 1.5 interstitials/DAU/day at $6 eCPM = $9/day; gross $1,350; net of 15% network take = **$1,150**.
- IAP: $0.030 gross ARPDAU (idle titles skew 60-70% ads, idle-design #26) = $900 gross; net of 25% blended store fee = **$675**.
- Broker CPAs: 6% of DAU-count cross $5 in the season, 50% complete KYC, 65% fund → ~20 funded accounts × $35 = **$700** SOLO ($1,000 at a $50 PARTNER rate).
- Sponsor: $0 in the base case.
- Net revenue: $2,525. Pool = 230 + 67.5 + 350 + 0 = **$647.5 (25.6% of net revenue; $0.0216 per DAU-day)**.
- Demand at a published rate of 1,000 LP = $1.00: 55% of DAU watch ~3 verified ads = 1,650 ads/day × 10 LP = $16.50/day = $495/season; IAP LP = $45; 20 Ignition Grants = $100; total **$640 ≤ $647.5**. Caps bind for heavy users, not the pool.
- Costs outside the pool: EU-style KYC not needed (broker CIP inside CPA); journal fees ~$20; infra ~$0.02/DAU-month = $20; 1099 admin negligible under caps. Game retains ~$1,850 (73%) for UA, team and margin.
- **Stress 1, token price zero:** no term changes; pool unchanged.
- **Stress 2, Robinhood never partners and affiliate CPAs vanish:** pool = $297.5; rate resets to 2,000 LP = $1 (the 0.5× floor), Ignition Grant paused; demand = $247 + $22 = $269 ≤ $297.5; payout ratio 16% of $1,825 net, still inside the ZBD 10-20% envelope (F19).
- **Stress 3, EU cohort at $7 blended eCPM (F20):** ad net ~$670, IAP net ~$600, CPA $0 (no documented Robinhood EU affiliate) → pool $194 → EU-specific published rate 3,000 LP = $1; demand ~$180. Per-geo rates are fixed-ratio within a season and mirror Bitcoin Miner's per-country caps (precedents skeptic 2).
- **Stress 4, 40% bot share pre-KYC:** bots earn LP but cannot pass the VPA, so their LP expire unredeemed (180-day expiry, Mistplay template); the pool is sized on realized revenue, and bot ad impressions that networks scrub never enter "net."

## 9. Launch sequencing (Q7)

**Phase 0 (months 0-3): the game, nothing else.** Server-authoritative core, break_infinity math shared client/server, LiveOps backend from day one (AdCap's lesson, idle-design #20). Soft launch in Canada and New Zealand (Canada is excluded from Stock Tokens, which makes it a clean pure-game test). Gate: D1 ≥ 40%, D7 ≥ 15%, ad ARPDAU ≥ $0.03 US-equivalent, rewarded opt-in ≥ 45%, replay-validation false-positive rate < 0.5%. Counsel: none beyond store compliance and COPPA neutral age screen.

**Phase 1 (months 3-6): US launch with Ledger Points and SOLO broker delivery.** Points first, no token. Broker-dealer agreement (Alpaca/Apex), affiliate CPA enrolment, Snowball Rule published, Earnings Call cadence starts. Counsel sign-off before launch: securities (open-market third-party stock via BD; Rule 2040 flat-fee marketing agreement; FINRA 2210 principal approval of co-branded copy), state gambling (deterministic, no NY/FL filings needed because no chance), FTC earnings-claim substantiation and in-app typical-earnings disclosure, tax characterisation (prize vs NEC) and state withholding, COPPA/18+ gating. Measured before the Phase 2 gate: payout ratio ≤ 25% of net revenue, verified-account fraud rate < 5%, ≥ 2% of DAU-count converting to funded accounts per season, chargeback/refund fraud < 0.3% of IAP.

**Phase 2 (months 6-9): DRIFT on Robinhood Chain mainnet, soulbound.** Audit booked in Phase 1 (8-14 weeks, F24), Clubhouse dapp, Season Pass credential minting, Robinhood Wallet linking, Arbitrum One mirror ready. Counsel: SEC-CFTC digital-tool memo; MiCA Art 4(3)/(5) opinion from Lithuanian counsel; Apple 3.1.1/3.1.5 and Google Play Financial Features declaration review; Robinhood Chain ToS and brand-guideline compliance. Gate: both stores approve the build with the token live; mint/burn ratio ≤ 2:1 in the first season; zero PII on-chain per DPIA.

**Phase 3 (months 9-12): EU/EEA rewards.** SOLO EURC credit via an EU EMI/CASP partner; PARTNER pitch to Robinhood Europe (they already run Share Token Giveaways) timed after HOOD Summit (Sept 29-30, 2026). Counsel: Lithuanian counsel on third-party mention of Stock Tokens and the Prospectus/MiFID distribution question (F14), UCPD copy review, per-country tax (DE, NL, FR, IE, LT), GDPR DPIA, EMT delivery mechanics. ## 10. Why this beats the obvious alternatives, and what gets cut first

**Against a tradeable engagement token (Hamster/Notcoin/Pixels):** every one lost 95-99.9% and shed users at ~1M/day post-TGE (F18); ours cannot, because there is nothing to dump and no payout depends on it.
**Against randomized stock rewards (Robinhood's old $5-$200):** chance plus gameplay effort is the highest-risk gambling design (F9), violates Google Play's fixed-ratio rule, and is exactly what Massachusetts fined (F7).
**Against a token redeemable for stock:** that is a custodial receipt or security-based swap (F10); we keep three ledgers with no arithmetic between them.
**Against the game holding or transferring Stock Tokens itself:** the prospectus consents only to licensed offerors and F14 puts Robinhood in the distributor seat; the SOLO EURC credit keeps us outside securities distribution in every jurisdiction.
**Against a pure ad-funded sats-style game:** it pays cents (Bitcoin Miner ~$0.25/day cap, F19) and has no acquisition economics; adding the broker CPA more than doubles the pool per 1,000 DAU and delivers the thing the ask actually wants: players holding an index fund.
**Against building the token first:** Big Time made $100M with no token sale; Gigaverse $4.5M with no token (p2e-lessons #21, #27). The token is the least load-bearing piece here.

**Cut order if forced:** (1) EU on-chain path first (fall back to LP → gift cards, keep the US path); (2) DRIFT entirely (the game and the stock rewards need nothing from it; fidelity to the ask drops, legal surface drops more); (3) sponsor drops; (4) Season Pass cap tiers. Never cut: the server-authoritative core, the published Snowball Rule, the VPA gate, or the honest "cents to a few dollars" positioning.

## Top risks
- Lithuanian counsel concludes that even a neutral in-app mention of swapping EURC into Stock Tokens is a promotion of a prospectus offer or MiFID distribution by an unlicensed party (F14 open question); mitigation is the EURC-only SOLO screen or the Robinhood Europe PARTNER route, and if both fail the EU path collapses to points-for-gift-cards.
- Rule 2040 / Exchange Act 15(a) finder risk if any broker compensation is construed as transaction-based; mitigation is a flat marketing fee per funded account with a counsel opinion, and if unresolved, dropping CPAs from the pool (modelled: pool still positive).
- App-store rejection: Apple 3.1.1 (crypto-based unlocks), 3.1.5 (crypto app paying for tasks) and Google Play's fixed-ratio loyalty and Financial Features declaration rules; mitigation is IAP-only entitlements on iOS, deterministic fixed-ratio LP, and no earnings marketing.
- Fraud and device farms: ~50% of Android finance-style installs fake and affiliate traffic at 36x SRN fraud rates (F22); the identity attestation stops payouts to bots but UA metrics and ad revenue quality will be polluted, and KYC cost per verified player must stay under 40% of first payout.
- Thin EU unit economics ($5-9 eCPM, no documented Robinhood EU affiliate CPA) yield a pool of ~$0.007 per DAU-day, which may read as insulting rewards; mitigation is honest positioning and geo-specific rates.
- Robinhood Chain governance: single sequencer with address hash-list filtering could reject the game's contracts, and ToS terminology/brand rules could trigger takedowns; mitigation is the Arbitrum One mirror, off-chain ledger of record and copy compliance.
- FTC earnings-claim enforcement and UCPD 'free' rules if marketing drifts toward 'earn stock by playing'; mitigation is typical-earnings disclosure and no 'free stock' copy anywhere.
- The core idle game fails to hit D1 ≥ 40% / D7 ≥ 15% in soft launch, in which case the reward layer must not ship (C6); the whole stack depends on the game being fun alone.
- Tax characterisation (prize vs compensation for services, state withholding, FR/DE/NL treatment) could impose reporting burdens the caps were designed to avoid; mitigation is counsel sign-off before Phase 1 and Phase 3.
- CLARITY fails and Regulation Crypto Assets stalls, leaving the token's US classification on the interpretive release alone; acceptable because DRIFT stays soulbound and nothing in the payout stack depends on it.
