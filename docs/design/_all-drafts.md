# DRAFT 1: Long Run

A patience-themed idle tycoon where you compound a holding company across 11 sectors and take it public; a closed-loop Ledger converts verified ad/IAP activity into cents-to-dollars of broad-market ETF delivered by a licensed broker (US) or Robinhood Europe (EU), while a separate consumptive LONG token on Robinhood Chain (points first, soulbound second, transferable only behind revenue and counsel gates) never touches the stock path.

# Long Run: architecture proposal (2026-09-01)

Design stance: the game must be a top-quartile idle game with every real-value feature switched off. Ledger (stock points) and LONG (token) are bolted onto monetizing events and cosmetic/LiveOps surfaces only; neither ever changes a generator, a cost curve, or a prestige formula. Anything needing Robinhood cooperation is marked PARTNER with a SOLO fallback (C9).

## 1. Concept and theme

**Name:** Long Run. **Fantasy:** you are a patient founder building a holding company one sector at a time, compounding profits, taking companies public, and eventually getting your conglomerate "included in the index". The name is the message: the game rewards holding, not trading.

**Why the theme survives the ESMA/SEC gamification lens (F7, legal-eu #18-19):** there is no buy/sell verb for any real asset anywhere in the app; the game never shows a real price, never celebrates a trade, has no streak tied to market activity, and never nudges toward a specific security. Real-world exposure is always a broad-market or sector ETF chosen by the player from a fixed list at claim time, never a single stock and never chosen by game logic (avoids the Massachusetts "scratch-off/free stock" pattern and issuer Reg M problems, F21). Compounding is the mechanic (exponential cost, reinvestment automation, prestige), not a lecture.

**Vocabulary that carries the theme:** 11 GICS-style sectors are the generator rows (Corner Store/Staples, Sneaker Lab/Discretionary, Machine Shop/Industrials, Quarry/Materials, Wind Farm/Energy, Water Works/Utilities, Credit Union/Financials, Clinic/Health, Apartments/Real Estate, Radio Station/Comms, App Studio/Tech). "Go Public" (IPO) is prestige; "Float" is the prestige currency; "Index Inclusion" is the second prestige layer that opens new worlds (Main Street, then Global Markets, then Frontier); "Analyst Reports" are dailies; "Earnings Season" is the weekend event; "Golden Charters" are per-business permanent multipliers; "Board Seats" are LONG stakes that vote on LiveOps content; "Buybacks" are literally the operator's revenue-funded LONG buybacks, named honestly.

**Copy rules (robinhood.md ToS 2026-08-24, legal-eu #13, #21):** EU copy says "Stock Tokens" and "Built on Robinhood Chain", never "tokenized stocks/equities", Long Run brand primary; the real-value reward is called the "Long Run Bonus" and is always labelled "a promotional reward, not an investment return, taxable, value can fall"; no earnings claims in store listings (Google Play Blockchain policy; Freecash removal, p2e #34).

## 2. Core loop and progression

**Currencies (all closed-loop except where stated):**
- Cash: run-scoped soft currency, big-number (break_infinity.js, idle-design #29).
- Float: prestige currency, +2% all profit per Float, permanent.
- Index Points: second-layer prestige, unlock worlds and global perks.
- Gems: IAP hard currency, non-expiring (Apple 3.1.1), never convertible out.
- Ledger: reward points (section 7), off-chain, closed-loop, redeemable only into the stock path. Not a token.
- LONG: on-chain token (section 3). Zero role in the loop above.

**Generators:** cost_next = base × r^owned (idle-design #1). Tier bases 4, 60, 720, 8.6e3, 1.04e5, 1.24e6, 1.49e7, 1.79e8, 2.15e9, 2.58e10, 3.1e11 (×12 per tier); r by tier 1.07, 1.15, 1.14, 1.13, 1.12, 1.11, 1.10, 1.09, 1.08, 1.08, 1.07; base cycle 0.6s, 3s, 6s, 12s, 24s, 96s, 384s, 1,536s, 6,144s, 36,864s, 86,400s; profit per cycle scales ~×12 per tier so cost/profit ratio drifts from 2.4 to ~8, creating walls at tiers 4, 7 and 10 (F23 "walls as energy system"). Milestones: ×2 profit at 25, 50, 100, 200, 300, 400, then ×2 speed at every further 100 (AdCap 25/50 and speed-doubler pattern). Managers (one-time cash purchase per row) automate collection; "Reinvest" (unlocked after first manager) auto-buys the cheapest affordable unit every cycle, which is the compounding fantasy made mechanical. Golden Charters (Gems or event reward) give ×7.77 per business, ×10 extra when all 11 rows are chartered (AdCap golden-ticket pattern).

**Prestige ("Go Public"):** Float = 150 × sqrt(L / K_world) − claimed, K tuned per world (AdCap uses different constants per world) so a day-1 player who plays three ~8-minute sessions reaches ~50 Float (×2) at first IPO in ~3 hours of elapsed time; doubling Float thereafter needs ~4× lifetime earnings, giving 1-2 IPOs/day in week 1 and ~1 every 2-3 days by week 3. Float shop (spend Float, not just hold it): warehouse hours, starting cash %, sector unlock discounts, and the "Prestige Exponent" upgrade that moves sqrt toward 0.55 (Antimatter Dimensions makes the exponent itself upgradeable) as the long-tail goal. Second layer: Index Inclusion at 1e15 Main Street lifetime earnings grants Index Points (cube-root of lifetime, Cookie Clicker style) which permanently multiply Float gain and unlock Global Markets (new 11 rows, new K) then Frontier; each world resets Float and cash, keeps Index Points, Charters and cosmetics.

**Offline:** Egg Inc silo model (idle-design missed facts): the Warehouse holds 8h at 100% rate at launch, upgradeable via Float to 12/16/24h; ad-free subscribers get 24h. Offline yield is computed server-side from server time and the last checkpoint; the return screen offers one rewarded ad for ×2 offline. 24h cap justifies the daily login without trapping players (Melvor at 24h).

**Session shape:** log in → collect offline (optional ×2 ad) → Reinvest has bought units, buy managers/milestone bumps → one Analyst Report task → optional ×2-for-15-min rewarded ad → set up the next wall → log out. Target 3-4 sessions/day of 6-10 min (Kolibri ~10 min, GameAnalytics 3.9 sessions/day).

**First 5 minutes:** tap to run the Corner Store (0-20s), buy second unit (30s), first manager on Corner Store (90s, tap loop ends), Sneaker Lab unlocks (2:30), first ×2 milestone at 25 (4:00, a visible "bump"), Machine Shop unlocks (5:00). No interstitial in session 1; first rewarded offer (×2 profit) appears at ~6 min and is optional.

**Day-1 beats:** Machine Shop → Quarry → Wind Farm by minute 25; first wall at Water Works (~45 min elapsed, forces the first real offline wait); Go Public tutorial when Float ≥ 30 (~2.5-3h elapsed); starter pack ($2.99, permanent ×3 profit + 1 Golden Charter) shown once at the third sector unlock, the documented conversion moment (idle-design #27). **Day-7 beats:** 6-10 IPOs, all 11 Main Street rows owned, first Earnings Season event completed, Index Inclusion in sight, season pass offer.

**Ads:** no interstitial before 90s or in session 1; from session 2, max 1 interstitial per session, 3-min spacing (idle-design #24). Rewarded placements: ×2 profit 15 min (cap 4/day), 4h time-skip (cap 2/day), ×2 offline (1/session), Analyst Report re-roll (1/day); hard cap 8/day, target 3/DAU (F20).

**Monetization that respects the player:** starter pack $2.99; Gems for Charters, time-skips, Float boosts (never for Ledger or LONG); ad-removal $3.99/30d ($0.99/7d) which also grants the 24h warehouse; Season Pass $9.99/30d tied to Earnings Season content (cosmetic HQ, event currency ×2, Float bonus). No loot boxes, no paid random items anywhere (keeps PEGI 12, avoids Belgium and odds-disclosure rules, legal-eu #22, #25, PEGI 2026-03-12). Expected split ~55% ads / 45% IAP.

**LiveOps:** weekly 72h Earnings Season (a temporary 6-row event sector with its own currency; rewards Golden Charters, Float % and cosmetics; Kolibri event mines doubled weekend revenue); monthly season with pass; daily Analyst Reports (3 tasks, none involving real assets); quarterly Index Inclusion world drops; LONG Board Seat votes choose which sector hosts the next Earnings Season (section 3). Backend from day one (PlayFab-style segmentation; AdCap lost events by launching client-only, idle-design #20).

**Targets:** D1 45%, D7 18%, D30 7%, blended ARPDAU $0.06-0.08, rewarded opt-in 50%+ (medians 22/4/0.75; idle titles report 60%+ D1, F23). Kolibri-style D1-only gate during soft launch.

**Anti-cheat baseline (before any real value):** client simulates, server checkpoints every 60s and on every purchase; deterministic replay of the input log since last checkpoint on the server (cheap: Tideward ~30k actions/24h); monotonic elapsedRealtime plus server time for offline; affordability and rate-limit checks; Play Integrity/App Attest only at claim and withdrawal (10k/day default quota, tech #29-30).

## 3. Token model (Q1: points first, soulbound second, transferable only behind gates)

**Legal category.** LONG is designed to be a "digital tool" under the SEC-CFTC interpretive release (F10; legal-us #19): membership/credential-like, consumptive, no yield, no revenue share, no buyback-to-holder promises, no marketing of price. Because it is paid "in exchange for" activity it is outside the airdrop interpretation (legal-us #20), so its non-security status rests entirely on utility and on never being promoted as an investment. In the EU it is a MiCA utility token relying on Art 4(3) "access to a good or service that exists or is in operation" (F13; legal-eu #2) because every utility below is live before any token exists; we do not rely on the "free" limb (personal data is collected). We never seek admission to a CASP trading platform, including Robinhood's crypto exchange (Art 4(4) would collapse the exemption; Robinhood listing is discretionary and fee-bearing anyway, robinhood.md #31). Art 4(5) means custody/transfer of an exempt token is not itself a CASP service, but a non-EU studio may not provide crypto services to EU players at all (legal-eu #6), so the studio only ever runs its own contracts and never custodies for others.

**Phase A (launch, months 0-9): no token.** Nothing to promise, nothing to dump.

**Phase B (month ~10): soulbound LONG on Robinhood Chain (chain 4663).** Immutable OZ ERC20Capped (1,000,000,000 fixed cap) with transfers reverting except mint, burn and calls into whitelisted utility contracts; upgradeable periphery behind a 7-day-timelocked multisig (tech #35, #37). Gas sponsored via Alchemy Gas Manager/ZeroDev, both documented on Robinhood Chain (robinhood.md AA facts); ETH-funded. Balances accrue off-chain and mint only when a verified player claims to a wallet (Robinhood Wallet via WalletConnect, which natively supports Robinhood Chain, or an embedded Dynamic/Turnkey wallet). Players who never want crypto never see a wallet. Wallet-to-account linkage lives off-chain with a DPIA (F17, EDPB v2.0).

**Emission (pre-published, never retroactive, F18):** genesis: 10 LONG per verified active day since launch, capped at 200 days, snapshot announced 30 days ahead; ongoing: 10 LONG per verified active day (Analyst Report completed), 300/season cap, one verified human per account (section 6). No emission for game performance, referrals, social posts or installs (Apple 3.1.5(v); Google fixed-ratio rule; UK/AppsFlyer referral fraud 36x, F22).

**Allocation:** players 40% (10% genesis, 30% seasonal reserve released only 1:1 against realized buybacks, max 2.5% of supply per season), treasury 30%, team 15% (12-month cliff, 36-month linear), liquidity/MM 5% (1-3% supply loan structure only after Phase C), Robinhood-community/ecosystem 10% (PARTNER: Lighter-style allocation to Robinhood Wallet users, robinhood.md #15; SOLO: Arbitrum Open House buildathon and ecosystem grants). No investor token sale; the company is equity-funded.

**Utility (all consumptive, all deterministic, all off the core loop):**
1. Ad-replacement boosts: spend LONG instead of watching a rewarded ad for the same ×2/time-skip boost (Android and web companion only; on iOS LONG is view-only because Apple 3.1.1 bars unlocking functionality via own mechanisms/crypto). Spent LONG is burned. This gives LONG a floor utility value of roughly one ad view (~1 cent) and earns zero Ledger, since no revenue occurred.
2. Board Seats: lock LONG for a season to vote which sector hosts the next Earnings Season and which cosmetic set ships (content governance, not corporate governance).
3. Charters and HQ cosmetics: burn LONG to mint digital collectibles (skins, plaques, sector badges) on Robinhood Chain; displayed in-app, never unlocking features (Apple 3.1.1 NFT rule).
4. Guild treasury and naming: create a Guild, register a name on the on-chain leaderboard, fund co-op Earnings Season goals.
5. PARTNER: Robinhood Connect application ID for fiat on-ramp into the web companion (docs exist at docs.robinhood.com/crypto/connect but require a Robinhood-issued application-id; robinhood.md skeptic correction to #20).

**Phase C (month 15+, gated): transferability.** Gates: 12 months of revenue history; buyback treasury pre-funded with six months of the 20%-of-net-revenue commitment; US counsel memo (digital tool, no investment marketing, MSB analysis of buybacks, NY geo-fence absent a BitLicense, F11); Lithuanian counsel memo (Art 4(3) holds, no admission to trading sought, marketing under Art 7 style disclaimers); Robinhood Chain sequencer filtering risk accepted or Arbitrum One fallback deployed (tech #5, #8). Only verified-human balances become transferable; unverified balances stay soulbound. Unlock is tranched: 25% at switch-on, 75% streamed over 12 months (own vesting contract or Sablier, already on chain 4663). Post-switch, ongoing player accrual is paid first from market buybacks (20% of net revenue, paced daily at ≤3% of daily volume, resting bids, economics #32) and only then from the seasonal reserve 1:1, so circulating supply to players grows only as revenue grows (Off The Grid 30%-of-gross pattern, p2e #25; Illuvium revenue-gated release, p2e #24).

**What stops Hamster Kombat (F18):** no engagement-minted supply after genesis; supply to players is revenue-matched; per-human daily/season caps; a spend sink (boosts/charters) that burns LONG before unlock; tranched unlock; rules published before play and changed only forward with 30-day notice; bot bans applied before genesis snapshot (Notcoin/Hamster precedent); "if a player farms LONG they get boosts and cosmetics, not a stock claim".

**Custody/wallet UX:** invisible until wanted; one tap "Claim to Robinhood Wallet" (WalletConnect deep link) or "Create wallet" (embedded); balance shown in-app on all platforms; spending on iOS routed to the web companion.

## 4. Stock reward path by jurisdiction (Q2)

The reward unit everywhere is the "Long Run Bonus": a USD/EUR-denominated credit from the Ledger (section 7) that becomes a broad-market ETF exposure (default S&P 500 ETF; player may pick any of 11 sector ETFs from a fixed list at claim). Never single stocks, never the sponsor's own stock (F21, Landa 2023). Minimum claim $5; delivery within 7 days after fraud review; the game never holds or transfers a security (C3).

**US (fractional shares via broker).**
- SOLO (launch rail): white-label broker-dealer with a B2B rewards primitive. Alpaca Broker API documents "JNLS" security journals explicitly for rewarding users and "JNLC" cash journals firm-to-user (economics #24, skeptic correction); Apex "Stock Rewards" is the Bits of Stock rail (legal-us #4). Flow: at first claim the player opens a brokerage account inside the app via the broker's embedded CIP/KYC (18+, name/DOB/address/SSN; broker owns the KYC data); the game funds the reward account; the broker journals cash and executes the player's standing ETF instruction (Cash App/Grifin "restricted cash credit, forfeited if unused in 90 days" template, precedents skeptic-2 missed facts). Player sees: "Long Run Bonus $5.00 → 0.0087 SPY, held at [Broker], sellable after 3 trading days, proceeds withdrawable after 30 days" (Robinhood's own lock pattern, robinhood.md #23). Funding: game's reward budget. Delivery: the broker-dealer. Legal basis: third-party listed security bought on the open market via a registered BD into a KYC'd account (F8); deterministic fixed-ratio value (F9, C1); Google Play fixed-ratio loyalty declaration; Apple 3.2.2(x) incentive for in-app actions.
- PARTNER: Robinhood delivers the same credit into a Robinhood brokerage account (no public rail exists, F4; Robinhood's Partner Stock Program only covers companies gifting their own stock). Compensation to Long Run structured as flat marketing fees or the public Impact affiliate CPA ($20-$50/qualifying action, economics #13), never per-trade, with counsel on FINRA 2040 (legal-us #6) and FINRA 2210 review of co-branded copy. Fractional shares cannot ACAT between brokers (legal-us #11), so a player who later wants Robinhood must liquidate the SOLO account; disclosed up front.
- Phase 3 option, not launch: Dinari (US-eligible dShares since 2026-08-04, but its own docs carry contradictory Reg S language, tech skeptic #15) or Superstate (US in scope, allowlist, small-caps only). Neither is Robinhood; neither needed for fidelity.

**EU/EEA (on-chain Stock Tokens).**
- Asset: Robinhood on-chain Stock Tokens (Jersey-issued debt securities, freely transferable ERC-20 on chain 4663, F1). Classic Stock Tokens are excluded outright: non-transferable derivatives likely within CFD product-intervention measures whose benefit ban covers gifts and bonuses (F14; legal-eu #14-15, recital 47). Broad ETF token resolved live from api.robinhood.com/rhj/assets (Invesco QQQ confirmed in the catalogue); addresses never hard-coded (counterfeit-ticker warning, tech missed facts).
- PARTNER (required for stock delivery at launch): Robinhood Europe UAB runs the promotion under its own giveaway-terms template (onboarded, appropriateness-tested clients, 180-day hold, "not available to the general public", F5, legal-eu #10); Long Run is the marketing partner and pays RHEU a promotion fee that funds the tokens; RHEU delivers into the player's Robinhood Wallet. Player sees: "Long Run Bonus EUR 5 → claim in Robinhood" → deep link → RHEU checks onboarding, returns an attestation (verified, 18+, EEA resident; no identity data, F17) → tokens appear in Robinhood Wallet with the 180-day hold displayed. Legal basis: the licensed distributor makes the offer under the FMA-Liechtenstein-approved base prospectus passported to 28 EEA states (legal-eu missed facts); the game never distributes securities. Counsel question left open by research and required before build: whether a third-party marketing partner of an Authorised Offeror is fine as "mere marketing" or needs a tied-agent structure (legal-eu #16).
- SOLO fallback (only if Lithuanian counsel clears): the Bonus is paid as a MiCA-compliant e-money token on Robinhood Chain (USDG is the chain's core stablecoin; EURC is used by Robinhood's own Dividend Match; issuer-entity MiCA status to be confirmed) to the player's Robinhood Wallet; the player self-directs a swap to a Stock Token inside Robinhood Wallet's own swap. Long Run embeds no swap widget and gives no instrument-specific instruction, to stay clear of RTO/placing and Prospectus Regulation distribution. If counsel says no: EU Ledger redeems to Gems at 2× value until PARTNER lands.

**Other eligible countries (Robinhood's 120+ list minus US/CA/UK/CH/UAE/sanctioned):** same PARTNER/SOLO structure as EU but default OFF; countries added one at a time with local counsel (RHJ restricted list mirrored, tech missed facts). No self-invented delivery.

**Excluded (UK, CA, CH, UAE, sanctioned, and any US state or country counsel has not cleared):** full game, Ledger accrues, redeems only to Gems at 2× value; no real-value reward (F16; UK free-crypto promotion ban, legal-eu #33; Canada crypto-only Robinhood). Under-18 accounts everywhere: same Gems-only rule (COPPA/GDPR Art 8 two-tier age gate: 13+ to play, 18+ for value).

## 5. Token-to-stock relationship (Q3)

Decision: **no value relationship.** LONG is never convertible to Ledger, to the Bonus, to stock or to cash by the operator, at any rate, fixed or floating; Ledger is never convertible to LONG. This is the only design that cannot be read as a custodial receipt or security-based swap (F10; legal-us #23) and keeps LONG outside money-transmitter "convertible value" (F11). The two systems share exactly one thing: the verified-human identity (section 6), so a banned sybil loses both. Thematic tie only: Board Seat votes can pick which sector ETF is featured in the next claim screen's default ordering (the player still chooses). We explicitly reject "stake LONG to raise the stock multiplier" and "LONG gates sponsor drops" because both let a token purchase buy stock exposure, which is the C2 line.

## 6. Identity, sybil and fraud (Q4)

**Primitive:** one verified adult human per game account, established by the licensed delivering party at first withdrawal (C5). US: the partner broker's CIP result; EU: RHEU's onboarding attestation (PARTNER) or a Sumsub/Veriff check run by Long Run (SOLO, $1.35-$1.89/check, tech #33). The game stores only {salted hash of the provider's unique person id, verified=true, age≥18, jurisdiction, timestamp}; KYC documents never reach the game and brokerage KYC is never repurposed (F17, AMLR retention). Duplicate hash → second account's Ledger frozen pending appeal; balances merge to the first account only after human review.

**Before verification (no real value at stake):** Play Integrity (PLAY_RECOGNIZED, LICENSED, MEETS_DEVICE_INTEGRITY) and App Attest at claim time only; device intelligence (emulator, cloned app, VPN/proxy, velocity); behavioural fingerprints (identical tap timing, synchronized logins, pixel-identical inputs, "fleet" devices; the Hamster ban playbook, precedents #31); series-of-events validation that Ledger-earning sessions contain plausible progression (Playrix pattern, p2e #36). Unverified Ledger balance capped at $5; verification unlocks the rest.

**Ad SSV:** Ledger for a rewarded view is credited only on the network's server-side callback (AdMob/Unity/AppLovin/LevelPlay signatures, tech #32), idempotent on transaction id, capped at 8/day, with view-rate anomaly flags; interstitials earn nothing.

**Withdrawal gating:** first claim requires verification and a 14-active-day tenure; every claim sits 7 days in "pending" (fraud review; IAP-derived Ledger vests after 35 days to cover refund fraud, p2e #36); daily/season/year caps (section 7); one destination account per identity; Robinhood Chain "Transaction rejected by chain policy" errors surfaced as a compliance block, not retried.

**Optional accelerator:** World ID Orb proof (~18M humans, cloud verify, nullifier stored per action, tech #34) raises the unverified cap to $10 and shortens review; never required (coverage), never a substitute for the broker/RHEU attestation.

**Cheaters:** published sanction ladder: Ledger and pending claims forfeited → reward program ban (game remains playable) → device and identity ban; every ban carries an appeal with human review inside 14 days (reward-app trust failures, p2e #33). Referrals pay nothing of real value (Apple 3.1.5(v); affiliate fraud 36×, F22). Biometric selfie checks are done only by the KYC vendor/broker, never stored by the game (JustPlay BIPA settlement, precedents missed facts).

**KYC cost handling:** paid from the operating budget as CAC for the verified cohort, incurred only when a player reaches the $5 claim threshold; ~$3/account all-in (vendor + broker onboarding); modelled at 7% of revenue in section 7. In the EU PARTNER path RHEU bears onboarding cost (it is acquiring a client).

## 7. Funding stack and payout rule (Q5)

**Revenue lines:** (1) rewarded video, (2) light interstitials, (3) IAP (starter pack, Gems, ad-removal, season pass), (4) broker acquisition bounties (affiliate CPA $20-$50 Robinhood, $20-$70 typical, F6/F20; PARTNER flat fees), (5) sponsor drop fees (pass-through), (6) LONG treasury (never counted toward payouts).

**Published Dividend Rule (C4):** each calendar month the Reward Budget B = 20% of net rewarded-ad revenue + 10% of net IAP revenue + 50% of realized broker bounties + 100% of pre-funded sponsor fees + carryover. Ledger accrual rates for the next month are published on the 25th and never applied retroactively. Ledger unit: 10,000 Ledger = $1.00.

**Accrual (fixed ratios, geo-tiered):** rewarded view: 20 Ledger (Tier 1: US), 10 (Tier 2: EU/EEA), 5 (Tier 3: other eligible); Analyst Report completed: 20/10/5; 7-day streak: 100/50/25; IAP: 1,000 Ledger per $1 net. Founding Bonus: on first verified claim a fixed FB (published monthly, floor $2, ceiling $10; $3 in SOLO, $10 with PARTNER bounties) is added. Sponsor drops: fixed $ per eligible verified head (14-day streak in the season), same for everyone, delivered 60 days later to absorb cancellations (Acorns pattern).

**Caps per account:** 500 Ledger/day ($0.05) from base accrual; $15 per 90-day season including Founding Bonus and sponsor drops; $50 per calendar year. This sits under every threshold that matters: 1099-MISC $2,000 (F12), Ireland EUR 3,000, NL EUR 449 (deterministic anyway), Lithuania EUR 200/prize; and it is honest: base yield is cents, the on-ramp is the point (C6).

**Sponsor drops (F21):** sponsors buy "Sector Sponsor" of an Earnings Season and fund a fixed per-head drop in a third-party ETF (never their own stock: Reg M/10b-18/Section 5, Landa 2023); funds are escrowed before the drop is announced (Bumped died when brands stopped paying); sponsors receive aggregate non-personal stats only; co-branded copy with a broker goes through FINRA 2210 principal review.

**Broker bounty use:** 50% into B (raising the Founding Bonus toward $10), 50% to margin/UA. Structure as standard affiliate CPA or flat marketing fee, never transaction-based (FINRA 2040).

**Worked example per 1,000 DAU (SOLO, token price zero, no Robinhood):** geo mix 60% US / 25% EU / 15% other; 3.0 rewarded views/DAU; net eCPM $12 US, $7 EU, $3 other (F20; economics #2-3) → blended $9.40 → $0.0282/DAU; interstitials 1.5/DAU at $5 → $0.0075; ad ARPDAU $0.036 = $1,080/month. IAP ARPDAU $0.03 gross (3% payers), net of store fees ~$0.023 → $690/month. Revenue $1,770/month. B = 20% × 1,080 + 10% × 690 = $285. Liabilities: base accrual 1,000 × 3.0 × 13 avg Ledger = $3.90/day + reports (60% completion × 12 avg) $0.72/day → $139/month; Founding Bonus 40 new verified accounts × $3 = $120; total $259 ≤ $285 (14.6% of revenue). Verification cost 40 × $3 = $120 (6.8%) from opex. Rewards plus verification = 21% of revenue; server/backend ~$60/month (Cloudflare DO/Supabase tiers, tech #26); remaining ~75% covers dev, support and UA. This is inside ZBD's 10-20% ad-revenue guidance and Mode's audited ≤~33% bound (F19).

**Same 1,000 DAU with PARTNER bounties:** 40 accounts × $30 = $1,200/month; 50% → B = $885; Founding Bonus $10 (40 × $10 = $400) + base $139 = $539 ≤ $885; total revenue $2,970; rewards 18%, verification borne by Robinhood. Bounties are upside, never a dependency.

**Zero-token-price test:** LONG appears nowhere in B; buybacks are a separate 20%-of-net-revenue commitment that starts only in Phase C and simply buys less when price is low.

## 9. Launch sequencing (Q7)

**Phase 0 (M0-M3): game only.** Soft launch in 2-3 test geos with no Ledger, no token. Gate: D1 ≥ 40%, D7 ≥ 15%, ARPDAU ≥ $0.04, rewarded opt-in ≥ 45%, server replay catching ≥ 99% of injected cheats. Counsel: none yet beyond standard game T&C, COPPA/GDPR age tiers.

## 10. Why this beats the obvious alternatives, and what to cut first

- **Versus an engagement-minted token (Hamster/Notcoin):** every such token collapsed 95-99% and shed ~1M users/day post-TGE (F18). Long Run mints only at genesis and thereafter matches player supply to buybacks; the token cannot be farmed for stock.
- **Versus a token redeemable for stock:** that is a custodial receipt or security-based swap unavailable to retail (F10) and makes the operator a money transmitter (F11). Separating Ledger (closed-loop) from LONG (consumptive) removes both.
- **Versus randomized stock drops / mystery boxes:** gameplay time is consideration in strict states (F9), NL taxes chance prizes at 37.8%, Belgium criminalizes them, Google forbids them in games; Massachusetts fined Robinhood for exactly this (F7). Deterministic fixed-ratio rewards cost nothing in fun because the fun lives in the idle loop, not in the reward.
- **Versus stock in the core loop (earn stock by reaching IPO):** Google's rule against rewards awarded or exponentiated by game performance, plus the design truth that extrinsic rewards attract extractors who leave on payout (Notcoin founders, p2e #15). Rewards here hang off monetizing events and tenure only.
- **Versus a Robinhood-only design:** no public equities API, no OAuth, no partner delivery path (F4). Every rail has a SOLO version; Robinhood adds bounties, in-account delivery and EU Stock Tokens, but its absence never breaks the product.

**Cut order if forced:** (1) Phase C transferability (keep LONG soulbound forever: removes audit, MM, buyback and Hamster risk with zero loss to the idle game); (2) rest-of-world Stock Token delivery; (3) sponsor drops; (4) the EU SOLO stablecoin fallback (EU becomes PARTNER-or-Gems); (5) Board Seat governance. Never cut: server authority, the Dividend Rule caps, LiveOps events, or the 14-day tenure gate. If the whole real-value layer were removed, Long Run would still be a competitive idle game with a compounding fantasy, which is the point.

# DRAFT 2: Compounders (token: DRIP) — "Built on Robinhood Chain"

A cozy idle game about patience and compounding, built on Robinhood Chain, where verified adult players earn a non-tradeable membership token (DRIP) held in Robinhood Wallet and small, deterministic, revenue-funded fractional-share (US) or Stock Token (EU/EEA) rewards delivered only by a licensed party.

# Compounders — architecture proposal (2026-09-01)

Framing: written as Robinhood's growth PM would build it, then split into **PARTNER** (needs Robinhood to act or build) and **SOLO** (a third-party studio alone). Every Robinhood capability cited is one the research confirms; where none exists (no OAuth, no equities API, no third-party Stock Token delivery path: F4; robinhood #21), the PARTNER item says "Robinhood would have to build this". Copy rule from day one: Robinhood's on-chain product is called "Stock Tokens", the network "Robinhood Chain", the mark usage "Built on Robinhood Chain", and the studio's brand is primary (Robinhood Chain ToS, robinhood missed-facts).

Mandatory positioning line (store listing, first run, and FTC earnings-claim substantiation): "Compounders is a game about patience. Member rewards are cents to a few dollars a year, paid in real fractional shares or Stock Tokens by a licensed partner. The point is a first, free, boring, diversified holding — not trading."

## 1. Concept and theme

**Name**: Compounders. **Token**: DRIP (Dividend Re-Investment Points) — the name mirrors what Robinhood's Stock Tokens actually do: dividends auto-reinvest via the ERC-8056 multiplier (F1; robinhood #5).

**Player fantasy**: you run a tiny founder's company in a hand-drawn city split into 12 sector districts (Staples, Discretionary, Tech, Health, Financials, Industrials, Energy, Materials, Utilities, Real Estate, Comms, plus an "Index" meta-district). Businesses throw off Cashflow; the only verb is **Reinvest**. Upgrades are **Moats**, automation is **Board Members**, prestige is an **IPO** (you take the company public, reset, keep permanent **Float**), and the second layer is **Index Inclusion**. The lesson the loop teaches, mechanically, is time × rate: the longer you leave things alone, the more they compound. That is deliberately the opposite of what the SEC's DEP concern (F7; legal-us #9) and ESMA's gamification statements (legal-eu #18) worry about: trading frequency.

**How real stocks appear without encouraging trading**:
- No real tickers, prices, P&L, charts, buy/sell verbs, market simulation, streaks, confetti, or money leaderboards anywhere in the loop (all named in the Massachusetts settlement, robinhood #28; SEC 2021 DEP list, legal-us #9). Leagues rank Float (a prestige number), prizes are cosmetic.
- Real securities exist in exactly one place: the **Vault**, a separate screen outside the loop, showing holdings delivered by the licensed partner. The reward menu is pre-published and diversified: default is an S&P 500 ETF (VOO/SPY), with an optional pick from the same 26 large caps ("2 largest S&P 500 companies in the top 13 sectors") Robinhood uses for its own Tiered Referrals (F6; robinhood #23). Robinhood's own EU giveaway used NVDA or VOO (legal-eu #10). Vault copy carries "no security is recommended; this is a promotional loyalty reward" and never links to a trade ticket.
- Sector districts are flavor ("this district makes the things you use every day"), not stock picks. IPO is a story beat about patience ("your company is worth more because you waited"), not an event to trade.
- Rewards are auto-held (US: partner broker's 3-trading-day sell lock and 30-day withdrawal lock, mirroring Robinhood's promo terms, robinhood #23; EU PARTNER: Robinhood's 180-day hold, F5).

## 2. Core loop and progression

**Currencies**: Cashflow (soft, per run, resets at IPO); Float (prestige 1, permanent); Index Points (prestige 2, permanent); DRIP (on-chain membership token, no cash value, never affects the economy of the loop); Stock Credit (USD/EUR cents in a separate off-chain ledger, only from money events, section 7). Rule: **time and performance earn DRIP; money events earn Stock Credit; nothing random touches either** (C1).

**Generators** (12 sector businesses per world): cost_k(n) = 4 × 11^(k−1) × r_k^n, output_k = 6^(k−1)/s per unit, r_k = 1.07 + 0.005(k−1) (1.07 → 1.125; canonical band, idle #1, #2). First-unit payback of tier k is 4 × (11/6)^(k−1) s (tier 6 ≈ 83 s, tier 9 ≈ 8.5 min, tier 12 ≈ 52 min), so walls arrive naturally ("a natural energy system", idle #7). Milestones: ×2 output at 25/50/100/200/300/400 owned, ×2 speed at 25/50/100 (AdCap, idle #1). Bulk-buy ×1/×10/×max with the closed-form max-affordable formula (idle #1).

**Automation**: Board Member per generator (auto-collect, buyable at 10× the generator's base cost); a CFO (auto-buys cheapest Moat) unlocks after the first IPO; a "Reinvest All" button from minute one. Tapping matters only in the first five minutes.

**Prestige**: IPO grants Float = floor(150 × sqrt(L/1e15)) − Float already claimed, L = lifetime Cashflow this world; +2% production per Float (square root, ~4× earnings to double, fast early resets: idle #4). First IPO is recommended at L ≈ 1e13 → 15 Float (+30%), reachable in 8–12 elapsed hours. Worlds: Main Street; **Wall Street** unlocks at L = 1e14 (AdCap's Moon pacing, idle #20; day 5–7); **Global** at 1e22, each with its own Float constant (per-world tuning, idle skeptic #4). Layer 2, **Index Inclusion**: IP = floor(cbrt(total Float earned across worlds / 1,000)), +1% all-worlds output and +1 h offline cap per IP (cube root, slow, idle #3). Numbers use break_infinity.js / BreakInfinity.cs (idle #29).

**Offline**: 8 h cap at full rate (inside the 2–24 h envelope, idle #13), +1 h per Index Point up to 12 h, 24 h with Compounders Plus. "Double offline earnings" rewarded ad on return is the top placement (idle #14, #28). Server-authoritative: monotonic elapsedRealtime on device, server checkpoint (state + server timestamp), deterministic replay of the input log before any DRIP or Stock Credit is written (idle #31, #32; tech #28).

**Session shape**: 3–4 sessions/day × 5–8 min ("log in, spend, log out": idle #7). Daily **Compounding Cycle** (collect offline → reinvest all → one Moat) is the DRIP-bearing ritual; there is no streak and nothing is lost by skipping a day.

**Ads** (all opt-in except interstitials): Bull Run ×2 for 4 h; Double Offline; Fast Forward 4 h; Bulk Discount −10% on next 10 buys. 8 rewarded/day hard cap, first 6 count for Stock Credit, 2-minute cooldown, S2S verified (tech #32). No interstitials in session one; then first after 90 s, ≤2 per session, ≥3 min apart (idle #24). Targets: opt-in ≥ 50%, 3 rewarded/DAU.

**IAP (no loot boxes anywhere, so no odds disclosure and PEGI stays 12: legal-eu missed-facts)**: Founder's Pack $4.99 permanent ×3 offered at the Wall Street unlock (third-content-unlock conversion moment, idle #27; "extremely compelling", idle #7); Compounders Plus $3.99/30 days (ad-free, 24 h offline, second CFO; Cats & Soup pricing, idle #19); Season Pass $9.99/90 days (cosmetics, event generators, Float boosts, DRIP boosts — never Stock Credit); Float packs (instant prestige). - Why legal: third-party listed stock bought on the open market through a broker-dealer avoids Section 5 (F8; legal-us #1); deterministic fixed-ratio value removes chance (F9); the broker's retail communications go through FINRA 2210 principal approval (legal-us #8).

**EU/EEA — on-chain Stock Tokens** (Jersey tokenised debt securities, passported prospectus; F1; robinhood #3, legal-eu missed-facts). Classic Stock Tokens are excluded: they are MiFID derivatives, non-transferable, and giving them away risks the CFD inducement ban (F14; legal-eu #14, #15, missed-facts).
- PARTNER: Robinhood Europe UAB (MiFID + CASP licences, Bank of Lithuania, legal-eu #8) runs a "Compounders Stock Token Grant" under its own June-2026 giveaway framework (F5; legal-eu #10): onboarded, appropriateness-tested, 18+ clients (legal-eu skeptic #37); tokens (default SPY/VOO Stock Token) delivered into the client's Robinhood Wallet or account with the 180-day hold; the studio pays Robinhood Europe a flat marketing fee, not per token. Robinhood is the regulated distributor; the game is a marketing partner whose copy Robinhood approves (MiFID Art 24; legal-eu #16).
- SOLO: Stock Credit is paid as EURC (Robinhood's own dividend match paid in EURC, robinhood missed-facts) by a MiCA-licensed EMI/CASP payout partner (ZBD holds NL EMI + MiCAR licences and does light KYC at first cash-out, precedents #18; p2e #31) into the player's Robinhood Wallet on Robinhood Chain. The player, if they choose, swaps EURC for a Stock Token on Uniswap inside Robinhood Wallet (Stock Tokens are plain ERC-20s tradable there, robinhood #4). The game never touches Stock Tokens and its copy never presents the securities' terms, because the RHJ prospectus consents only to offers by Authorised Offerors and an "offer to the public" is any communication with sufficient information to decide (robinhood missed-facts; legal-eu missed-facts). Whether even this steering is a MiFID/Prospectus act is the open question F14 flags; it is a Phase 3 counsel gate.

**Excluded countries** (UK, Canada, Switzerland, UAE, RHJ prohibited list; F1, F16; robinhood #7, #32): game and DRIP only; Stock Credit never accrues, and the Vault says so at install. Rest of world where Robinhood offers Stock Tokens: no accrual until a licensed rail is live per country (Phase 4, PARTNER via Robinhood's distributing entity).

## 5. Token-to-stock relationship (Q3)

DRIP is never redeemable for stock or cash, at any rate, fixed or floating (C2; a fixed-rate redeemable token is a custodial receipt or security-based swap, legal-us #23). DRIP does not scale accrual: Stock Credit rates are fixed ratios to money events regardless of Float, DRIP balance, or game performance (Google Play's fixed-ratio, not-exponentiated-by-performance rule, legal-us #32). The only relationships are eligibility and friction: Tier I gates sponsor drops (each drop grants the same fixed amount to every eligible completer), Tier II shortens pending, and each withdrawal burns 50 DRIP. Membership credential in, no price out.

## 6. Identity, sybil and fraud (Q4)

**Primitive**: a verified-human attestation from the licensed counterparty that will hold the asset — one identity hash per human, no PII to the game (F17; brokerage KYC cannot be repurposed, legal-eu #39). The attestation is `{verified, age18, jurisdiction, identity_hash = H(partner_customer_id ‖ salt)}`. PARTNER: Robinhood issues it (there is no OAuth today, F4; Robinhood would have to build it). SOLO: the white-label broker (US) or EMI/CASP (EU) issues it from its own KYC ($1.35–1.89 list, ~$4 all-in, F22; economics #22). World ID (cloud verify, nullifier per action, ~18M Orb-verified, tech #34) is an optional accelerator that raises DRIP caps, never a substitute for the asset-side attestation. GDPR: linkage record off-chain, DPIA before deploy (legal-eu #38).

**Tiers**: T0 play (email/Apple/Google sign-in). T1 on-chain DRIP claim: Play Integrity MEETS_DEVICE_INTEGRITY / App Attest at claim time only (10k/day default quota, tech #29, #30), device intelligence (Fingerprint/Sardine: emulator, VM, fleet patterns, tech #31), WalletConnect signature for Robinhood Wallet linking. T2 Stock Credit withdrawal (min $5/€5, 14-day pending): the partner attestation above. One game account per identity_hash; a second account's pending credit is forfeited and its DRIP frozen. Stock Credit accrues in "pending" before T2 but is capped at $5 pending so unverified accounts create no liability.

**Ad SSV**: credit only on the network's server-to-server callback (AdMob ECDSA, Unity HMAC, MAX, LevelPlay; tech #32), idempotent on transaction ID, 6/day, 2-min cooldown, plus a progression-sequence check (Playrix cut fraudulent rewarded credits 12.5% that way, p2e #36).

**Behavioural signals**: synchronized logins, same-pixel taps, identical action timing across accounts, VPN masking (also banned by Robinhood Chain ToS), many accounts to one bound wallet — the Hamster/Pixels playbook (precedents #31; p2e #18). Expect ~22% of non-organic installs and ~40% of affiliate traffic to be fraudulent and half of Android finance installs to be fake pre-KYC (F22; p2e #35; economics #21); the KYC gate moves the cost to the attacker.

**Cheaters**: published policy, forfeiture of pending credit and DRIP, permanent loss of the real-value rail, a human-reviewed appeal within 14 days (opaque bans are the top trust complaint, p2e #33). Delivered assets are not clawed back except for fraud proven inside the broker's 30-day hold (Stash reserves the same right, legal-us #2). Cost handling: KYC is paid once, only for players who reach the $5 threshold; in the broker-funnel path the broker already KYCs at its own cost.

## 7. Funding stack and payout rule (Q5)

**Revenue lines**: rewarded ads (~60% of idle revenue, idle #17), interstitials, IAP and subscriptions, sponsor platform fees (20% of drop escrow), and broker acquisition bounties (public affiliate CPAs $20–70 per funded account; Robinhood $20–50 per qualifying action; F20; economics #13).

**Published payout rule (the "Compounders Reserve Rule")**: the reward budget for a period is 20% of net rewarded-ad revenue (ZBD guidance 10–20%, F19; p2e #31), 8% of gross IAP/subscription revenue, a fixed $5 Welcome Grant per funded broker account (≈14% of a $35 CPA), and 100% pass-through of sponsor escrow. Per-event rates are fixed and published per season and country: $0.003 per verified rewarded ad (US; €0.0015 EU), $0.40 per Plus month, 8% of each IAP, $5 per funded broker account. If trailing-30-day accruals exceed 80% of trailing-30-day budget, next season's rates are lowered with 30 days' notice; accrued credit is always honored (never retroactive, C1). Caps per identity: $0.50/day, $40/season, $150/year US; €0.30/day, €25/season, €100/year EU (under the US $2,000 1099-MISC threshold, F12; under Ireland's €3,000 gift exemption, legal-eu #43; deterministic, so outside NL gaming tax, legal-eu #41).

**Broker bounty use**: SOLO uses the public affiliate programs (Robinhood via its affiliate network, Webull as fallback), which pay per qualifying account opening, not per trade or asset — the structure counsel must bless under FINRA 2040 (legal-us #6). The game is barred from Robinhood's retail referral program as a "Robinhood-paid creator" (robinhood #23). PARTNER: a flat monthly marketing fee (cleanest under 2040) plus Robinhood co-funding the Welcome Grant as its own "separate sign-up stock reward" (F6). The bounty funds the Welcome Grant, the broker onboarding, and margin.

**Sponsor drops** (F21): a sponsor escrows cash with the broker partner; every Tier-I verified player who completes the sponsored Sector Week during the window receives the same fixed amount (e.g., $1 of VOO) until escrow is exhausted, with the quota published up front (no chance, no race-based variation in amount). Sponsors see aggregates only. Reg M caveat: sponsors may fund third-party stock or ETFs only; a public company wanting its own shares granted is refused by default and, if ever allowed, requires broker-executed 10b-18 open-market purchases paused during restricted periods and counsel sign-off (Landa was terminated, economics #30).

**Worked example — 1,000 DAU, US, per day.** Assumptions: 3 verified rewarded views/DAU at $15 eCPM (F20; economics #1, #2); interstitial/banner $0.010 ARPDAU; IAP + subs $0.025 ARPDAU (2–5% payers, hybrid split, idle #26); 1,000 DAU ≈ 80 installs/day (DAU/installs ≈ 12 at D1 40/D7 18/D30 7); 1% of installs → funded broker account at $35 CPA.

## 10. Why this beats the obvious alternatives, and what gets cut first

- Token redeemable for stock at a fixed rate: a custodial receipt or security-based swap, unsellable to retail (legal-us #23). Rejected outright.
- Pay stock directly for play time or performance: violates Google's fixed-ratio rule and, with any chance leak, becomes prize + chance + "considerable time" consideration in strict states (F9); also unfunded by revenue, the SLP/HMSTR failure signature (p2e #1, #12).
- Tradeable token at launch with revenue buybacks: every comparable fell 95–99% (F18); it makes the studio an administrator/exchanger and CASP, drags in UK promotions rules, and buybacks are "closer to a symbolic gesture" (p2e #29). Rejected; revenue goes to real stock instead.
- Game vault hands out Stock Tokens in the EU: unlicensed distribution against a prospectus that consents only to Authorised Offerors (robinhood missed-facts; F14). Rejected in favor of Robinhood-as-distributor or player self-swap.
- Robinhood-only, no SOLO: no partner API exists (F4), so the product would be hostage to a deal. Every PARTNER item here has a SOLO twin.
- No token at all: cleaner, but loses the ask's Robinhood Wallet/Chain anchor; it is the first thing to cut, and the design is built so cutting it costs nothing.

**Cut order if forced**: (1) open-transfer Phase C (already conditional); (2) sponsor drops; (3) the EU SOLO EURC rail (EU becomes PARTNER-only or game-only); (4) on-chain DRIP (keep Drip Points); (5) ad-funded Stock Credit (keep only the broker-funded Welcome Grant). What remains at the bottom is a good idle game with a broker affiliate funnel that still pays real fractional shares through a licensed party — which is the only version of this product that has ever survived (precedents #1–#8).

# DRAFT 3: Bellwether

Bellwether is an eleven-sector idle tycoon whose non-transferable BELL credential on Robinhood Chain proves you played, and whose revenue-funded, fixed-ratio Stock Credit walks verified adults onto a brokerage account (US, fractional shares) or into Robinhood Wallet Stock Tokens (EEA) at cents-to-dollars scale, with every Robinhood dependency labelled and a unilateral fallback for each.

# Bellwether: architecture proposal (regulatory-perimeter-first)

Design stance: every real-value flow is deterministic, revenue-funded, capped, and delivered by a licensed party; the token is a credential, not a currency; the game must be worth playing with the reward tab hidden. PARTNER = needs Robinhood cooperation; SOLO = buildable alone. Facts cited as F-numbers (brief) or file#finding.

## 1. Concept and theme

**Name:** Bellwether. **Token:** BELL (a non-transferable "Charter" credential). **Loyalty ledger:** Stock Credit (USD/EUR-denominated, off-chain). **Soft currencies:** Capital (run cash), Float (IPO prestige), Weight (index prestige).

**Player fantasy:** grow a one-kiosk startup into a bellwether conglomerate across the eleven GICS sectors, take it public, get included in the index, and start again bigger. Compounding is literal: exponential generator costs versus linear output (F23), prestige converts exponential growth back into a ladder. Sectors are the generator tiers (Consumer Staples kiosk through Real Estate REIT). IPO is the first prestige (you convert your private company into Float, i.e. permanent shareholders). Index Inclusion is the second prestige (Weight unlocks new markets). Dividends, splits, diversification and fees are taught through an in-game "Prospectus" reading room, written plainly and counsel-reviewed, and the Stock Token ERC-8056 multiplier (F1) is explained as "your one token quietly becomes 1.0006 tokens after a dividend".

**What the theme deliberately does not do (ESMA/SEC/Massachusetts lens, F7; legal-us#9; legal-eu#18-19):** no live prices, charts, P&L, or "buy now"; no leaderboards ranked by real value; no confetti or celebration tied to any financial action; no streak penalties; no chance in anything with cash value (C1); no naming of specific securities in reward copy (player self-directs; broad ETF default). Copy uses Robinhood's mandated terms only: "Stock Tokens", "Built on Robinhood Chain", own brand primary (robinhood.md skeptic missed facts: Chain ToS 2026-08-24 and brand guidelines). Store listings never mention earnings (Google Blockchain policy, legal-eu missed facts; FTC earnings-claim posture, legal-us#29). Honest positioning in-app: "Stock Credit is cents to a few dollars a year. The point is that it lands in a real account you own."

## 2. Core loop and progression

**Generators (11 sectors).** cost_next = base × r^owned; production = base_rev × owned × multipliers (idle-design#1). Constants follow the AdCap ladder: cost step ×12 per tier, revenue step ×6-10, cycle step ×2-4; r = 1.07 (tier 1), 1.15 (tier 2), then declining 1.14 → 1.07 through tier 11 (idle-design#1-2). Tier 1 "Corner Kiosk": cost 4, revenue 1, 1 s cycle. Tier 11 "Skyline REIT": ~3e11 base cost, ~10-hour cycle. Milestones: ×2 profit at 25/50/100/200/300/400 owned, then every 100 to 1,000; speed doublers at 25/50/100 for tiers 1-4 (F23). "Make it bumpy" (idle-design#7).

**Automation and upgrades.** One Manager per sector (cost ≈ 250× tier base cost) converts tap-to-collect into auto-cycle. "Analyst reports" are ×3 sector multipliers priced at ~50× the tier's cost at purchase time; "Bellwether Reports" are global ×2 at Capital thresholds 1e6, 1e12, 1e18…

**Prestige 1: IPO.** Float = 150 × sqrt(lifetime_capital / 1e15) − Float already claimed; each Float unit = +2% all profit (idle-design#4; ~4× earnings to double). First IPO is reachable at Float ≈ 50 (×2) after roughly 3-4 wall-clock hours including offline time. Float is spent on "Underwriters" (permanent perks: S-1 filing ×3 all profit at 1e5 Float; instant Manager unlock; offline capacity).

**Prestige 2: Index Inclusion.** Unlocks at lifetime Float ≥ 1e6 (engaged players ~day 10-14). Weight = floor(cbrt(lifetime_Float / 1e4)) (cube-root: ~8× to double, long runs, idle-design#3). Each Weight = +10% Float effectiveness and unlocks Markets 2 and 3 (Europe, Asia) with their own generator sets and per-market Float constants (AdCap Earth/Moon/Mars pattern, idle-design skeptic missed facts). Per-sector ×7.77 "Golden Analyst" bought with Weight; full set grants ×10 more.

**Number system.** break_infinity.js / BreakInfinity.cs from day one; content authored to ~1e100 in year one; a third layer ("Conglomerate") is reserved for the 1e308 wall (idle-design#10, #29).

**Offline rules.** 8 h at 100% at launch (inside the 2-24 h envelope, F23), upgradeable with Float to 16 h and 24 h (Egg Inc silo pattern, idle-design skeptic missed facts). Offline yield is computed server-side from the server clock; the client's SystemClock.elapsedRealtime is used only for UI (idle-design#31). Return moment offers a rewarded ×2 offline ad. Rewarded 4 h time-skips, max 3/day.

**Session shape.** 3-5 sessions/day of 4-8 min: ring the Bell (daily claim), collect offline, spend all Capital, buy managers/reports, queue an event action, leave (idle-design#7, #17).

**First five minutes.** 0:00 tap Kiosk; 0:20 second Kiosk; 0:45 Microgrid unlock; 1:30 first Manager (hands go free); 2:00 ×2 at 25 Kiosks; 3:00 Machine Shop; 3:30 first Analyst report; 4:00 first rewarded offer (×2 for 4 h, opt-in); 5:00 Bell tutorial ("ring it tomorrow; your Charter grows with play", no value language). No interstitial in sessions 1-3; first interstitial only after 60-90 s of a later session (idle-design#24).

**Day 1 beats.** Four sectors, first offline return with ×2 offer, first IPO ceremony (modest, no confetti), Sector Sprint visible, starter pack offered at the third sector unlock (the conversion moment, idle-design#27): permanent ×3 for $2.99 (idle-design#7).

**Day 7 beats.** 8-11 sectors, 3-5 IPOs, first cooperative Index Sprint, battle pass offer, and (US/EEA only) the Vault tab reveal: "Stock Credit pending: $0.07. Verify to open your Vault and receive the $5.00 Listing Bonus." The trickle is honest; the on-ramp is the event.

**Targets.** D1 45% floor / 55% target, D7 20%, D30 8% (mobile medians 22/4/1, idle titles far higher, F23; Kolibri prototype D1 63-81%, idle-design#17). 3.5 rewarded views/DAU at 55% opt-in; interstitials ≤ 2/session, 2-3 min spacing, never > 3 (20-30% D7 penalty, idle-design#24).

**LiveOps.** Weekly Sector Sprint (event-mine pattern; doubled weekend revenue for Kolibri, idle-design#17), monthly Season with a $4.99 pass (ad-free days, cosmetics, Float boosters, never Stock Credit), quarterly "Earnings Season" cooperative goals. Backend from day one (PlayFab/Supabase class, tech#26) so events and segments exist at launch (AdCap's post-launch regret, idle-design#20).

**Monetization.** Permanent ×3 starter pack, ad-removal subscription $3.99/30 d (idle-design#19), time warps, battle pass, cosmetics. Two ad surfaces only: rewarded (primary) and light interstitials. Nothing essential is ad-gated (idle-design#33).

**Anti-cheat.** Server-authoritative checkpoints (state + server timestamp + seed) with deterministic replay of the client input log from the last checkpoint; affordability and rate-plausibility checks; leaderboards and any reward-bearing event are written only after replay validation (idle-design#32, tech#28). Play Integrity / App Attest requested only at Bell claim and redemption to stay inside the 10k/day default quota (tech#29-30).

## 3. Token model (Q1)

**What BELL legally is.** A non-transferable, non-convertible credential earned only by verified play and consumed inside the Bellwether ecosystem. US: a "digital tool" (membership/ticket/credential; no yield, no income rights) under the SEC-CFTC interpretation (F10; legal-us#19). We do not rely on the airdrop interpretation because BELL is issued in exchange for gameplay (legal-us#20). It is closed-loop and not convertible to fiat or crypto, so the studio is not an FinCEN administrator/MSB and BELL falls inside the NY BitLicense gaming-unit exclusion (F11; legal-us#27-28). EU: because BELL cannot be transferred, it is arguably not a MiCA crypto-asset at all; if a regulator disagrees, it is a utility token for a service that already operates (Art 4(3)(c)), never admitted to trading (Art 4(4) preserved), with no CASP needed for our own custody/transfer of it (Art 4(5)) and no voluntary white paper (Art 4(8)) (F13; legal-eu#2, missed facts). UK: outside "qualifying cryptoasset" because it cannot be transferred or sold except by redemption with the issuer and is limited-use (legal-eu#33). BELL is never marketed with the words earn, invest, value or price.

**Transferability decision (Q1): non-transferable at launch and for at least 12 months after going on-chain; "tradeable" only if three gates are met later (US and Lithuanian counsel opinions; a demand source that is not new players; twelve months of published emission data), and we tell players now that no such change is planned and no TGE will ever be announced as a reward.** Rationale: a transferable game token has no exogenous demand and every comparable collapsed 95-99.9% (F18; p2e#11-16, precedents#30-33); transferability would also void the MiCA exemption, the UK exclusion and the FinCEN/BitLicense exclusions in one stroke.

**Supply and emission.** Hard cap 1,000,000,000 BELL, immutable ERC-20 (OpenZeppelin 5.7.x ERC20Capped + MINTER_ROLE, tech#35, skeptic note on 5.7.0). 90% player emissions, 10% LiveOps reserve minted only through events, 0% team/investors/sale. Season 1 global cap 2% of supply, declining 10% per season. Per account: daily Bell claim 10 BELL (one per day, integrity-attested), milestone bounties (first IPO 50, each Index Inclusion 100, Sprint participation 20/week), hard cap 60/day and 1,500/season. Rules are published before Phase 2 and never changed retroactively (C1; Hamster Kombat's fatal error, precedents#31). Balances accrue off-chain and are minted on-chain via a Merkle claim distributor only for verified Seats, so bots never cost us gas.

**Utility list (all sinks burn BELL):** (1) Settlement ticket: 25 BELL burned per Stock Credit redemption; (2) LiveOps governance: 10 BELL per vote on next week's Sprint sector; (3) Charter seat: 100 BELL/season to join a cooperative Syndicate for Index Sprints; (4) Listings: cosmetic nameplates/skins minted with BELL (also sold as IAP, satisfying Apple 3.1.3(b) parity); (5) tenure proof: sponsor drops require ≥ 7 Bell claims in the current season; (6) composability: `seasonsActive(address)` and burn history are public so other Robinhood Chain apps can read proof-of-play (the Lighter-points precedent shows third-party points programs inside Robinhood's ecosystem, robinhood.md#15). BELL spending happens in the web hub ("Exchange Floor") and is displayed read-only in the mobile builds, because Apple 3.1.1 bars in-app crypto mechanisms from unlocking content or functionality (precedents skeptic missed facts).

**Where it lives.** Chain 4663, deployed permissionlessly (F3), transfer override permits only mint-from-minter, burn-to-registered-sink, and one-time migration between a Seat's own verified addresses. Gas sponsored via ERC-4337 (EntryPoint v0.7 0x0000000071727De22E5E9d8BAf0edAc6f37da032; AA on Robinhood Chain is Alchemy-powered, tech missed facts) at ~$0.001 + 8% (tech#3, #23). If the sequencer's ArbOS 61 filter ever rejects our contract ("Transaction rejected by chain policy", tech#5, missed facts), the ledger is authoritative and we redeploy on Arbitrum One from the ledger, a rule published in advance. Audit: boutique ERC-20 tier, $5-20k, 8-14 weeks (tech#36).

**Custody/wallet UX.** At verification an embedded wallet is created (Privy/Dynamic; budget $0.05/MAU, since Privy's free tier is only 0-499 MAU per the skeptic correction to tech#22), keys exportable; players may link Robinhood Wallet via WalletConnect on chain 4663 (tech#21) and migrate BELL there, where it is visible like any ERC-20 (robinhood.md#19).

**What stops Hamster Kombat:** no market, so no price to crash; no airdrop or TGE promise, enforced in the contract; allocation rules immutable; all external value flows through Stock Credit, not BELL; per-account caps and behavioural sybil filters; a bot that mints BELL gains nothing it can sell.

## 4. Stock reward path by jurisdiction (Q2)

Stock Credit is a dollar/euro-denominated promotional credit with no cash value until redeemed, held pending until verification (Mistplay pattern, precedents#26), and delivered in the Cash App legal form: a restricted credit the recipient self-directs into a purchase inside a licensed account, forfeited if unused (precedents skeptic-2 missed facts). The game never holds or transfers securities (C3).

**United States (fractional shares).**
- PARTNER: Robinhood Financial delivers the fixed $5 Listing Bonus as its own sign-up stock reward (fixed value, no odds; its current referee reward exists but value/odds are unstated, robinhood.md#23) and accepts Stock Credit as tagged promotional deposits into the player's brokerage account; Robinhood supplies a signed attestation (verified, 18+, US, unique account hash) per hashed player. Compensation to the game is a flat marketing fee, not per-trade or per-asset (FINRA 2040/Exchange Act 15(a), legal-us#6); all co-branded copy is principal-approved under FINRA 2210 (legal-us#8). No public API or OAuth exists (F4), hence PARTNER.
- SOLO: a white-label "Vault Account" opened in our flow with a FINRA broker-dealer partner; first choice Alpaca Securities LLC, whose Broker API documents JNLS security journals precisely for rewarding users with small share quantities and JNLC cash journals (economics#24, missed facts), and which is also the broker/custodian in Robinhood's own RHJ Stock Token stack (robinhood.md skeptic missed facts). The BD performs CIP (name, DOB, address, TIN; effectively 18+, F12; legal-us#33). Stock Credit lands as a cash journal the player self-directs, with a broad-market ETF default. The Listing Bonus is funded by the Robinhood affiliate bounty ($20-50 per qualifying action via Impact, economics#13) when the player also opens and funds Robinhood through our link, if the affiliate terms permit incentivized referrals; if not, it is funded from IAP margin at $3. Legal basis: third-party listed stock bought on the open market through a BD avoids Section 5 (F8; legal-us#1-2); deterministic value removes chance (F9); Google Play fixed-ratio loyalty rule satisfied (legal-us#32).
- Player sees: "Vault opened. $5.00 Listing Bonus + $0.07 credit arrived as cash. Pick any stock or ETF, or keep the default." Under PARTNER the same screen says "in your Robinhood account".

**EU/EEA (on-chain Stock Tokens).** Classic Stock Tokens are MiFID OTC derivatives; handing them out is a prohibited non-monetary benefit under the CFD product-intervention measures and a circumvention risk (F14; legal-eu#9, #14-15). They are excluded (C8).
- PARTNER: Robinhood Europe UAB runs a "Bellwether Stock Token promotion" under its own giveaway template: onboarded, appropriateness-tested clients, 180-day hold, tokens delivered by RHEU (F5; legal-eu#10). We ask that the instrument be the on-chain Jersey Stock Token, that the eligible set be broad-market ETFs or player choice (no single-name nudge), and that RHEU return a signed eligibility attestation. The game is a marketing partner under MiFID Art 24 with RHEU approving creative (legal-eu#16). Flat fee. This is the only path that puts Stock Tokens into a Robinhood account.
- SOLO: the game never touches Stock Tokens (they are prospectus-passported debt securities whose public offer is reserved to Authorised Offerors; a third-party retail distribution is the open question in F14 and legal-eu missed facts). Instead, EUR Stock Credit is disbursed as USDG (the chain's core stablecoin, robinhood.md#12) to the player's self-custody address on chain 4663, proven by a WalletConnect signature from Robinhood Wallet or any EVM wallet (tech#21). The player then, self-directed, swaps in Robinhood Wallet on Uniswap/RFQ (robinhood.md#3-4). Robinhood Europe itself pays a Dividend Match in EURC to customers (robinhood.md skeptic missed facts), so stablecoin payouts to EU retail have a Robinhood precedent. Disbursement is executed by a MiCA-authorised EMI/CASP partner so a non-EU studio never provides a crypto-asset service (ESMA 2026-04-17, legal-eu#6). Our copy never names a Stock Token and never says "buy"; the swap UI is Robinhood's. Gate: Lithuanian counsel must confirm this is neither reception/transmission nor a marketing communication about a financial instrument; if refused, EU SOLO falls back to EUR via SEPA/PSP and the player self-funds Robinhood Europe.
- Player sees: "€5.00 sent as USDG to your Robinhood Wallet. Hold it, or swap it inside your wallet. Stock Tokens are tokenised debt securities issued by Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Ltd; they are not shares." Eligibility: 18+ EEA resident (RHEU Customer Agreement 4.2-4.3, legal-eu missed facts), sanctions screen against the RHJ list (robinhood.md#7).

**Excluded countries (UK, CA, CH, UAE, sanctioned).** Full game and BELL (non-transferable, no value), no Stock Credit accrual at all (F16; legal-eu#33-35; robinhood.md#7). Rest of world where Robinhood offers Stock Tokens: same SOLO path as EEA, enabled country by country in Phase 4 after local promo-law review.

## 5. Token-to-stock relationship (Q3)

BELL is never redeemable for, priced against, or a multiplier on Stock Credit; a token redeemable for stock at a fixed rate would be a custodial receipt or a retail-prohibited security-based swap (F10; legal-us#23) and would breach C2. The only links are deterministic gates that prove humanity and tenure: the 25-BELL settlement ticket per redemption (a ticket, a digital tool), and the seven-Bell-claim tenure requirement for sponsor drops. The published Stock Credit ratios are identical for a player with 0 BELL and one with 10,000 BELL. BELL governs LiveOps (which sector gets the Sprint), never money. This keeps Google Play's "not exponentiated by game performance" rule satisfied (legal-us#32) and keeps BELL's value consumptive rather than financial.

## 6. Identity, sybil and fraud (Q4)

**Primitive: the Verified Seat.** One Seat per human; one game account per Seat. Created at first redemption, never earlier. Sources: US SOLO, the BD partner's CIP (one account per SSN/TIN); US PARTNER, Robinhood's attestation; EU PARTNER, RHEU's attestation; EU SOLO, our own KYC (Sumsub $1.35-1.85 or Veriff $0.80-1.89 with liveness and document/face de-duplication, tech#33; economics#22) bound to a wallet address by WalletConnect signature. We store only an attestation record (vendor reference, jurisdiction, 18+ flag, verified-at, address set) off-chain; no PII on-chain (EDPB v2.0, F17; legal-eu#38) and brokerage KYC is never repurposed (AMLR retention, legal-eu#39).

**Duplicate enforcement.** A second game account presenting the same identity is denied Seat linkage and its pending credit is forfeited (published rule). Because the Seat is the only route to value, extra accounts can only pollute ad metrics, which ad networks scrub anyway.

**Withdrawal gating.** Minimum redemption $5 / €5; weekly Settlement Days; 25 BELL burned; ad credits pending 7 days, IAP stock-back pending 60 days (past the store refund windows; adjoe refund-fraud pattern, p2e#36); withdrawal allowance grows with play (ZBD's decoupled earn-rate/withdrawal-limit pattern, precedents skeptic missed facts).

**Device and behavioural signals (supplementary, never sufficient, C5).** Play Integrity verdicts (PLAY_RECOGNIZED, LICENSED, MEETS_DEVICE_INTEGRITY) and App Attest at claim/redeem (tech#29-30); Fingerprint/Sardine-class emulator, VM, root, Frida, cloned-app, VPN and fleet-pattern signals, since fraud has shifted to physical device farms (+72% YoY, tech#31); behavioural fingerprints proven by Hamster Kombat's bans (same-pixel taps, synchronized logins, identical progression curves, precedents#31); VPN blocks rewards only, not play (Robinhood Chain ToS also bars VPN masking).

**Ad SSV.** Credit only on server-side callbacks (AdMob ECDSA, Unity HMAC, MAX, LevelPlay), idempotent on transaction_id/oid/EVENT_ID, ≤ 6 credited views/day, ≥ 2 min apart, client-claim mismatch flags the account (tech#32).

**Cheaters.** Automation, multi-accounting, emulator farms, refund abuse: pending Stock Credit forfeited, BELL burned, 12-month reward ban, game access retained. Human-reviewed appeal within 14 days and quarterly published enforcement stats, because opaque pre-payout bans are the top complaint in reward apps (p2e#33). Biometric KYC vendors must be BIPA-compliant (JustPlay $4.5M settlement, precedents skeptic missed facts).

**KYC cost handling.** PARTNER paths cost us $0. US SOLO: inside the BD per-account fee (assumption $3/account, unpublished, economics#24 open question), funded from the bounty/IAP margin. EU SOLO: ~$2 per check, incurred only at a ≥ €5 redemption, funded from the EU pool's unclaimed breakage and IAP margin. Worst-case exposure per fake identity that beats KYC is one $5 Listing Bonus plus the account fee, which is below the market cost of a clean synthetic identity.

## 7. Funding stack and payout rule (Q5)

**Revenue lines.** (1) Rewarded and interstitial ads; (2) IAP; (3) sponsor drop fees (sponsor funds 100% of the drop plus a 20% service fee); (4) on-ramp bounties (SOLO: Robinhood affiliate $20-50, comparable brokers $20-70, F20; economics#13; PARTNER: negotiated flat marketing fee, benchmarked against Robinhood's ~$150-222 per net funded add and $187 ARPU, economics#10, missed facts).

**The Bell Rule (published, in-app and on the web).** Monthly Reward Pool P = 0.20 × A + 0.04 × I + S + 0.25 × B, where A = net ad revenue, I = gross IAP, S = sponsor drop funds, B = bounties received (ZBD's 10-20% of ad revenue guidance, F19; p2e#31). Player accruals are fixed ratios published per geo tier for the period: per SSV-verified rewarded view US $0.003, EEA tier-1 $0.0014, EEA tier-2/3 $0.0008, recomputed monthly as 20% × trailing-30-day realized eCPM ÷ 1,000 (JustPlay's floating rate, precedents#27) with 30 days' notice and never retroactively; IAP stock-back 4% of gross spend (Stash/Acorns ~0.5-1% territory, F21); Listing Bonus $5 once (US; €2 EU SOLO, €5 EU PARTNER); sponsor drops fixed $0.25-0.50 per qualifying deterministic action. A segregated reserve ≥ 3 months of trailing payouts is held before any ratio increase (Sky Mavis stability-fund logic, p2e#4).

**Caps per account.** Daily $1.00 (ad credits ≤ $0.03), season (month) $25, year $200, which is under the $2,000 1099-MISC threshold (F12; economics#25), Ireland's €3,000 gift exemption and Lithuania's €200 prize exemption (legal-eu#43-44). Sponsor drops ≤ 4/month.

**Sponsor drops (F21, Reg M).** A sponsor (ETF issuer, consumer brand, public company) funds a fixed $ per verified player who completes a published deterministic action inside a window (e.g. "build 50 Microgrids during Grid Week"); the first N completers get it, N published in advance, so there is no chance. Credit is self-directed; the default is a broad ETF containing the sponsor. A sponsor may not require purchase of its own stock: issuer-funded own-stock giveaways hit Section 5, Reg M Rules 101/102 and Rule 10b-18 (Landa 2023, economics#30), so own-stock drops happen only if counsel clears 10b-18-compliant open-market purchases through the BD with blackout monitoring; Robinhood's Partner Stock Program (companies gifting their own common stock into Robinhood accounts with issuer 1099-NEC/W-2 reporting, precedents skeptic-2 missed facts) is a PARTNER option for such sponsors, counsel-gated. In the EU sponsor drops are EUR/USDG credits, never derivatives.

**Bounty use.** 25% to the pool (funds the Listing Bonus), 25% to verification and account fees, 50% margin and reserve.

**Worked example per 1,000 DAU (assumptions from F20-F22, economics#1-7).** Geo mix US 45% / EEA 30% / UK-CA-CH-UAE 10% / RoW 15%. Rewarded eCPM US $15, EEA $7, UK-group $10, RoW $3; 3.5 rewarded views/DAU (55% opt-in); 1.5 interstitials/DAU at ~55% of rewarded eCPM; IAP gross ARPDAU $0.03 (2.5% payers), blended store fee 25%.
- Rewarded: 3,500 views → US $23.6 + EEA $7.4 + UK $3.5 + RoW $1.6 = $36.0/day. Interstitials: $8.4/day. Ad ARPDAU $0.044 (inside the $0.036-0.05 band, F20).
- IAP: $30 gross, $22.5 net. Net revenue ≈ $67/day ≈ $2,000/month.
- Pool ex-bounties: 0.20 × $44.4 + 0.04 × $30 = $10.08/day ≈ $302/month.
- Accruals: US views 1,575 × $0.003 = $4.73/day; EEA 1,050 × $0.0014 = $1.47/day; total $186/month. IAP stock-back 4% × $900 = $36/month. ## 10. Why this beats the obvious alternatives, and what to cut

# DRAFT 4: Float

An 11-sector "patient compounder" idle city whose loyalty balance is delivered monthly as fixed-ratio stock rewards through licensed rails (USDG-to-Robinhood-Wallet self-directed into Stock Tokens in the EU/ROW, broker-journaled ETF fractions in the US), with a consumptive FLOAT utility token living on Robinhood Chain that is deliberately never redeemable for, or multiplied into, stock.

# Float — architecture proposal (Robinhood Chain-native lens)

Date of record 2026-09-01. Facts cited as F-numbers (brief) or `file.md #n` (research finding), `file.md S1/S2-missed` (skeptic missed facts). Anything requiring Robinhood cooperation is tagged **PARTNER** with a **SOLO** fallback.

## 1. Concept and theme

**Name:** Float. **Token:** FLOAT (ERC-20, Robinhood Chain, chain ID 4663). **Tagline:** "Build the city. Collect the dividend. Never trade."

**Player fantasy: the patient compounder.** You run a holding company that owns a city laid out as the 11 GICS sectors, each a district: Main Street (Staples), the Mall (Discretionary), the Yards (Industrials), Bank Row (Financials), the Campus (Tech), the Clinic (Health), the Field (Energy), the Quarry (Materials), the Grid (Utilities), the Blocks (Real Estate), the Tower (Comms). Every business is fictional and every number is in-game Cash. Growth comes from reinvestment (compounding), diversification (sector synergies) and going public (prestige). There is no buy/sell at a moving price, no chart, no ticker feed, no timing decision anywhere in the game.

**How it ties to real stocks without encouraging trading (F7, legal-eu.md #18-19):** Massachusetts fined Robinhood over confetti, scratch-off stock lotteries, tap games and referral stock (F7); the SEC's DEP list names games, streaks, prizes and free stock (legal-us.md #9). Float has none of the flagged patterns: no chance with value (C1), no celebration tied to any real transaction, no referral rewards with value (Apple 3.1.5(v), legal-us.md #31), no notifications about real assets, no leaderboards of real holdings, no prices of real securities in the client. Real stocks appear exactly once: on **Dividend Day**, the first trading day of each month, the loyalty balance a player earned is delivered as a fixed-ratio reward by a licensed party (section 4). The game teaches "hold and reinvest"; it never rewards frequency of anything financial. Copy follows Robinhood Chain ToS (robinhood.md S1-missed): we say "Stock Tokens", never "tokenized stocks"; our brand is primary; "Built on Robinhood Chain" is the only chain mark.

**Thin client over verifiable state, where it helps.** The simulation is a normal server-authoritative game (Cloudflare Durable Objects running the same deterministic TypeScript sim as the client, break_eternity.js numerics). Only what benefits from public verifiability lives on Robinhood Chain: season results (Merkle roots), FLOAT emission and claims, FLOAT sinks, sponsor-drop escrow and distribution lists, monthly payout roots, and team/treasury vesting via Sablier (deployed on 4663, tech.md #35). No PII on-chain (EDPB v2.0, F17); the Seat registry stores only an opaque id and a wallet address.

## 2. Core loop and progression

**Currencies (five, one job each):** Cash (soft, closed-loop, never withdrawable); Shareholders (prestige 1); Index Weight (prestige 2); Gold Bars (hard, IAP, never expire per Apple 3.1.1); Dividend Ledger (real, cents) and FLOAT (on-chain) — the last two never affect Cash production.

**Generators:** 44 (11 sectors x 4). `cost = base * r^owned`, `production = rate * owned * multipliers` (idle-design.md #1). r rises by sector: 1.07, 1.08, 1.09, 1.10, 1.11, 1.12, 1.13, 1.14, 1.15, 1.15, 1.15. Sector unlocks at lifetime Cash 0, 1e3, 1e5, 1e7, 1e9, 1e12, 1e15, 1e18, 1e21, 1e24, 1e27. Milestones: x2 profit at 25, 50, 100, 200, 300, 400, then every 100; x2 speed at 25/50/100 (bumps, idle-design.md #7). First generator "Corner Store": base cost 4, r 1.07, 1.67/s (AdCap constants). Bulk buy x10/x100/max via the closed-form cost.

## 3. Token model (Q1)

**Legal shape.** US: FLOAT is a *digital tool* (membership, ticket, credential) under the SEC-CFTC release (F10, legal-us.md #19): it has no yield, no revenue share, no stock claim, no governance over economics, and consumptive utility from day one. Because it is paid *for gameplay*, the airdrop interpretation does not cover it (legal-us.md #20), so its non-security status rests on the absence of any investment contract: no sale, no marketing of appreciation, no buyback, no liquidity provision, no listing. The game issues but never redeems FLOAT for fiat or crypto, so it is not a FinCEN "administrator" (F11, legal-us.md #27). NY is permanently geofenced from on-chain claims (BitLicense outside-market test, legal-us.md #28). EU: MiCA utility token under Art 4(3) "existing utility" (F13, legal-eu.md #2): the game operates before any claim; we never draft a white paper (Art 4(8)), never seek admission to trading (Art 4(4)), and Art 4(5) lets us run claim/sink contracts without CASP status (legal-eu.md S1-missed). UK: pre-unlock FLOAT is non-transferable and redeem-only, outside "qualifying cryptoasset" (legal-eu.md #33); post-unlock UK persons are excluded from claims.

**Transferability decision (Q1): deployed at launch, transfer-locked until a published Unlock Gate, then a plain ERC-20 that third parties may pool on Robinhood Chain DEXs; the studio never lists, never seeds liquidity, never buys back, never sells.** Locked mode allows only player -> sink contracts and Ledger -> player claims. If the gate is never met, FLOAT stays a soulbound on-chain ledger of standing — still real utility, still on Robinhood Chain.

**Supply and emission:** 1,000,000,000 FLOAT hard cap (OZ 5.7 ERC20Capped + Permit, immutable), MINTER_ROLE held by an Emission contract behind a 3-of-5 multisig with 7-day timelock. Allocation: 55% Play (10-year schedule, year-1 12% of Play, then −2 pp/yr to a 3% floor); 15% Team (Sablier, 4y linear, 1y cliff); 15% Treasury/LiveOps (timelocked, published budget); 10% Robinhood Chain ecosystem grants (integrations, Arbitrum Open House co-sponsorship, F3); 5% Founding Seats (retro to soft-launch players by a rule published before soft launch). Year-1 daily ceiling ≈ 180,800 FLOAT, but tokens are **minted only as earned**: each verified Seat earns fixed amounts per discrete milestone (Closing Bell 10, first Go Public of the day 10, Ladder objective 10, Fund contribution 10; max 40/day), never scaled by score or purchases (Google fixed-ratio rule, legal-us.md #32). Unearned ceiling is never minted, so emission scales *down* with real activity (the Axie/SLP failure inverted, p2e-lessons.md #5). Per-action amounts are fixed per season and published 30 days ahead; if Seats exceed the ceiling the Treasury tops up within the season and the next season's amounts drop — never retroactive (Hamster lesson, precedents.md #31).

On iOS none of this runs inside the app (Apple 3.1.1 bars crypto-unlocked functionality, precedents.md S1-missed); the iOS app shows balances and accrual only; claims and spends happen on the web companion or in Robinhood Wallet. Android declares Financial features and never advertises earnings (idle-design.md S1-missed).

**Where it lives, custody, UX.** Contracts on 4663: FLOAT; SeatRegistry (opaque seatId -> address, upgradeable, timelocked); Emission + Merkle distributor (per-season roots); Sinks; SponsorEscrow (USDG); PayoutLedger (Dividend Day roots). Gas sponsored via Alchemy Gas Manager or ZeroDev, both documented on Robinhood Chain (robinhood.md S1-missed AA; 8% overhead, tech.md #23), funded in ETH. Custody is the player's: Robinhood Wallet via WalletConnect (supported on Robinhood Chain, tech.md #21) as the default; an embedded 7702 wallet (Privy/Dynamic) with key export as fallback, provisioned only for claiming players to keep MAU fees small (Privy free tier is 0-499 MAU, tech.md S1-missed). Audit: boutique tier $15-25k, 10-14 weeks (tech.md #36). Chain risk: the sequencer can filter addresses (tech.md #5); every contract is chain-agnostic and state is server-held, so a filtered contract is redeployed on Arbitrum One with re-rooted Merkle claims; we monitor the 7-day Security Council timelock notices (robinhood.md S1-missed governance).

**What stops it becoming Hamster Kombat (F18):** (1) real value is paid in dollars from realized revenue, never in FLOAT; (2) no TGE, listing, liquidity, market maker or sale — there is no price for the team to promote; (3) per-human caps through Seats, not accounts; (4) emission tracks verified activity downward; (5) rules published a season ahead and never changed retroactively; (6) team/treasury streamed on-chain via Sablier; (7) unlock only after demonstrated sink demand; (8) FLOAT carries no rights to revenue, stock, yield or economic governance.

## 4. Stock reward path by jurisdiction (Q2)

Every player has a **Dividend Ledger**: a dollar-denominated loyalty balance accrued at fixed, published ratios (section 7). It is cents, not stock, until Dividend Day, when it is delivered at execution price. Between accrual and delivery there is no chance and no market exposure. The player chooses from "the Float 12": a broad-market ETF plus 11 sector ETFs (third-party securities bought on the open market, F8, F21). Delivery is monthly; minimum balance $1 (EU/ROW) or $5 (US, journal cost).

**US — fractional shares via broker.** SOLO: Alpaca Broker API. The player opens a brokerage account inside a partner flow (broker CIP: name, DOB, address, TIN, 18+; legal-us.md #33); the game funds a firm account that holds Float 12 inventory; on Dividend Day the broker journals shares to the player (JNLS journals are documented precisely for "reward your users", economics.md S1-missed Alpaca). Alpaca Securities LLC is also the custodian named in Robinhood's own Stock Token product spec (robinhood.md S1-missed product), a useful alignment for a later PARTNER handoff. The game never holds or transfers securities (C3). Funder: the Dividend Pool (section 7). Player sees: "Your $6.40 dividend was delivered as 0.0121 shares of [ETF]" with the broker's confirmation. PARTNER: Robinhood signs a flat marketing-fee agreement (no per-transaction compensation, FINRA 2040 caveat, legal-us.md #6) and delivers the sign-up stock itself under its own promotion terms; there is no Robinhood API to push our balances into Robinhood accounts (F4), so PARTNER means Robinhood credits, we pay a fee. Legal basis: third-party listed stock delivered by a registered broker-dealer into a KYC'd account (F8); deterministic ratios remove chance (F9); annual caps keep every player under the $2,000 1099-MISC threshold (F12).

**EU/EEA and other eligible countries (Stock Tokens).** SOLO (launch): on Dividend Day the game transfers the balance as USDG on Robinhood Chain to the player's self-custodied wallet (Robinhood Wallet recommended; USDG is a Robinhood Chain core token, tech.md S1 #1; MiCA status of the specific EMT to be confirmed before launch, EURC as alternate). Paying our own reward obligation in an EMT is not a crypto-asset service; the game never touches a security. The player then, self-directed, swaps USDG for any Stock Token on Uniswap/1inch through Robinhood Wallet's dapp browser (venues per robinhood.md #3; wallet features per #19). This copies the Cash App "stock gift" form — a restricted cash credit the recipient self-directs — which is the standard legal shape for "free stock" (precedents.md S2-missed Cash App). The Locker tab shows Stock Token holdings read from the RHJ registry and ERC-8056 `uiMultiplier` (robinhood.md S1-missed API; tech.md #9), with no prices, P&L or notifications. We deliberately do not prefill swap links or present named tokens: Robinhood's own materials do the offering (RHJ site terms restrict offers to Authorised Offerors, robinhood.md S1-missed). SOLO-CONDITIONAL (Phase 2b): a Voucher contract that routes the USDG into the player-chosen Stock Token in one call, only if Lithuanian counsel clears that a self-directed on-chain redemption via a decentralized venue is not MiFID execution/placing by the game (open question F14). PARTNER (preferred end state): Robinhood Europe UAB runs the promotion as licensed distributor — onboarded, appropriateness-tested clients, 180-day hold, its own terms (F5, legal-eu.md #10) — crediting Stock Tokens funded by the game's payment, plus a flat marketing fee per funded EU account. Classic Stock Tokens are never used: they are non-transferable MiFID derivatives exposed to the CFD inducement ban (F14, legal-eu.md #14-15, S1-missed Description of Services). Legal basis: no chance (deterministic), no stake (free to play), cash-value prize allowed under DE/NL/AT law when those two hold (F15); Robinhood's 18+ and EEA residency gates mirrored at Seat verification (legal-eu.md S1-missed Customer Agreement).

**Excluded countries (UK, CA, CH, UAE, sanctioned).** Stock Tokens are restricted there (F1, robinhood.md #7); UK bans "free crypto" incentives (F16). The Ledger accrues as Gold Bars at a published fixed ratio instead; no real-value reward, no token claim (CH: FLOAT claim allowed post-unlock). Sanctioned jurisdictions: no accounts.

## 5. Token-to-stock relationship (Q3)

**None, by design.** FLOAT is never redeemable for stock, never priced against stock, and holding or locking FLOAT never raises any Dividend Ledger ratio, cap, or sponsor-drop allocation. Any such link makes token purchases a claim on securities (custodial receipt or security-based swap, F10, legal-us.md #23), breaks C2, and violates Google's "not exponentiated by game performance" rule once value is attached. The only shared elements are the Seat (one identity gates both) and the on-chain PayoutLedger roots that let anyone audit both flows. FLOAT is the game's social economy; the Ledger is the game's loyalty program funded from revenue. Two separate systems is the boring, legal, fundable answer, and it is also what keeps FLOAT from becoming a pseudo-security the moment a price appears.

## 6. Identity, sybil and fraud (Q4)

**Primitive: the Seat** — one per verified human, 18+, jurisdiction-tagged, bound to exactly one wallet (SIWE signature via WalletConnect from Robinhood Wallet or the embedded wallet), with wallet changes requiring re-signature and a 7-day cooldown. Off-chain record (GDPR/AMLR, F17); on-chain only seatId -> address. There is no Robinhood OAuth (F4) and a WalletConnect signature proves wallet control, not KYC, so PARTNER = a Robinhood attestation ("verified, 18+, jurisdiction X"); SOLO = our KYC vendor (Sumsub Basic $1.35, Compliance $1.85 with AML, economics.md #22) in EU/ROW, and the broker's CIP in the US (broker returns an account id, never PII). Dedup: vendor face/document dedup across our applicant pool; in the US the broker's one-account-per-TIN. World ID was considered and rejected for now (Router not on 4663, Orb coverage limits; tech.md #34).

**When:** no Seat is required to play, accrue, or see the Ledger. Seat verification happens at the first real-value event (first Dividend Day delivery or first FLOAT claim), like Mistplay/ZBD (F19, precedents.md #26). Unverified accruals expire after 180 days of inactivity (published).

**Withdrawal gating:** verified Seat; 7-day pending window after each Dividend Day root (reversal on fraud); $1/$5 minimum; daily/season/year caps (section 7); geo checks (IP + KYC country) against the RHJ restricted list (robinhood.md S1-missed sanctions list) and the Robinhood Chain VPN prohibition.

**Device and behaviour:** Play Integrity (10k/day default quota, so only at claim/withdraw, tech.md #29) and App Attest; server replay of input logs; rewarded-ad SSV callbacks from AdMob/Unity/MAX/LevelPlay, idempotent on transaction id — an ad view that has no verified callback accrues nothing (tech.md #32); Fingerprint Smart Signals for emulator, device-farm and cloning (device farms are up 72% YoY, tech.md #31); behavioural clustering (synchronized logins, identical tap coordinates, many Seats paying to one exchange address — the Hamster playbook, precedents.md #31). Expect ~half of Android finance-style installs to be fake pre-KYC (economics.md #21); the Seat moves the fraud cost to the attacker: a real ID, liveness and a unique face for at most ~$15-30/year of extractable value.

**Cheaters:** published policy; pending balances forfeited, Seat suspended, future emission excluded (on-chain FLOAT already claimed cannot be clawed); human-reviewed appeal within 72h (the opaque-ban trust cost, p2e-lessons.md #33). We never reward referrals with value, removing the referral-farm vector entirely.

**KYC cost handling:** paid only for players reaching a first delivery (perhaps 5-10% of installs), budgeted at $2.50 per Seat in the Dividend Pool; US KYC is broker-borne.

## 7. Funding stack and payout rule (Q5)

**Revenue lines:** rewarded video (SSV-verified), interstitials, IAP, Season Pass and ad-removal subscriptions, sponsor drop fees, broker acquisition bounties.

**Published payout rule.** Each day, `Dividend Pool = 30% x net rewarded-video revenue + 15% x net IAP/subscription revenue + 100% x sponsor fees designated to drops + 50% x broker bounties actually received (paid only to the converting player as a Graduation Bonus)`. This is ~19% of net revenue in the examples below, inside ZBD's 10-20%-of-ads guidance once IAP is added and under Mode's audited ~25-33% bound (F19, economics.md #19 skeptic). Accrual ratios per geo tier are set each season so that expected accrual at 100% vesting ≤ pool; a coverage ratio (pool/accrual) is published monthly; if coverage <1.0 for 14 days, next-season ratios fall — accrued cents are always honoured (C1, C4). FLOAT never appears in the rule, so a zero token price changes nothing.

**Accrual ratios (EU tier / US tier):** verified rewarded view $0.0020 / $0.0035 (max 4/day); daily Closing Bell $0.003 / $0.006; weekly Earnings Call $0.02 / $0.04; Season Pass +$0.02 / +$0.04 per day (the loyalty benefit tied to a purchase, strengthening the Google posture, legal-us.md #32); Listing Bonus $2 one-time at Seat verification; Graduation Bonus (US only) = 50% of any bounty received, cap $10, disclosed as a material connection (legal-us.md #29). ROW tier-3 geos run at 0.5x EU (eCPMs $1-5, idle-design.md #23), like Bitcoin Miner's per-country caps (precedents.md S2 #16).

**Caps per Seat:** $0.03/day base, $0.06 with Pass (US $0.06/$0.10); $2 per 30-day season base, $4 with Pass (US $4/$6); $50/year plus one-time bonuses ≤$12 plus sponsor drops ≤$60 -> hard annual ceiling $150, far below the $2,000 1099-MISC line (F12), the NL EUR 449 gaming-tax line (moot: deterministic) and Ireland's EUR 3,000 gift exemption (legal-eu.md #41, #43).

**Sponsor drops (F21):** a sponsor escrows USDG (EU/ROW) or funds the broker firm account (US) in SponsorEscrow; the drop is a fixed amount per verified Seat completing a published objective during Earnings Season; if oversubscribed, allocation is by Seat seniority ("book-building"), deterministic and pre-published, never by chance. Default asset is the sponsor's *sector ETF*; a sponsor's own stock only under issuer counsel within Rule 10b-18 limits and outside Reg M restricted periods (Landa precedent, economics.md #30), and never a private company (F5). PARTNER option: Robinhood's Partner Stock Program, which already lets companies gift shares to customers' Robinhood accounts with issuer 1099 duties (precedents.md S2-missed), same Reg M caveat.

**Broker bounty use:** US SOLO = public affiliate programs ($20-50 Robinhood, $20-70 Webull-class, F6/F20, economics.md #13) structured as flat marketing fees; half to the player, half funds delivery and KYC. EU: none at launch (MiFID introducer questions, legal-eu.md #16). The model must close with bounty = 0, and it does.

## 10. Why this beats the obvious alternatives, and what to cut first

*Pure points, no token* loses the ask's second leg and Robinhood Chain composability; Float keeps the token real (on 4663, verifiable emission, sinks) without the failure modes. *Tradeable token at launch with seeded liquidity* is the NOT/HMSTR/PIXEL path (F18): our unlock gate, zero treasury liquidity and revenue-denominated payouts are the difference. *Token redeemable for stock* is a custodial receipt or swap (C2, legal-us.md #23) — illegal for retail. *Classic Stock Tokens as rewards* are non-transferable derivatives under a likely inducement ban (F14). *US-first* has no on-chain rail (F2) and the richest fraud (economics.md #21); non-US-first on Robinhood Chain uses rails that exist today. *Robinhood-dependent* designs die if the partnership never lands; here Robinhood Wallet and USDG are public rails and Alpaca is public, so PARTNER only upgrades UX and economics. The exciting part survives: real Stock Tokens in the player's own Robinhood Wallet, a real on-chain token, sponsor drops with an on-chain audit trail, and an idle game tuned to genre-best retention.

**Cut order if forced:** (1) the Unlock Gate — FLOAT stays soulbound on-chain forever (still utility, zero market risk); (2) the US rail — EU/ROW only, no broker integration; (3) sponsor drops; (4) the web companion — Android-only token claims; (5) Season Ladder and Funds; (6) last, the Dividend Ledger itself, leaving a good idle game with a locked token — which is the honest floor of this product.

# DRAFT 5: Snowball Inc.

A server-authoritative idle tycoon about compounding in which verified adult players convert capped, revenue-funded loyalty credit into fractional index-fund holdings via a licensed broker (US) or a self-directed EURC credit on Robinhood Chain (EU/EEA), with a non-transferable membership token on Robinhood Chain that never touches the stock.

# Snowball Inc. — architecture proposal (CFO / tokenomics lens)

Design principle: every real-value payout is funded from realized, banked revenue of the prior season; nothing is minted against engagement; the token is never in the payout formula; the game is fun with the reward tab hidden. Anything requiring Robinhood cooperation is tagged PARTNER with a SOLO fallback (C9).

## 1. Concept and theme

**Name:** Snowball Inc. **Token:** DRIFT (a snowball is made of drift). **Player fantasy:** you inherit a lemonade stand and grow it into a compounding conglomerate across the real economy's sectors. Every generator is a business in one of 12 sectors (Consumer Staples lemonade stand, Utilities water tower, Materials quarry, Industrials rail yard, Energy wind farm, Real Estate towers, Health Care clinic, Financials credit union, Consumer Discretionary theme park, Communications satellite, Technology chip fab, Space logistics). The theme teaches compounding by feel, not by charts: reinvesting output into more capacity is the only lever, milestone doublers are literally "economies of scale," and the prestige layer is an **IPO**: you take the company public, cash out to "Founder Shares," and start the next venture with a permanent edge. The second prestige layer is a **Holding Company** that owns your IPO'd firms.

How it ties to real stocks without encouraging trading (F7, ESMA gamification lens, legal-eu #18-19): the only real asset the player ever receives is a broad index fund (VOO in the US; the SPY or VOO Stock Token on Robinhood Chain in the EU). No ticker selection, no price charts, no buy/sell buttons in the app, no celebrations tied to any trade, no streaks with cash prizes, no leaderboards for rewards (in-game cash leaderboards only). The message is "you now own a slice of everything, hold it," which is the opposite of the Massachusetts complaint pattern (F7) and of the SEC 2021 DEP list (legal-us #9). Sponsors appear as generator skins ("Sector Week presented by X") and fund index-fund grants, never their own stock (F21). Copy rules from Robinhood Chain ToS (robinhood.md missed facts): say "Stock Tokens" and "Built on Robinhood Chain," never "tokenized stocks," and keep the Snowball brand primary.

## 2. Core loop and progression

**Currencies (four, deliberately separated; p2e-lessons #10, #18):**
- *Cash* (soft, per run, resets at IPO).
- *Founder Shares* (prestige 1, permanent, off-chain).
- *Blueprints* (hard currency, IAP only, never expire per Apple 3.1.1; also earned slowly from milestones).
- *Ledger Points* (LP): the reward-accrual points. Off-chain ledger entry, "promotional, no cash value until redeemed," fixed-ratio accrual (C1, C7), 18+ tab. Not tradeable, not a token.
- *DRIFT* is not a game currency; it is a membership credential (section 3).

**Generators and costs (F23, idle-design #1-2):** cost_next = base × r^owned with r = 1.07 for sectors 1-4, 1.10 for 5-8, 1.12 for 9-12; base cost per tier ≈ 11× the previous, base output ≈ 6.5× (Cookie Clicker ratio). Production = base × owned × multipliers. Milestone "scale" doublers ×2 at 25/50/100/200/300/400/500 owned (AdCap), plus a ×7.77 "golden ticket" per sector from a Season Pass track (cosmetic-free progression reward). Bulk-buy ×1/×10/×100/max. Numbers on break_infinity.js (idle-design #29); the server uses the same deterministic library.

**Automation:** managers (buy once per sector) collect automatically; "Analysts" (prestige shop) auto-buy the cheapest generator; "CFO" (Holding Company layer) auto-prestiges.

**Prestige 1 (IPO):** Founder Shares gained = 150 × sqrt(lifetime_run_earnings / 1e15) − already_claimed (AdCap square-root, needs ~4× earnings to double, idle-design #4-5). Each share +2% profit. First IPO reachable at ~$1e12 lifetime (≈5 shares) around hour 5-6 of play (end of day 2 for a typical player). Runs shorten as shares stack; expect 8-12 IPOs in week one for engaged players.

**Prestige 2 (Holding Company):** unlocks at 1e60 cumulative earnings across IPOs; grants Compound Points, multiplicative ×1.05 each (Egg Inc prophecy-egg style, idle-design #12), and resets Founder Shares. Layer 3 (Index) reserved for the 1e308 wall (idle-design #10), month 6+ content.

**Offline:** server computes offline yield from its own timestamp (never device wall clock, idle-design #31): 8h cap at full rate, +2h per Silo research level to 12h, 24h with the Season Pass. "Double offline earnings" rewarded ad at return.

**Session shape:** 3-5 sessions/day of 3-8 minutes: collect, spend all cash, hit a milestone bump, optionally watch one 2× boost (10 min), leave. First interstitial after 90 s, max 2 per session, 3-min spacing (idle-design #24). Rewarded placements: 2× income (10 min), 4h time-skip, double offline, "rush IPO" (skip the last 10% of a run). Target 3-4 rewarded views per DAU at 55% opt-in (F20, F23).

**First 5 minutes:** tap the lemonade stand (manual clicks), buy stands to 10 by 0:45, first manager at 1:00 (automation moment), first ×2 at 25 owned by 3:00, unlock Utilities at 4:00, first "scale" popup explains compounding in one sentence. No ad before 90 s, no rewards tab visible.

**Day-1 beats:** 4 sectors, 2 managers, first 2× boost, first Blueprint from a milestone, starter pack offer (permanent ×3, $2.99) at the third sector unlock (idle-design #27). Rewards tab appears at the end of session 2 for players who self-declare 18+, with the honest line: "Most players earn under $1 a month; the point is owning a slice of the market."

**Day-7 beats:** 3+ IPOs, Analysts, first Sector Week event (weekend, 2× event revenue like Kolibri's event mines, idle-design #17), first Holding Company tease, and ~15% of D7 survivors crossing the $5 LP threshold that triggers the brokerage/KYC prompt.

**Targets (C6):** D1 45-55%, D7 20-25%, D30 8-10% (idle titles beat the 22/4/0.75 mobile medians, F23; we set targets below Kolibri's 63-81% D1). Kill criterion: D1 < 35% in soft launch means the core is not fun, and no reward layer ships until it is.

**LiveOps:** 30-day Seasons with a $4.99 Season Pass (event track, 24h offline, cosmetics, no reward multiplier), weekend Sector Weeks, a monthly public "Earnings Call" that publishes the payout pool, points issued, rate, mint/burn ratio and fraud actions. Monetization that holds (F23): permanent multipliers, ad-removal $3.99/30d, Blueprint packs $1.99-$19.99, Season Pass. No loot boxes, no gacha, no chance anywhere, even cosmetic (keeps Belgium, PEGI 16 default and Play odds rules out of scope, legal-eu #25, missed facts).

## 3. Token model (Q1)

**What DRIFT legally is.** US: a "digital tool" under the SEC-CFTC interpretive release (F10; legal-us #19): a membership/credential that performs a practical function, carries no yield, no revenue rights, no redemption right, and is never marketed as an investment (C2). The airdrop safe harbor does not apply because DRIFT is earned (legal-us #20), so its non-security status rests on the digital-tool category plus the absence of any investment-contract promise. EU: a MiCA utility token (F13) giving access to a service that already operates (the Clubhouse and Season Pass), relying on the Art 4(3) "existing utility" exemption from Title II; because it is non-transferable and never admitted to trading, Art 4(5) means no CASP licence is needed to hold or move it for players (legal-eu missed facts). UK: a redeem-only, non-transferable token is outside "qualifying cryptoasset" (F16), but we still do not promote it there.

**Transferability decision (Q1): non-transferable at launch and for at least 18 months; transferability is an optional Phase 4 exercised only if (a) CLARITY is law or Regulation Crypto Assets is final, (b) US and Lithuanian counsel sign off, (c) a MiCA Title II white paper is filed, and (d) a Robinhood PARTNER listing path exists (F4, robinhood.md #31). Default outcome: it stays a soulbound pass forever, and the product loses nothing.** A tradeable engagement token is the single common factor in every collapse in F18; we refuse to be number 15.

**Contract and chain.** Immutable ERC-20 (OpenZeppelin 5.7 ERC20Capped + AccessControl MINTER_ROLE), cap 1,000,000,000, 18 decimals, `_update` override allowing only mint, burn, and transfers to or from the Clubhouse and Redeemer contracts (soulbound). Deployed on Robinhood Chain mainnet (chain 4663, ETH gas, ~$0.001 fees, permissionless deployment, F3), verified on Blockscout. Periphery (Clubhouse, Redeemer, Sablier-based sponsor-drop vesting; Sablier is already on 4663, F3) is upgradeable behind a 3-of-5 multisig with a 7-day timelock. Audit budget $10-20k, 8-14 weeks (F24). Sequencer risk: Robinhood can hash-list-filter a contract address (F3, tech #5); mitigation is a pre-audited mirror deployment on Arbitrum One and an off-chain ledger of record so a filtered chain never blocks gameplay or payouts.

**Supply and emission ("points first, token later" at both product and player level).** No sale, no team/investor allocation (Big Time precedent, p2e-lessons #21), no TGE event. Emission is a function of revenue, not headcount (p2e-lessons #1, #5, #25): each season the minter is authorised to mint 1 DRIFT per $0.01 of the previous season's net revenue, distributed pro rata to verified players' fixed-ratio Ledger Points, capped at 5,000 DRIFT per account per season, plus 100 DRIFT per Season Pass purchase (IAP-receipt verified). DRIFT accrues as a pending off-chain balance and is minted on-chain only after the player's identity attestation (section 6), so bots never touch the chain. Published target: burns ≥ 50% of the season's mint, reported in the Earnings Call; if the mint/burn ratio exceeds 2:1 for two seasons, next season's mint halves (Axie's 4-6× ratio was the failure signal, p2e-lessons #3).

**Utility and sinks (all deterministic):**
1. Clubhouse membership credential (web dapp on Robinhood Chain): sponsor-drop enrolment requires holding ≥ 500 DRIFT (a gate, not a burn, so no "stake").
2. Burn 200 DRIFT for a 4h time-skip or 2× boost instead of watching an ad, on Android and web only.
3. Burn for cosmetic generator skins and IPO badges (digital collectibles) in the Clubhouse; on iOS, cosmetics are unlocked by IAP receipts, never by token balance, because Apple 3.1.1 bars crypto-based unlocks (precedents missed facts).
4. One-account-one-vote polls on next Sector Week theme (balance-independent, so holding more confers nothing).
5. Season Pass holders' redemptions batch weekly instead of monthly (a convenience, not value).

**Custody and wallet UX.** Each player silently gets an ERC-4337 smart account (EntryPoint v0.7 at 0x0000000071727De22E5E9d8BAf0edAc6f37da032 on 4663; Dynamic or Privy embedded wallets; Alchemy Gas Manager sponsorship at an 8% fee on ~$0.001 gas, tech #22-23 and robinhood.md missed facts). Players never hold ETH. "Link Robinhood Wallet" via WalletConnect deep link (F4, tech #21) registers a Robinhood Wallet address as the display and EU credit destination. Personal data stays off-chain; on-chain there is only the smart-account address and a season-badge nullifier (F17, EDPB v2.0).

**What stops it becoming Hamster Kombat (F18):** no engagement mint; no tradeability; no unlock cliff (there is nothing to unlock); rules published 7 days before each season and immutable within it (Hamster's late PPH-to-keys switch caused the boycott, p2e-lessons #11); three-ledger separation (points, token, stock) so a zero token price changes no payout; hard per-account caps; mint only to verified humans; and if Phase 4 ever happens, a 10%-of-net-revenue buyback paced at 2-5% of daily volume (economics #32) with the game explicitly designed to keep working at price zero.

## 4. Stock reward path by jurisdiction (Q2)

**United States: fractional VOO through a registered broker-dealer.**
- SOLO: Snowball Rewards LLC (the marketing affiliate) signs with Alpaca Securities (Broker API; JNLS security journals are documented for exactly "reward your users" flows, economics missed facts) or Apex via a Bits of Stock-style white label (legal-us #4). Player crosses $5 pending credit, opens a KYC'd 18+ brokerage account inside the app (CIP: name, DOB, address, SSN; legal-us #33). Snowball Rewards pre-funds a firm account, buys VOO at the close each day against claimed credits (Webull's claim-then-buy template, economics missed facts), and the broker journals fractional shares (5-decimal, Apex) into the player's account; sale proceeds are non-withdrawable for 30 days (Robinhood/Grifin template). The player sees "5,000 LP → $5.00 → 0.0087 VOO in your Snowball Brokerage account (by Alpaca)" and a read-only holdings card. Alternative SOLO form if the broker prefers it: a restricted cash credit that self-directs into VOO and is forfeited if unused within 60 days (Cash App's legal form, precedents skeptic 2).
- PARTNER: Robinhood buys VOO on the open market and credits the player's Robinhood brokerage account; Robinhood's referee sign-up stock reward (F6) serves as the ignition grant; Robinhood Connect application ID for wallet funding. No API or partner program exists today (F4), so this is a Phase 3 pitch.
- Why legal: third-party listed securities bought on the open market and delivered by a broker-dealer into a KYC'd account avoid Section 5 (F8); deterministic fixed-ratio credit removes chance (F9); compensation to the game is a flat marketing CPA never tied to trades or balances (Rule 2040 / 15(a), legal-us #6, counsel sign-off item); no Robinhood Stock Token ever reaches a US person (F2).

**EU/EEA: on-chain Stock Tokens, with Robinhood or a licensed party as the only distributor.**
- PARTNER (preferred, mirrors Robinhood's own practice): Robinhood Europe UAB runs the "Snowball Season Grant" under its own promotion terms, exactly like its Share Token Giveaway (VOO/NVDA tokens for no consideration, onboarded and appropriateness-tested clients only, 180-day hold; legal-eu #10). The player completes Robinhood EU onboarding (18+, EEA resident, legal-eu missed facts), Robinhood credits SPY/VOO Stock Tokens to the player's Robinhood Wallet, and Snowball pays RHEU a flat fee per verified redemption as a marketing partner with copy approved under MiFID II Art 24. Classic Stock Tokens are excluded entirely: they are cash-settled derivatives inside the CFD inducement ban and are non-transferable anyway (F14, legal-eu #14-15, missed facts).
- SOLO: the reward is a EURC credit (a MiCA e-money token; Robinhood Europe itself pays its Dividend Match in EURC capped at 1,000 EURC, robinhood.md missed facts) delivered by an EU-authorised EMI/CASP partner (ZBD holds MiCAR and EMI licences in the Netherlands, precedents #18) to the player's linked Robinhood Wallet on Robinhood Chain. The player may then, self-directed inside Robinhood Wallet, swap EURC for the SPY or VOO Stock Token on Uniswap (F1: 24/7 DEX, freely transferable Jersey debt securities). The game never holds or transfers a security (C3), never says "free stock" (UCPD Annex I item 20, legal-eu #21), and shows a neutral "hold / withdraw / swap" screen with no token recommendation. Whether even that neutral mention is a "promotion" of a prospectus offer is the open question in F14 and must be cleared by Lithuanian counsel before Phase 3; if not cleared, the SOLO screen drops the swap mention and the player simply receives EURC.
- Why legal: the Jersey tokens are MiFID transferable securities under an FMA-approved, passported prospectus whose consent covers only licensed Authorised Offerors (robinhood.md missed facts), so the game must never be the offeror; F14 puts Robinhood, not the game, in the distributor seat; deterministic allocation keeps NL/BE/IT gambling and promo-draw rules out (F15).

**Other eligible countries (Robinhood's 120+ list minus restricted):** the EU SOLO template, country by country, only after local counsel clears prize/promo, tax and e-money rules; Phase 3 launches with EEA only. **Excluded (UK, CA, CH, UAE, sanctioned list: Cuba, Belarus, Iran, North Korea, Russia, Syria, Ukraine, South Sudan, Sudan, Myanmar, Venezuela; F1, tech missed facts):** full game, LP redeemable only for Blueprints or gift cards via licensed rails where lawful, no real-asset rewards, no token promotion in the UK (F16). US persons are screened out of the Stock Token path by residency KYC, IP/geo, self-certification and Reg S wording.

## 5. Token-to-stock relationship (Q3)

DRIFT has no conversion, no fixed rate, no price link and no multiplier on any stock reward (C2; a token redeemable for stock is a custodial receipt or security-based swap, F10). The only relationships are gates and sinks: holding DRIFT is the membership condition for sponsor-drop enrolment, and burning DRIFT replaces an ad view for boosts on Android/web. Reward caps follow the Season Pass IAP entitlement, not token balance, so Google Play's "fixed ratio, not exponentiated by performance" rule (F9) and Apple 3.1.1 hold. Three ledgers (LP, DRIFT, VOO/Stock Token), three custodians (game, chain, broker/wallet), zero arithmetic between them.

## 6. Identity, sybil and fraud (Q4)

**Binding primitive (C5):** a Verified Person Attestation (VPA) issued by the licensed delivering party at first withdrawal: {hash(partner_customer_id), jurisdiction, is_18plus, issued_at}, signed, no PII to the game (F17). US: the broker's CIP produces it; EU SOLO: the EMI/CASP partner's KYC (Sumsub Basic $1.35 + AML ~$0.50, F22, tech #33); PARTNER: Robinhood onboarding. One VPA maps to exactly one game account (unique index on the hash); a second account presenting the same VPA is merged into the oldest and its pending credit forfeited. Brokerage KYC data is never repurposed for the game (AMLR retention, legal-eu #39).

**Pre-withdrawal signals (device and behaviour, never sufficient alone):** Play Integrity (PLAY_RECOGNIZED, LICENSED, MEETS_DEVICE_INTEGRITY; 10k/day default quota means we request only at claim and withdraw, tech #29) and App Attest; server-authoritative simulation with deterministic replay of the client input log from the last checkpoint (F23), so impossible progress per elapsed second is rejected; rewarded-ad server-side verification callbacks (AdMob ECDSA, Unity HMAC, AppLovin MAX, LevelPlay) with idempotent transaction IDs, and LP credited only on a verified callback (F24, tech #32); a 3-minute rewarded cooldown and 10/day hard cap; Hamster-style behavioural clustering (same-pixel taps, synchronised logins, shared ASN/IP, 400-accounts-one-address patterns, precedents #31) and Sardine-class fleet detection for physical device farms (+72% YoY, tech #31).

**Withdrawal gating (C4):** $5 minimum; 14-day settlement window (Acorns holds 60-120 days, Mistplay uses pending windows); first withdrawal requires the VPA; VPN/proxy/emulator blocks withdrawal, never gameplay; per-account daily/season/year caps; velocity rules on claim-to-withdraw timing.

**Cheaters:** pending credit forfeited, rewards tab disabled, game still playable; a 72-hour human appeal path and monthly published enforcement counts (opaque bans right before payout are the top trust complaint, p2e-lessons #33); legitimate players never see retroactive rule changes (C1).

**KYC cost handling:** US CIP cost sits inside the broker CPA economics; EU checks (~$2 all-in) are paid only when pending credit is ≥ $5, so KYC never exceeds 40% of a first payout and is amortised across the $30 annual cap; budget comes from the non-pool half of CPAs and general margin. Expect ~50% of Android finance-style installs to be fake pre-KYC (F22); the funnel, not the payout layer, absorbs them.

## 7. Funding stack and payout rule (Q5)

**Revenue lines:** (1) rewarded video (SSV-verified), (2) interstitials, (3) IAP (starter pack, Blueprints, ad-removal, Season Pass), (4) broker acquisition CPAs (affiliate $20-$50 per funded account SOLO, F6/economics #13; bespoke PARTNER at $50+, the value Robinhood itself now assigns to one funded referral, F6), (5) sponsor escrow.

**The Snowball Rule (published in-app and in the Earnings Call):**
Pool(S+1) = 20% × net ad revenue(S) + 10% × net IAP revenue(S) + 50% × broker CPAs cash-received(S) + 100% × sponsor escrow earmarked for drops in S+1. "Net" means after network and store fees; "received" means banked, never accrued. The season LP rate is set 7 days before S+1: rate(S+1) = clamp(Pool(S+1) / projected LP(S+1), 0.5 × rate(S), 1.25 × rate(S)), fixed for the whole season. Accrual is fixed-ratio: 10 LP per verified rewarded ad, 5 LP per $1 of IAP, nothing for referrals, installs or social posts (Apple 3.1.5(v), F9). The token is not a term in the formula; the CPA term can be zero.

**Per-account caps:** $0.15/day and $3/season and $30/year (base); $0.25/day, $5/season, $50/year with the Season Pass entitlement; plus one lifetime $5 "Ignition Grant" of VOO or EURC paid from the CPA share, pre-published as contingent on a CPA actually being received (if the broker channel closes, the grant pauses next season, not mid-season). Maximum legal annual value ≈ $55, an order of magnitude under the $2,000 1099-MISC threshold (F12), under Mistplay's $550 (F19), and in the spirit of Stash's $10/month bonus cap (precedents skeptic 2).

**Sponsor drops (F21):** sponsors buy a Sector Week skin plus a fixed grant, e.g. "$0.50 of VOO to the first 10,000 verified players who complete Sector Week," funded 100% into escrow before the event, deterministic and pre-published. Rewards are always a third-party index or sector ETF, never the sponsor's own stock (Reg M Rules 101/102, Rule 10b-18, Section 5; Landa 2023 terminated, economics #30). Pitch data: Bumped's +40% spend and +100% after $5-$10 grants (F21). Sponsor money is additive; the base pool never depends on it (Bumped died when brands stopped paying, precedents #3). EU drops run only through the PARTNER path or as EURC credit.

**Broker bounty use:** half of every CPA funds the Ignition Grant and the pool; half covers KYC, broker journal fees, 1099 administration and margin. Robinhood's new Tiered Referrals is for individual US residents and excludes paid creators (F6), so the game uses affiliate CPAs SOLO and negotiates a flat-fee marketing agreement PARTNER; counsel confirms the agreement is not transaction-based (Rule 2040).

**Worked example, US-heavy cohort, per 1,000 DAU, one 30-day season (assumptions from F20-F22):**
- Ads: 3.0 rewarded/DAU/day at $12 eCPM = $36/day; 1.5 interstitials/DAU/day at $6 eCPM = $9/day; gross $1,350; net of 15% network take = **$1,150**.
- IAP: $0.030 gross ARPDAU (idle titles skew 60-70% ads, idle-design #26) = $900 gross; net of 25% blended store fee = **$675**.
- Broker CPAs: 6% of DAU-count cross $5 in the season, 50% complete KYC, 65% fund → ~20 funded accounts × $35 = **$700** SOLO ($1,000 at a $50 PARTNER rate).
- Sponsor: $0 in the base case.
- Net revenue: $2,525. Pool = 230 + 67.5 + 350 + 0 = **$647.5 (25.6% of net revenue; $0.0216 per DAU-day)**.
- Demand at a published rate of 1,000 LP = $1.00: 55% of DAU watch ~3 verified ads = 1,650 ads/day × 10 LP = $16.50/day = $495/season; IAP LP = $45; 20 Ignition Grants = $100; total **$640 ≤ $647.5**. Caps bind for heavy users, not the pool.
- Costs outside the pool: EU-style KYC not needed (broker CIP inside CPA); journal fees ~$20; infra ~$0.02/DAU-month = $20; 1099 admin negligible under caps. Game retains ~$1,850 (73%) for UA, team and margin.
- **Stress 1, token price zero:** no term changes; pool unchanged.
- **Stress 2, Robinhood never partners and affiliate CPAs vanish:** pool = $297.5; rate resets to 2,000 LP = $1 (the 0.5× floor), Ignition Grant paused; demand = $247 + $22 = $269 ≤ $297.5; payout ratio 16% of $1,825 net, still inside the ZBD 10-20% envelope (F19).
- **Stress 3, EU cohort at $7 blended eCPM (F20):** ad net ~$670, IAP net ~$600, CPA $0 (no documented Robinhood EU affiliate) → pool $194 → EU-specific published rate 3,000 LP = $1; demand ~$180. Per-geo rates are fixed-ratio within a season and mirror Bitcoin Miner's per-country caps (precedents skeptic 2).
- **Stress 4, 40% bot share pre-KYC:** bots earn LP but cannot pass the VPA, so their LP expire unredeemed (180-day expiry, Mistplay template); the pool is sized on realized revenue, and bot ad impressions that networks scrub never enter "net."

## 9. Launch sequencing (Q7)

## 10. Why this beats the obvious alternatives, and what gets cut first

**Against a tradeable engagement token (Hamster/Notcoin/Pixels):** every one lost 95-99.9% and shed users at ~1M/day post-TGE (F18); ours cannot, because there is nothing to dump and no payout depends on it.
**Against randomized stock rewards (Robinhood's old $5-$200):** chance plus gameplay effort is the highest-risk gambling design (F9), violates Google Play's fixed-ratio rule, and is exactly what Massachusetts fined (F7).
**Against a token redeemable for stock:** that is a custodial receipt or security-based swap (F10); we keep three ledgers with no arithmetic between them.
**Against the game holding or transferring Stock Tokens itself:** the prospectus consents only to licensed offerors and F14 puts Robinhood in the distributor seat; the SOLO EURC credit keeps us outside securities distribution in every jurisdiction.
**Against a pure ad-funded sats-style game:** it pays cents (Bitcoin Miner ~$0.25/day cap, F19) and has no acquisition economics; adding the broker CPA more than doubles the pool per 1,000 DAU and delivers the thing the ask actually wants: players holding an index fund.
**Against building the token first:** Big Time made $100M with no token sale; Gigaverse $4.5M with no token (p2e-lessons #21, #27). The token is the least load-bearing piece here.

**Cut order if forced:** (1) EU on-chain path first (fall back to LP → gift cards, keep the US path); (2) DRIFT entirely (the game and the stock rewards need nothing from it; fidelity to the ask drops, legal surface drops more); (3) sponsor drops; (4) Season Pass cap tiers. Never cut: the server-authoritative core, the published Snowball Rule, the VPA gate, or the honest "cents to a few dollars" positioning.

## Top risks
- Lithuanian counsel concludes that even a neutral in-app mention of swapping EURC into Stock Tokens is a promotion of a prospectus offer or MiFID distribution by an unlicensed party (F14 open question); mitigation is the EURC-only SOLO screen or the Robinhood Europe PARTNER route, and if both fail the EU path collapses to points-for-gift-cards.
- Rule 2040 / Exchange Act 15(a) finder risk if any broker compensation is construed as transaction-based; mitigation is a flat marketing fee per funded account with a counsel opinion, and if unresolved, dropping CPAs from the pool (modelled: pool still positive).
- App-store rejection: Apple 3.1.1 (crypto-based unlocks), 3.1.5 (crypto app paying for tasks) and Google Play's fixed-ratio loyalty and Financial Features declaration rules; mitigation is IAP-only entitlements on iOS, deterministic fixed-ratio LP, and no earnings marketing.
- Fraud and device farms: ~50% of Android finance-style installs fake and affiliate traffic at 36x SRN fraud rates (F22); the identity attestation stops payouts to bots but UA metrics and ad revenue quality will be polluted, and KYC cost per verified player must stay under 40% of first payout.
- Thin EU unit economics ($5-9 eCPM, no documented Robinhood EU affiliate CPA) yield a pool of ~$0.007 per DAU-day, which may read as insulting rewards; mitigation is honest positioning and geo-specific rates.
- Robinhood Chain governance: single sequencer with address hash-list filtering could reject the game's contracts, and ToS terminology/brand rules could trigger takedowns; mitigation is the Arbitrum One mirror, off-chain ledger of record and copy compliance.
- FTC earnings-claim enforcement and UCPD 'free' rules if marketing drifts toward 'earn stock by playing'; mitigation is typical-earnings disclosure and no 'free stock' copy anywhere.
- The core idle game fails to hit D1 ≥ 40% / D7 ≥ 15% in soft launch, in which case the reward layer must not ship (C6); the whole stack depends on the game being fun alone.
- Tax characterisation (prize vs compensation for services, state withholding, FR/DE/NL treatment) could impose reporting burdens the caps were designed to avoid; mitigation is counsel sign-off before Phase 1 and Phase 3.
- CLARITY fails and Regulation Crypto Assets stalls, leaving the token's US classification on the interpretive release alone; acceptable because DRIFT stays soulbound and nothing in the payout stack depends on it.
