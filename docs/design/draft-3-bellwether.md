# DRAFT 3: Bellwether

Bellwether is an eleven-sector idle tycoon whose non-transferable BELL credential on Robinhood Chain proves you played, and whose revenue-funded, fixed-ratio Stock Credit walks verified adults onto a brokerage account (US, fractional shares) or into Robinhood Wallet Stock Tokens (EEA) at cents-to-dollars scale, with every Robinhood dependency labelled and a unilateral fallback for each.

# Bellwether: architecture proposal (regulatory-perimeter-first)

Design stance: every real-value flow is deterministic, revenue-funded, capped, and delivered by a licensed party; the token is a credential, not a currency; the game must be worth playing with the reward tab hidden. PARTNER = needs Robinhood cooperation; SOLO = buildable alone. Facts cited as F-numbers (brief) or file#finding.

## 1. Concept and theme

**Name:** Bellwether. **Token:** BELL (a non-transferable "Charter" credential). **Loyalty ledger:** Stock Credit (USD/EUR-denominated, off-chain). **Soft currencies:** Capital (run cash), Float (IPO prestige), Weight (index prestige).

**Player fantasy:** grow a one-kiosk startup into a bellwether conglomerate across the eleven GICS sectors, take it public, get included in the index, and start again bigger. Compounding is literal: exponential generator costs versus linear output (F23), prestige converts exponential growth back into a ladder. Sectors are the generator tiers (Consumer Staples kiosk through Real Estate REIT). IPO is the first prestige (you convert your private company into Float, i.e. permanent shareholders). Index Inclusion is the second prestige (Weight unlocks new markets). Dividends, splits, diversification and fees are taught through an in-game "Prospectus" reading room, written plainly and counsel-reviewed, and the Stock Token ERC-8056 multiplier (F1) is explained as "your one token quietly becomes 1.0006 tokens after a dividend".

**What the theme deliberately does not do (ESMA/SEC/Massachusetts lens, F7; legal-us#9; legal-eu#18-19):** no live prices, charts, P&L, or "buy now"; no leaderboards ranked by real value; no confetti or celebration tied to any financial action; no streak penalties; no chance in anything with cash value (C1); no naming of specific securities in reward copy (player self-directs; broad ETF default). Copy uses Robinhood's mandated terms only: "Stock Tokens", "Built on Robinhood Chain", own brand primary (robinhood.md skeptic missed facts: Chain ToS 2026-08-24 and brand guidelines). Store listings never mention earnings (Google Blockchain policy, legal-eu missed facts; FTC earnings-claim posture, legal-us#29). Honest positioning in-app: "Stock Credit is cents to a few dollars a year. The point is that it lands in a real account you own."

## 2. Core loop and progression

**Generators (11 sectors).** cost_next = base × r^owned; production = base_rev × owned × multipliers (idle-design#1). Constants follow the AdCap ladder: cost step ×12 per tier, revenue step ×6-10, cycle step ×2-4; r = 1.07 (tier 1), 1.15 (tier 2), then declining 1.14 → 1.07 through tier 11 (idle-design#1-2). Tier 1 "Corner Kiosk": cost 4, revenue 1, 1 s cycle. Tier 11 "Skyline REIT": ~3e11 base cost, ~10-hour cycle. Milestones: ×2 profit at 25/50/100/200/300/400 owned, then every 100 to 1,000; speed doublers at 25/50/100 for tiers 1-4 (F23). "Make it bumpy" (idle-design#7).

**Automation and upgrades.** One Manager per sector (cost ≈ 250× tier base cost) converts tap-to-collect into auto-cycle. "Analyst reports" are ×3 sector multipliers priced at ~50× the tier's cost at purchase time; "Bellwether Reports" are global ×2 at Capital thresholds 1e6, 1e12, 1e18…

**Prestige 1: IPO.** Float = 150 × sqrt(lifetime_capital / 1e15) − Float already claimed; each Float unit = +2% all profit (idle-design#4; ~4× earnings to double). First IPO is reachable at Float ≈ 50 (×2) after roughly 3-4 wall-clock hours including offline time. Float is spent on "Underwriters" (permanent perks: S-1 filing ×3 all profit at 1e5 Float; instant Manager unlock; offline capacity).

**Prestige 2: Index Inclusion.** Unlocks at lifetime Float ≥ 1e6 (engaged players ~day 10-14). Weight = floor(cbrt(lifetime_Float / 1e4)) (cube-root: ~8× to double, long runs, idle-design#3). Each Weight = +10% Float effectiveness and unlocks Markets 2 and 3 (Europe, Asia) with their own generator sets and per-market Float constants (AdCap Earth/Moon/Mars pattern, idle-design skeptic missed facts). Per-sector ×7.77 "Golden Analyst" bought with Weight; full set grants ×10 more.

**Number system.** break_infinity.js / BreakInfinity.cs from day one; content authored to ~1e100 in year one; a third layer ("Conglomerate") is reserved for the 1e308 wall (idle-design#10, #29).

**Offline rules.** 8 h at 100% at launch (inside the 2-24 h envelope, F23), upgradeable with Float to 16 h and 24 h (Egg Inc silo pattern, idle-design skeptic missed facts). Offline yield is computed server-side from the server clock; the client's SystemClock.elapsedRealtime is used only for UI (idle-design#31). Return moment offers a rewarded ×2 offline ad. Rewarded 4 h time-skips, max 3/day.

**Session shape.** 3-5 sessions/day of 4-8 min: ring the Bell (daily claim), collect offline, spend all Capital, buy managers/reports, queue an event action, leave (idle-design#7, #17).

**First five minutes.** 0:00 tap Kiosk; 0:20 second Kiosk; 0:45 Microgrid unlock; 1:30 first Manager (hands go free); 2:00 ×2 at 25 Kiosks; 3:00 Machine Shop; 3:30 first Analyst report; 4:00 first rewarded offer (×2 for 4 h, opt-in); 5:00 Bell tutorial ("ring it tomorrow; your Charter grows with play", no value language). No interstitial in sessions 1-3; first interstitial only after 60-90 s of a later session (idle-design#24).

**Day 1 beats.** Four sectors, first offline return with ×2 offer, first IPO ceremony (modest, no confetti), Sector Sprint visible, starter pack offered at the third sector unlock (the conversion moment, idle-design#27): permanent ×3 for $2.99 (idle-design#7).

**Day 7 beats.** 8-11 sectors, 3-5 IPOs, first cooperative Index Sprint, battle pass offer, and (US/EEA only) the Vault tab reveal: "Stock Credit pending: $0.07. Verify to open your Vault and receive the $5.00 Listing Bonus." The trickle is honest; the on-ramp is the event.

**Targets.** D1 45% floor / 55% target, D7 20%, D30 8% (mobile medians 22/4/1, idle titles far higher, F23; Kolibri prototype D1 63-81%, idle-design#17). 3.5 rewarded views/DAU at 55% opt-in; interstitials ≤ 2/session, 2-3 min spacing, never > 3 (20-30% D7 penalty, idle-design#24).

**LiveOps.** Weekly Sector Sprint (event-mine pattern; doubled weekend revenue for Kolibri, idle-design#17), monthly Season with a $4.99 pass (ad-free days, cosmetics, Float boosters, never Stock Credit), quarterly "Earnings Season" cooperative goals. Backend from day one (PlayFab/Supabase class, tech#26) so events and segments exist at launch (AdCap's post-launch regret, idle-design#20).

**Monetization.** Permanent ×3 starter pack, ad-removal subscription $3.99/30 d (idle-design#19), time warps, battle pass, cosmetics. Two ad surfaces only: rewarded (primary) and light interstitials. Nothing essential is ad-gated (idle-design#33).

**Anti-cheat.** Server-authoritative checkpoints (state + server timestamp + seed) with deterministic replay of the client input log from the last checkpoint; affordability and rate-plausibility checks; leaderboards and any reward-bearing event are written only after replay validation (idle-design#32, tech#28). Play Integrity / App Attest requested only at Bell claim and redemption to stay inside the 10k/day default quota (tech#29-30).

## 3. Token model (Q1)

**What BELL legally is.** A non-transferable, non-convertible credential earned only by verified play and consumed inside the Bellwether ecosystem. US: a "digital tool" (membership/ticket/credential; no yield, no income rights) under the SEC-CFTC interpretation (F10; legal-us#19). We do not rely on the airdrop interpretation because BELL is issued in exchange for gameplay (legal-us#20). It is closed-loop and not convertible to fiat or crypto, so the studio is not an FinCEN administrator/MSB and BELL falls inside the NY BitLicense gaming-unit exclusion (F11; legal-us#27-28). EU: because BELL cannot be transferred, it is arguably not a MiCA crypto-asset at all; if a regulator disagrees, it is a utility token for a service that already operates (Art 4(3)(c)), never admitted to trading (Art 4(4) preserved), with no CASP needed for our own custody/transfer of it (Art 4(5)) and no voluntary white paper (Art 4(8)) (F13; legal-eu#2, missed facts). UK: outside "qualifying cryptoasset" because it cannot be transferred or sold except by redemption with the issuer and is limited-use (legal-eu#33). BELL is never marketed with the words earn, invest, value or price.

**Transferability decision (Q1): non-transferable at launch and for at least 12 months after going on-chain; "tradeable" only if three gates are met later (US and Lithuanian counsel opinions; a demand source that is not new players; twelve months of published emission data), and we tell players now that no such change is planned and no TGE will ever be announced as a reward.** Rationale: a transferable game token has no exogenous demand and every comparable collapsed 95-99.9% (F18; p2e#11-16, precedents#30-33); transferability would also void the MiCA exemption, the UK exclusion and the FinCEN/BitLicense exclusions in one stroke.

**Supply and emission.** Hard cap 1,000,000,000 BELL, immutable ERC-20 (OpenZeppelin 5.7.x ERC20Capped + MINTER_ROLE, tech#35, skeptic note on 5.7.0). 90% player emissions, 10% LiveOps reserve minted only through events, 0% team/investors/sale. Season 1 global cap 2% of supply, declining 10% per season. Per account: daily Bell claim 10 BELL (one per day, integrity-attested), milestone bounties (first IPO 50, each Index Inclusion 100, Sprint participation 20/week), hard cap 60/day and 1,500/season. Rules are published before Phase 2 and never changed retroactively (C1; Hamster Kombat's fatal error, precedents#31). Balances accrue off-chain and are minted on-chain via a Merkle claim distributor only for verified Seats, so bots never cost us gas.

**Utility list (all sinks burn BELL):** (1) Settlement ticket: 25 BELL burned per Stock Credit redemption; (2) LiveOps governance: 10 BELL per vote on next week's Sprint sector; (3) Charter seat: 100 BELL/season to join a cooperative Syndicate for Index Sprints; (4) Listings: cosmetic nameplates/skins minted with BELL (also sold as IAP, satisfying Apple 3.1.3(b) parity); (5) tenure proof: sponsor drops require ≥ 7 Bell claims in the current season; (6) composability: `seasonsActive(address)` and burn history are public so other Robinhood Chain apps can read proof-of-play (the Lighter-points precedent shows third-party points programs inside Robinhood's ecosystem, robinhood.md#15). BELL spending happens in the web hub ("Exchange Floor") and is displayed read-only in the mobile builds, because Apple 3.1.1 bars in-app crypto mechanisms from unlocking content or functionality (precedents skeptic missed facts).

**Where it lives.** Chain 4663, deployed permissionlessly (F3), transfer override permits only mint-from-minter, burn-to-registered-sink, and one-time migration between a Seat's own verified addresses. Gas sponsored via ERC-4337 (EntryPoint v0.7 0x0000000071727De22E5E9d8BAf0edAc6f37da032; AA on Robinhood Chain is Alchemy-powered, tech missed facts) at ~$0.001 + 8% (tech#3, #23). If the sequencer's ArbOS 61 filter ever rejects our contract ("Transaction rejected by chain policy", tech#5, missed facts), the ledger is authoritative and we redeploy on Arbitrum One from the ledger, a rule published in advance. Audit: boutique ERC-20 tier, $5-20k, 8-14 weeks (tech#36).

**Custody/wallet UX.** At verification an embedded wallet is created (Privy/Dynamic; budget $0.05/MAU, since Privy's free tier is only 0-499 MAU per the skeptic correction to tech#22), keys exportable; players may link Robinhood Wallet via WalletConnect on chain 4663 (tech#21) and migrate BELL there, where it is visible like any ERC-20 (robinhood.md#19).

**What stops Hamster Kombat:** no market, so no price to crash; no airdrop or TGE promise, enforced in the contract; allocation rules immutable; all external value flows through Stock Credit, not BELL; per-account caps and behavioural sybil filters; a bot that mints BELL gains nothing it can sell.

## 4. Stock reward path by jurisdiction (Q2)

Stock Credit is a dollar/euro-denominated promotional credit with no cash value until redeemed, held pending until verification (Mistplay pattern, precedents#26), and delivered in the Cash App legal form: a restricted credit the recipient self-directs into a purchase inside a licensed account, forfeited if unused (precedents skeptic-2 missed facts). The game never holds or transfers securities (C3).

**United States (fractional shares).**
- PARTNER: Robinhood Financial delivers the fixed $5 Listing Bonus as its own sign-up stock reward (fixed value, no odds; its current referee reward exists but value/odds are unstated, robinhood.md#23) and accepts Stock Credit as tagged promotional deposits into the player's brokerage account; Robinhood supplies a signed attestation (verified, 18+, US, unique account hash) per hashed player. Compensation to the game is a flat marketing fee, not per-trade or per-asset (FINRA 2040/Exchange Act 15(a), legal-us#6); all co-branded copy is principal-approved under FINRA 2210 (legal-us#8). No public API or OAuth exists (F4), hence PARTNER.
- SOLO: a white-label "Vault Account" opened in our flow with a FINRA broker-dealer partner; first choice Alpaca Securities LLC, whose Broker API documents JNLS security journals precisely for rewarding users with small share quantities and JNLC cash journals (economics#24, missed facts), and which is also the broker/custodian in Robinhood's own RHJ Stock Token stack (robinhood.md skeptic missed facts). The BD performs CIP (name, DOB, address, TIN; effectively 18+, F12; legal-us#33). Stock Credit lands as a cash journal the player self-directs, with a broad-market ETF default. The Listing Bonus is funded by the Robinhood affiliate bounty ($20-50 per qualifying action via Impact, economics#13) when the player also opens and funds Robinhood through our link, if the affiliate terms permit incentivized referrals; if not, it is funded from IAP margin at $3. Legal basis: third-party listed stock bought on the open market through a BD avoids Section 5 (F8; legal-us#1-2); deterministic value removes chance (F9); Google Play fixed-ratio loyalty rule satisfied (legal-us#32).
- Player sees: "Vault opened. $5.00 Listing Bonus + $0.07 credit arrived as cash. Pick any stock or ETF, or keep the default." Under PARTNER the same screen says "in your Robinhood account".

**EU/EEA (on-chain Stock Tokens).** Classic Stock Tokens are MiFID OTC derivatives; handing them out is a prohibited non-monetary benefit under the CFD product-intervention measures and a circumvention risk (F14; legal-eu#9, #14-15). They are excluded (C8).
- PARTNER: Robinhood Europe UAB runs a "Bellwether Stock Token promotion" under its own giveaway template: onboarded, appropriateness-tested clients, 180-day hold, tokens delivered by RHEU (F5; legal-eu#10). We ask that the instrument be the on-chain Jersey Stock Token, that the eligible set be broad-market ETFs or player choice (no single-name nudge), and that RHEU return a signed eligibility attestation. The game is a marketing partner under MiFID Art 24 with RHEU approving creative (legal-eu#16). Flat fee. This is the only path that puts Stock Tokens into a Robinhood account.
- SOLO: the game never touches Stock Tokens (they are prospectus-passported debt securities whose public offer is reserved to Authorised Offerors; a third-party retail distribution is the open question in F14 and legal-eu missed facts). Instead, EUR Stock Credit is disbursed as USDG (the chain's core stablecoin, robinhood.md#12) to the player's self-custody address on chain 4663, proven by a WalletConnect signature from Robinhood Wallet or any EVM wallet (tech#21). The player then, self-directed, swaps in Robinhood Wallet on Uniswap/RFQ (robinhood.md#3-4). Robinhood Europe itself pays a Dividend Match in EURC to customers (robinhood.md skeptic missed facts), so stablecoin payouts to EU retail have a Robinhood precedent. Disbursement is executed by a MiCA-authorised EMI/CASP partner so a non-EU studio never provides a crypto-asset service (ESMA 2026-04-17, legal-eu#6). Our copy never names a Stock Token and never says "buy"; the swap UI is Robinhood's. Gate: Lithuanian counsel must confirm this is neither reception/transmission nor a marketing communication about a financial instrument; if refused, EU SOLO falls back to EUR via SEPA/PSP and the player self-funds Robinhood Europe.
- Player sees: "€5.00 sent as USDG to your Robinhood Wallet. Hold it, or swap it inside your wallet. Stock Tokens are tokenised debt securities issued by Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Ltd; they are not shares." Eligibility: 18+ EEA resident (RHEU Customer Agreement 4.2-4.3, legal-eu missed facts), sanctions screen against the RHJ list (robinhood.md#7).

**Excluded countries (UK, CA, CH, UAE, sanctioned).** Full game and BELL (non-transferable, no value), no Stock Credit accrual at all (F16; legal-eu#33-35; robinhood.md#7). Rest of world where Robinhood offers Stock Tokens: same SOLO path as EEA, enabled country by country in Phase 4 after local promo-law review.

## 5. Token-to-stock relationship (Q3)

BELL is never redeemable for, priced against, or a multiplier on Stock Credit; a token redeemable for stock at a fixed rate would be a custodial receipt or a retail-prohibited security-based swap (F10; legal-us#23) and would breach C2. The only links are deterministic gates that prove humanity and tenure: the 25-BELL settlement ticket per redemption (a ticket, a digital tool), and the seven-Bell-claim tenure requirement for sponsor drops. The published Stock Credit ratios are identical for a player with 0 BELL and one with 10,000 BELL. BELL governs LiveOps (which sector gets the Sprint), never money. This keeps Google Play's "not exponentiated by game performance" rule satisfied (legal-us#32) and keeps BELL's value consumptive rather than financial.

## 6. Identity, sybil and fraud (Q4)

**Primitive: the Verified Seat.** One Seat per human; one game account per Seat. Created at first redemption, never earlier. Sources: US SOLO, the BD partner's CIP (one account per SSN/TIN); US PARTNER, Robinhood's attestation; EU PARTNER, RHEU's attestation; EU SOLO, our own KYC (Sumsub $1.35-1.85 or Veriff $0.80-1.89 with liveness and document/face de-duplication, tech#33; economics#22) bound to a wallet address by WalletConnect signature. We store only an attestation record (vendor reference, jurisdiction, 18+ flag, verified-at, address set) off-chain; no PII on-chain (EDPB v2.0, F17; legal-eu#38) and brokerage KYC is never repurposed (AMLR retention, legal-eu#39).

**Duplicate enforcement.** A second game account presenting the same identity is denied Seat linkage and its pending credit is forfeited (published rule). Because the Seat is the only route to value, extra accounts can only pollute ad metrics, which ad networks scrub anyway.

**Withdrawal gating.** Minimum redemption $5 / €5; weekly Settlement Days; 25 BELL burned; ad credits pending 7 days, IAP stock-back pending 60 days (past the store refund windows; adjoe refund-fraud pattern, p2e#36); withdrawal allowance grows with play (ZBD's decoupled earn-rate/withdrawal-limit pattern, precedents skeptic missed facts).

**Device and behavioural signals (supplementary, never sufficient, C5).** Play Integrity verdicts (PLAY_RECOGNIZED, LICENSED, MEETS_DEVICE_INTEGRITY) and App Attest at claim/redeem (tech#29-30); Fingerprint/Sardine-class emulator, VM, root, Frida, cloned-app, VPN and fleet-pattern signals, since fraud has shifted to physical device farms (+72% YoY, tech#31); behavioural fingerprints proven by Hamster Kombat's bans (same-pixel taps, synchronized logins, identical progression curves, precedents#31); VPN blocks rewards only, not play (Robinhood Chain ToS also bars VPN masking).

**Ad SSV.** Credit only on server-side callbacks (AdMob ECDSA, Unity HMAC, MAX, LevelPlay), idempotent on transaction_id/oid/EVENT_ID, ≤ 6 credited views/day, ≥ 2 min apart, client-claim mismatch flags the account (tech#32).

**Cheaters.** Automation, multi-accounting, emulator farms, refund abuse: pending Stock Credit forfeited, BELL burned, 12-month reward ban, game access retained. Human-reviewed appeal within 14 days and quarterly published enforcement stats, because opaque pre-payout bans are the top complaint in reward apps (p2e#33). Biometric KYC vendors must be BIPA-compliant (JustPlay $4.5M settlement, precedents skeptic missed facts).

**KYC cost handling.** PARTNER paths cost us $0. US SOLO: inside the BD per-account fee (assumption $3/account, unpublished, economics#24 open question), funded from the bounty/IAP margin. EU SOLO: ~$2 per check, incurred only at a ≥ €5 redemption, funded from the EU pool's unclaimed breakage and IAP margin. Worst-case exposure per fake identity that beats KYC is one $5 Listing Bonus plus the account fee, which is below the market cost of a clean synthetic identity.

## 7. Funding stack and payout rule (Q5)

**Revenue lines.** (1) Rewarded and interstitial ads; (2) IAP; (3) sponsor drop fees (sponsor funds 100% of the drop plus a 20% service fee); (4) on-ramp bounties (SOLO: Robinhood affiliate $20-50, comparable brokers $20-70, F20; economics#13; PARTNER: negotiated flat marketing fee, benchmarked against Robinhood's ~$150-222 per net funded add and $187 ARPU, economics#10, missed facts).

**The Bell Rule (published, in-app and on the web).** Monthly Reward Pool P = 0.20 × A + 0.04 × I + S + 0.25 × B, where A = net ad revenue, I = gross IAP, S = sponsor drop funds, B = bounties received (ZBD's 10-20% of ad revenue guidance, F19; p2e#31). Player accruals are fixed ratios published per geo tier for the period: per SSV-verified rewarded view US $0.003, EEA tier-1 $0.0014, EEA tier-2/3 $0.0008, recomputed monthly as 20% × trailing-30-day realized eCPM ÷ 1,000 (JustPlay's floating rate, precedents#27) with 30 days' notice and never retroactively; IAP stock-back 4% of gross spend (Stash/Acorns ~0.5-1% territory, F21); Listing Bonus $5 once (US; €2 EU SOLO, €5 EU PARTNER); sponsor drops fixed $0.25-0.50 per qualifying deterministic action. A segregated reserve ≥ 3 months of trailing payouts is held before any ratio increase (Sky Mavis stability-fund logic, p2e#4).

**Caps per account.** Daily $1.00 (ad credits ≤ $0.03), season (month) $25, year $200, which is under the $2,000 1099-MISC threshold (F12; economics#25), Ireland's €3,000 gift exemption and Lithuania's €200 prize exemption (legal-eu#43-44). Sponsor drops ≤ 4/month.

**Sponsor drops (F21, Reg M).** A sponsor (ETF issuer, consumer brand, public company) funds a fixed $ per verified player who completes a published deterministic action inside a window (e.g. "build 50 Microgrids during Grid Week"); the first N completers get it, N published in advance, so there is no chance. Credit is self-directed; the default is a broad ETF containing the sponsor. A sponsor may not require purchase of its own stock: issuer-funded own-stock giveaways hit Section 5, Reg M Rules 101/102 and Rule 10b-18 (Landa 2023, economics#30), so own-stock drops happen only if counsel clears 10b-18-compliant open-market purchases through the BD with blackout monitoring; Robinhood's Partner Stock Program (companies gifting their own common stock into Robinhood accounts with issuer 1099-NEC/W-2 reporting, precedents skeptic-2 missed facts) is a PARTNER option for such sponsors, counsel-gated. In the EU sponsor drops are EUR/USDG credits, never derivatives.

**Bounty use.** 25% to the pool (funds the Listing Bonus), 25% to verification and account fees, 50% margin and reserve.

**Worked example per 1,000 DAU (assumptions from F20-F22, economics#1-7).** Geo mix US 45% / EEA 30% / UK-CA-CH-UAE 10% / RoW 15%. Rewarded eCPM US $15, EEA $7, UK-group $10, RoW $3; 3.5 rewarded views/DAU (55% opt-in); 1.5 interstitials/DAU at ~55% of rewarded eCPM; IAP gross ARPDAU $0.03 (2.5% payers), blended store fee 25%.
- Rewarded: 3,500 views → US $23.6 + EEA $7.4 + UK $3.5 + RoW $1.6 = $36.0/day. Interstitials: $8.4/day. Ad ARPDAU $0.044 (inside the $0.036-0.05 band, F20).
- IAP: $30 gross, $22.5 net. Net revenue ≈ $67/day ≈ $2,000/month.
- Pool ex-bounties: 0.20 × $44.4 + 0.04 × $30 = $10.08/day ≈ $302/month.
- Accruals: US views 1,575 × $0.003 = $4.73/day; EEA 1,050 × $0.0014 = $1.47/day; total $186/month. IAP stock-back 4% × $900 = $36/month. EU SOLO Listing Bonuses: with MAU ≈ 4× DAU and 1.5% of MAU verifying monthly (60 seats: 36 US, 24 EU), 24 × €2 ≈ $52. Total $274/month = 91% of the ex-bounty pool.
- US Listing Bonuses 36 × $5 = $180, funded by 25% of $720 bounties ($20 × 36) when the affiliate allows incentivized traffic.
- Cash-out after 50% unverified breakage on ad credits: ≈ $93 + $36 + $52 + $180 = $361/month. Verification costs: EU 24 × $2 = $48; US 36 × $3 = $108; sponsored gas ≈ $5. Reward-side outflow ≈ $522/month against $2,000 revenue + $720 bounties = 19% of revenue.
- Token price zero: identical (BELL has no price by construction). Robinhood never partners: this is the SOLO case above. Affiliate bans incentivized traffic: bounties $0, US Listing Bonus cut to $3 from IAP margin ($108), outflow ≈ $450 = 22.5% of $2,000, still inside the Mode/ZBD 10-25% envelope (F19; economics#19). eCPM falls 40%: the floating ratio cuts accruals 40% the following month, pool stays at 20% by definition.

## 9. Launch sequencing (Q7)

**Phase 0 (Sep-Dec 2026): counsel and core.** US memos: BD/finder structure and Rule 2040 support letter; 50-state gambling read on deterministic time-based rewards (open question in legal-us); app-store review strategy (web redemption); 1099 characterisation (prize vs services). Lithuanian memo: BELL outside MiCA or Art 4(3)/(5); SOLO USDG disbursement not RTO/placing/marketing of a financial instrument; GDPR DPIA. Pitch Robinhood at HOOD Summit (Sept 29-30, 2026) and apply to the Arbitrum Open House buildathon (F3). Ship the game to closed beta with off-chain points only. Book the ERC-20 audit. ## 10. Why this beats the obvious alternatives, and what to cut

**Alternatives rejected.** A tradeable engagement-minted token with a promised airdrop: the entire cohort lost 95-99.9% and bled users at ~1M/day post-TGE (F18). Chance-based stock drops (Robinhood's own former scratch-off): the thing Massachusetts fined and the strict-state lottery risk when time is consideration (F7, F9). A token redeemable for stock at a rate: a security or a retail-banned swap (F10). A game vault that buys and pushes Stock Tokens to EU wallets: unlicensed distribution of prospectus securities (F14). Cash-only rewards: legal and dull, with no on-ramp to ownership. Waiting for a Robinhood partnership: never ships, which is why every Robinhood-dependent piece here has a SOLO twin.

**Why this works.** The value engine is the on-ramp bounty and the loyalty trickle, both paid from realized revenue under a published rule; the token cannot crash because it cannot trade; verification is a licensed party's KYC, not a device fingerprint; the game stands alone at idle-genre benchmarks; and the player truly ends up holding fractional shares (US) or Stock Tokens in Robinhood Wallet (EEA).

**Cut order if forced.** 1) Sponsor drops. 2) EU SOLO USDG path (keep the RHEU PARTNER track only; EU gets the game plus BELL). 3) On-chain BELL (keep it as off-chain points until a Robinhood Chain partner wants composability). 4) Second prestige layer and Markets 2-3. Never cut: server authority, verification-at-withdrawal, the published Bell Rule, and the no-chance rule.
