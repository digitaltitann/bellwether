# Adversarial review findings (3 of 4 reviewers completed)

## Reviewer 1

No fatal flaw: the load-bearing choices (third-party ETF credit delivered by a FINRA BD into a KYC'd account, deterministic fixed-ratio credit funded from banked revenue, a bound non-priced token with zero linkage to stock, cut order that removes each exposed feature) are the right ones and match every surviving precedent. The must-fix problems are structural rather than conceptual: (1) the US SOLO rail as written has the unregistered studio curating a securities menu and auto-defaulting cash into an ETF with no RIA in the chain; (2) sponsor-drop economics pay the studio a percentage of securities placed, with an ETF issuer as the first sponsor, which is distribution compensation to a non-BD and also breaks the published Bell Rule arithmetic; (3) drops keyed to game objectives violate Google Play's game-performance rule; (4) the mandatory positioning line names Robinhood's Reg S 'Stock Tokens' in the US store and contradicts D16 and the EEA SOLO reality; (5) the SOLO revenue stack lists per-account broker bounties that either do not exist or are the finder pattern the ADR rejects, and the PARTNER 'flat fee' is benchmarked per account; (6) the closed-loop token argument leaks through unbound ERC-1155 cosmetics; (7) the no-chance invariant does not cover Washington's 'thing of value' doctrine because purchasable/ad-paid randomness exists in progression. All seven have cheap, concrete fixes that should be reflected in sections 1, 3, 4, 7 and the gates before Phase 2. Remaining items are polish (iOS 3.1.1 handling of BELL-funded effects, an unverified 'Partner Stock Program' citation, tax-exemption framing, T1 age gating, in-app brokerage onboarding).

- **[major] 4 US SOLO (the Vault), 1 Vault tab, D9 index-only menu**: The game (an unregistered entity compensated by ads/IAP) curates a 12-ETF menu, maps game sectors to sector ETFs, and auto-invests the player's cash credit into an S&P 500 ETF after 30 days. Curating securities for retail with a default and defaulting uninstructed cash into a security is investment advice / discretionary investing. No RIA exists in the ADR's chain, and the BD partner is exposed to Reg BI and the live Massachusetts fiduciary rule for a themed nudge list.
  - Evidence: Every surviving precedent has an adviser in the loop: Stash rewards 'fulfilled by Stash Investments LLC' (SEC-registered RIA) with a user-chosen 'Default Investment' (legal-us#2; precedents skeptic-2 missed facts); Bits of Stock = Emcee Invest, Inc., an SEC-registered investment adviser (legal-us skeptic-2 #4); Acorns Advisers LLC (precedents#5). Robinhood's own program makes the customer pick from 26 and never auto-invests (robinhood#23). Massachusetts fiduciary rule reinstated by the SJC Aug 25, 2023 and the Jan 2024 order targeted 'popularity-based stock lists' and nudges (legal-us missed; robinhood#28). SEC 2021 DEP RFI flags games/prizes that steer securities choices (legal-us#9). Alpaca B2B partner terms are unpublished (economics#24), so 'the broker will run a default' is an assumption.

D10's 'third-party ETFs only' is defined against the sponsor's own STOCK and does not cover an ETF issuer sponsoring its own ETF. ## Reviewer 2

No fatal flaw: the core choices (no chance touching value, zero token-to-stock linkage, broker-delivered cash credit, bound-transfer token, member-state EEA rollout) survive the MiCA, MiFID/CFD-intervention, Prospectus, gambling, GDPR and store-policy lenses. Add the internal contradictions on affiliate CPAs (finder pattern the decision log says was rejected), the mislabelled 'existing template' EEA PARTNER path, BELL-unlocked skins on iOS (3.1.1), and performance-awarded ETF drops on Android. Every fix is a design or copy change within the existing chassis; none requires new counsel questions beyond widening item 1 to cover any per-account broker payment.
