# Topic: p2e-lessons

## Summary

Across every named title, the same failure signature recurs: an uncapped or player-count-scaled reward currency (SLP, GST, BERRY, HMSTR points, NOT taps) whose only demand was new-player inflow or speculative belief in future price. Once inflow slowed, sinks (breeding, sneaker repair, crafting) collapsed faster than emissions because spending required optimism while earning required only presence. Every token in the set now trades 95-99.9% below its all-time high as of 1 Sept 2026: SLP -99%, GST from $7.80 to ~$0.0013, HMSTR -98.3%, NOT -98.4%, CATI -95.9%, PIXEL -99.5%, BIGTIME -99.3%, PRIME -99.2%, ILV -99.9%, GUN -97.4%, SONIC -98.2%, BLUM -99.2%, DOGS -97.5%, X Empire -98.6%. DAU followed price: Axie 2.7M DAU (Nov 2021) to <500K; Hamster Kombat 300M claimed users to 48.5M MAU within five weeks of TGE, losing ~1M users/day; Ronin average DAU fell 47.6% QoQ in Q2 2025 and active addresses fell ~70% across 2025 as Pixels faded from a 1.6M-DAU peak.

Bot/sybil share was consistently a large fraction of reported users: Pixels' CEO estimated 40% of daily active wallets were bots (Nov 2023), an outside CEO estimated 70% (May 2024), Pixels banned 750K accounts in one week; Hamster Kombat banned 2.3M accounts pre-airdrop and Telegram-verified users (155M) were about half the claimed 300M; Sky Mavis halted all SLP ranked emissions on 7 Jan 2026 explicitly because a shrinking player base plus unchanged rewards created bot-farming at scale. Removing bots historically cut DAUW by up to 70% (Splinterlands, Alien Worlds), so headline DAU in reward games should be discounted heavily.

The teams' own stated lessons converge: Notcoin's founders (May 2025) said tap-to-earn is "probably dead because [it is] not sustainable" and that farm-motivated users leave immediately after extracting; Illuvium's CEO admitted being "too heavy on micro-transactions while lacking in basic gameplay"; Sky Mavis reframed SLP as a "reward currency requiring stable value" with a 2% deflation target and a USDC-funded stability fund rather than an appreciating asset. Delphi Digital's 2025-26 work found participation dropped as soon as emissions slowed, that burns are "closer to a symbolic gesture," that performance-gated unlocks work, and that a revenue-weighted token portfolio returned +30.6% (Jan 2025-May 2026) while BTC fell 17.2%.

What survivors did differently: (1) separate the soft currency from the tradeable token and gate withdrawals (Pixels BERRY vs PIXEL with $35 VIP and 20-50% "Farmer Fee"; Sweatcoin points vs SWEAT token; Big Time's non-crypto Time Crystals); (2) tie payouts to revenue rather than to player count (Off The Grid commits 30% of gross revenue to buy back GUN for performance-based player distribution; Parallel funds prize pools from half of a 10% NFT royalty plus battle-pass buybacks and caps PRIME earning at 5 wins/day; Illuvium Staking V3 releases ILV only at 150 ETH revenue milestones; Pixels targets Return-on-Reward-Spend >1.0); (3) hard caps per player per day (Parallel 5 wins, Gods Unchained 10 games, Mistplay 2 hrs/game and ~$550/yr); (4) sell the game first (Gigaverse: ~$10 entry, ~37% paying, $3M in 111 days, no token; Big Time: $100M revenue with zero token sales). Regulation moved in favour of pure utility/collectible tokens: the SEC/CFTC joint interpretive release of 17 March 2026 classes in-game items and utility tokens as non-securities unless marketed with profit expectations from issuer effort. In the Philippines, P2E income has been taxable since Aug 2021 and the BSP VASP moratorium was extended indefinitely in Aug 2025.

For a game that pays out real assets, the best-documented payout benchmark is ZBD's published guidance of a reward budget at 10-20% of ad revenue, enforced with daily withdrawal caps, device attestation, VPN detection and light KYC at first cash-out. Mistplay pays roughly $0.003 per unit, $0.50-$3 per hour, and has paid $150M total over ~10 years against 40M+ MAU. Fraud baselines: AppsFlyer's 2026 report puts ~22% of non-organic installs under fraud signals, casual-game Android at 18-22%, affiliate/rewarded-type sources near 40% versus ~1% for self-reporting networks; offerwall scrub rates run 10-25% in tier-1 geos.

## Findings (36)

1. **[high]** Axie Infinity SLP fell from ~$0.39 (July 2021) to under $0.004 by mid-2022; DAU peaked at 2.7M in Nov 2021 and MAU fell to ~700K by mid-2022. Root cause: SLP emissions scaled linearly with player count while the only meaningful sink (breeding) did not; sources (scholarship fiat, minting) vastly exceeded sinks. Inference from both sources: the model was pyramid-shaped, new deposits funding old withdrawals.
   - Source: https://chainclarity.io/axie-infinity (2026-05-31)
   - Relevance: Establishes the canonical emission-vs-sink failure the spec must avoid: never let reward supply scale with headcount when demand does not.

2. **[medium]** Yu-kai Chou's Axie post-mortem identifies three design failures: imbalanced sources/sinks, speculation-driven rather than utility-driven token value, and extractive player psychology ('I'm still down $600, hoping I can break even') where loss-avoidance, not fun, held players.
   - Source: https://yukaichou.com/gamification-study/economy-design-framework-axie-infinity-collapse/ (2026-03-19)
   - Relevance: Names the 'extractive player' problem: players who arrive to earn behave as liquidity, not community, and churn on the first price drop.

   - Source: https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2022/02/08/axie-infinity-reduces-slp-emissions-to-prevent-collapse (2022-02-08)
