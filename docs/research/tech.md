# Topic: tech

## Summary

Robinhood Chain is real, live, and usable as of September 2026: public testnet on 2026-02-10, public mainnet on 2026-07-01, built on Arbitrum Orbit/Nitro settling to Ethereum L1 with EIP-4844 blob DA, ETH gas, ~250 ms blocks, median fees ~$0.001, permissionless contract deployment, and ERC-4337 + EIP-7702 live at canonical addresses (chain ID 4663; testnet 46630; public RPC rpc.mainnet.chain.robinhood.com; Blockscout explorer). The catch is governance: a single Robinhood-operated sequencer, a whitelisted validator set, and ArbOS 61 "Elara" transaction filtering (sequencer address hash-lists plus an on-chain ArbFilteredTransactionsManager precompile that can force-fail any tx hash, even force-included ones). L2BEAT therefore rates it with no guaranteed inclusion if the sequencer censors. The canonical bridge is Ethereum<->Robinhood Chain (7-day withdrawals); reaching it from Arbitrum One requires third-party routes (Relay, Across, LiFi, Stargate, CCIP). Arbitrum One remains a sane fallback (~$0.004 avg fee, 250 ms soft confirm, BoLD permissionless validation).

Tokenized-stock delivery is the hard constraint. Robinhood Stock Tokens are plain 18-decimal ERC-20s with ERC-8056 uiMultiplier for corporate actions and a Chainlink feed per token; the docs say they can be held or transferred in any compatible wallet, and no contract-level allowlist is documented — but they are Jersey-issued tokenised debt securities explicitly not offered to US persons (also restricted in Canada, UK, Switzerland, UAE), with mint/burn limited to KYB'd Authorized Participants. Backed xStocks and Ondo Stocks are likewise freely transferable but non-US only (Backed excludes US/UK/Canada/Australia; Ondo prohibits US persons and enforces via deemed representations plus KYC-gated redemption). US-eligible paths are permissioned: Dinari (SEC transfer agent + FINRA broker-dealer, Reg D 506(c) accredited and Reg A+ retail, B2B API/widget, KYB for partners, blacklist-style TransferRestrictor per its GitHub, though a July 2026 summary describes wallet whitelisting), Superstate Opening Bell (allowlist-only, Ethereum and Solana, transfer-agent-registered), and Securitize DS Protocol (whitelisted wallets). ERC-3643 (Final) is the reference model for identity-registry-gated transfers; ERC-1400 remains Draft. The SEC's 2026-09-01 transfer-agent proposal (Rule 17ad-31) contemplates smart-contract-enforced restrictive legends. Inference: for a US-facing game, the only documented on-chain route is a Dinari-style partner integration where the issuer KYCs the player and settles to a KYC-bound wallet; otherwise fall back to a custodial ledger entry via a broker, or restrict on-chain stock delivery to non-US players using permissionless tokens.

Wallet onboarding is commoditized: Privy (free to 50k MAU, 7702-based sponsorship on Arbitrum, custom chains by request), Dynamic ($249/mo for 5k MAU), Turnkey ($0.05–$0.10/signature), Alchemy Gas Manager (8% fee, Arbitrum supported; Robinhood Chain support unconfirmed), Coinbase paymaster (Base only, 7%). Robinhood Wallet supports WalletConnect on Robinhood Chain and Arbitrum; Robinhood Connect is a fiat on-ramp with no public API docs.

Oracles: Chainlink 24/5 US Equities Streams (Jan 2026, marketStatus enum, stale-when-closed, corporate-action halts not flagged) and Pyth (24/5 via Blue Ocean, 24/7 Indices, zero EVM update fees, Hermes now needs an API key) both cover equities; neither lists Robinhood Chain for Streams/pull contracts, but Chainlink push feeds exist per Robinhood stock token.

Backend and integrity costs are low and well documented: Cloudflare DO, Supabase, PlayFab, Colyseus, Heroic; Play Integrity is free with a 10k/day default quota; App Attest quotas are unpublished; rewarded-ad SSV callbacks exist for AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin MAX, and LevelPlay with signature schemes and retry rules; KYC runs $0.80–$1.89/verification (Veriff/Sumsub); World ID (~18M Orb-verified) and Human Passport (threshold 20) give sybil resistance but World's on-chain router is not on Arbitrum. Contract patterns (OZ 5.6.1 ERC20Capped + MINTER_ROLE, Merkle claims, Sablier — already deployed on Robinhood Chain — and Superfluid) are mature; an ERC-20 audit costs $5k–$25k with 8–14 weeks end to end.
