# Topic: robinhood

## Summary

As of September 1, 2026 Robinhood runs two distinct tokenized-stock products and its own Ethereum L2, but none of it is available to US customers. (1) "Classic Stock Tokens" (launched June 30, 2025) are derivative contracts issued by Robinhood Europe UAB (Bank of Lithuania-regulated) inside the Robinhood EU app for 30 EU/EEA countries, settled on Arbitrum One, trading 24/5, zero commission ("other fees may apply"), dividends paid into the app account, grown from 200+ to 2,000+ tokens. They remain in-app; no evidence they can be withdrawn to self-custody. (2) On July 1, 2026, alongside the Robinhood Chain public mainnet, Robinhood launched separate on-chain "Stock Tokens": tokenized debt securities issued by Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Limited under a Jersey base prospectus (dated June 30, 2026; 400+ Final Terms), standard freely-transferable ERC-20s (18 decimals) held in Robinhood Wallet or any EVM wallet, traded 24/7 on DEXs (Uniswap, Rialto, Lighter, Arcus, 1inch), usable as DeFi collateral, with Chainlink price feeds and an ERC-8056 "uiMultiplier" that absorbs dividends/splits (dividends auto-reinvest by raising the multiplier, no cash payout). Minting/redemption is restricted to KYB'd Authorised Participants (initially BBVI); secondary holders face no on-chain whitelist, but the offer is Reg S/non-US-persons only and distribution is restricted in the US, Canada, UK and Switzerland, plus a sanctions list. 90+ tokens at launch, 190+ by August 2026; 120+ countries. The 2025 OpenAI/SpaceX SPV-backed giveaway (~150k EU users, ~$1M) drew OpenAI's disavowal and a Bank of Lithuania clarification request; no public resolution was found.

Robinhood Chain: Arbitrum-stack L2 settling to Ethereum with blob DA, ETH gas, chain ID 4663 (testnet 46630), Blockscout explorer, FCFS single sequencer (~100 ms), permissionless contract deployment, ERC-4337, canonical Arbitrum bridge (7-day exit) plus Stargate/CCIP/Relay/Across/LiFi/0x. Testnet Feb 10, 2026 (200M+ tx), mainnet July 1, 2026. Developer support: docs.robinhood.com/chain, $1M to Arbitrum Open House 2026 (four buildathons, two Founder Houses); pays 10% of protocol net revenue to Arbitrum DAO/Dev Guild under AEP. No Robinhood native token or points program; Lighter grants 2x points for perps in Robinhood Wallet.

US regulation: the SEC's tokenized-stock "innovation exemption," expected by May 18, 2026, was delayed May 26 after Nasdaq/NYSE/Cboe pushback; the SEC's Aug 18, 2026 "Regulation Crypto Assets" proposal (Rel. 2026-76) covers crypto-asset investment contracts, not tokenized equities. Tenev publicly pressed regulators the same day; no US launch date exists. Adjacent US rails: DTCC tokenization service (no-action Dec 2025, launch Oct 2026), Nasdaq pilot approval (Mar 2026), NYSE filing (Apr 2026).

Platform/APIs: Robinhood Wallet (Robinhood Non-Custodial Ltd, Cayman) supports Ethereum, Bitcoin, Solana, Dogecoin, Arbitrum, Polygon, Optimism, Base, Robinhood Chain; funds via Robinhood Connect or Sardine. Robinhood Connect (2023) still exists as a funding rail but no developer docs were located. Only a US-only Crypto Trading API (personal API keys, May 2024) exists; no equities API or third-party OAuth. Bitstamp (closed June 2, 2025, $200M, 50+ licenses) provides REST/WebSocket/FIX APIs (400 req/s) and powered the Aug 10, 2026 UK crypto launch.

Growth mechanics: a new tiered referral program (effective Sept 1, 2026) pays fixed milestone bonuses ($50 for 1 referral up to $10,000 for 200; $15,000/yr cap) in stock chosen from 26 large-caps, replacing the lottery-style $5–$200 reward (~99% paid $5). Gold ($5/mo, 4.8M subs) gives a 3% IRA match, 3.35% APY; the 1% deposit boost ended Nov 2024; a 2% IRA transfer bonus ran Jan 8–Apr 30, 2026. Regulators have targeted Robinhood's gamification before: Massachusetts' Dec 2020 complaint (confetti, scratch-off free stock, $500 referral credits, push notifications, lists) settled Jan 18, 2024 for $7.5M with undertakings to drop games-of-chance features; separate SEC settlements of $65M (Dec 2020, PFOF/best execution) and $45M (Jan 2025).

Scale (Q2 2026): $1.31B revenue; crypto revenue $100M (−38% YoY) on $40B notional; prediction markets $156M on 13.6B contracts (Rothera JV with Susquehanna, $0.01/contract cap); 28.4M funded customers; $369B platform assets; 28M customers in 38 countries. Last disclosed CAC was $15–$20 per funded account (2020–21 S-1), with >80% of new accounts organic/referral.
