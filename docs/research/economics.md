# Topic: economics

## Summary

Ad-side economics (Sept 2026 status): rewarded video is the top-yielding format everywhere, but the money is concentrated in a few geos. Appodeal-derived 2025 data puts US rewarded eCPM at roughly $15 combined (Android ~$16.5 / iOS ~$19.6 in the older 2024 cut), UK ~$10.65, Europe ~$5.10 Android / $8.80 iOS, LATAM ~$1.90/$3.75. TopOn H1 2025 shows casual-game rewarded eCPM in "EU & North America" at $12.24 iOS and roughly $8.90 Android, with midcore titles ~$20 iOS. Nobody publishes NL, LT, FR or DE rewarded eCPMs separately in accessible form; treat continental EU as $5-9 blended and Lithuania as a Tier-2/3 EU market at the low end. Idle games are among the heaviest rewarded-video consumers (Appodeal: 73.2 rewarded videos per user over a ~7-month window, only Merge-3 higher), and practitioner guidance is 2-4 rewarded impressions per DAU per day. Genre ARPDAU benchmarks cluster at $0.03-$0.10 for casual and $0.15-$0.50 for hybrid-casual; a US-heavy idle title doing 3 rewarded/DAU at $12 eCPM yields ~$0.036 ad ARPDAU, i.e. only a few cents per player-day to fund stock rewards. Payer-conversion benchmarks by genre were not found; AppMagic shows hybrid-casual ARPPU rising sharply (+71% App Store day-90) and Sensor Tower puts hybrid-casual puzzle/lifestyle at 59% IAP / 41% ads.

Broker acquisition economics are the real prize. Robinhood spent $399M on marketing in 2025 for +1.8M net funded customers (~$222 per net add, an upper bound) and earns $171 ARPU; Webull's F-1 shows $138.7M marketing for +0.4M net funded accounts, including $24.0M of free-stock promotions and $9.4M paid to referral partners mostly in stock; eToro spent $147M for ~+0.45M funded accounts on ~$225 net contribution per account. Public affiliate CPAs are $20-$70 per funded account (Webull) and $20-$50 (Robinhood), Robinhood's standard referral pays $5 stock ~99% of the time, and its new Tiered Referrals Program (launched Sept 1, 2026) pays $50 for a single qualifying funded referral, scaling to $10,000 for 200. Morgan Stanley paid ~$2,500 per E*TRADE account in 2020. Implication: a game that delivers KYC'd, funded broker accounts can plausibly earn $20-$70 per account from affiliate programs, and far more via direct partnership, which dwarfs the ~$1-3 of ad revenue a typical idle player generates.

Reward-app payout reality: Mistplay caps redemptions at $550/year and third parties measure $0.50-$1.50/hour; Mode Mobile's audited FY2025 1-K shows $22.2M net revenue with user redemptions buried in a $9.85M S&M line (payouts appear to be at most ~25% of revenue); ZBD's Bitcoin Miner pays 1-10 sats per session (~$0.05-0.20/day). No quantitative study of churn after payout cuts was found. Fraud: AppsFlyer's 2026 report shows affiliate/incentivized traffic runs 36x the fraud rate of SRNs, organic is 52% of fraudulent installs, and roughly half of Android finance-app installs are fake; KYC costs $1.35-$1.85 per check at Sumsub list price, ~$4 all-in.

Delivery: fractional shares are available from $1 (Robinhood, Fidelity) down to 1e-8 shares (DriveWealth); Alpaca offers a "Journal" gifting primitive but B2B pricing is unpublished. Tax: 1099-B is not required for fractional-share sales under $20 gross proceeds, and the 1099-MISC prize threshold rose to $2,000 for tax years after 2025. Stash funds its own Stock-Back (1% base); Acorns Earn and Bits of Stock are brand/merchant-funded (~0.5% back); Bumped's study showed +40% spend at rewarded brands and +100% after $5-$10 stock grants, but Bumped's site is now dead. Legally, "free" stock is a sale "for value" under Section 2(a)(3) (SEC 1999 no-action letters), and SEC staff in 2023 challenged an issuer's free-share incentive under Reg M Rules 101/102 until it was terminated; open-market purchases of a sponsor's own stock implicate Rule 10b-18 (25% ADTV) and Reg M restricted periods. Token side: 2025 saw >$1.4B of revenue-funded buybacks; recommended designs commit 20-50% of net revenue with daily pacing at 2-5% of volume; points-to-token programs give 24-54% of supply to community; game tokens allocate 20-34% to player rewards; MM loan/option deals take 1-3% of circulating supply for 6-12 months.
