## legal-us (35 findings; skeptic1={"confirmed":19,"partially_correct":15,"unverifiable":1} skeptic2={"confirmed":20,"partially_correct":15})

Status as of 2026-09-01. (1) Securities delivery: fractional shares can only be delivered into a brokerage account opened at an SEC-registered broker-dealer/FINRA member (Stash: Stash Capital LLC introducing + Apex Clearing; Bits of Stock/Grifin/Bumped: Apex; Acorns: Acorns Securities). Every live program requires the recipient to open and KYC a brokerage account (CIP: name, DOB, address, TIN; effectively 18+) and the sponsor/partner funds the shares; none is structured as a chance-based sweepstakes except Robinhood-style referral stock, which uses disclosed odds (99% $5 / 0.9% $10 / 0.1% $200, $1,500/yr cap, 3-day sell lock, 30-day withdrawal lock). The 1999 SEC "free stock" orders hold that stock given for registering, supplying data or referring others is a "sale for value" under Securities Act 2(a)(3) — an issuer cannot give away its own securities for gameplay; giving away third-party listed stock bought on the open market through a broker avoids Section 5 but triggers FINRA 2210 communications rules, the 2021 SEC DEP concern about "games, streaks, prizes... free stock", the Massachusetts gamification precedent ($7.5M, 2024), and FINRA Rule 2040/Exchange Act 15(a) if the broker pays the game operator per account (finder/unregistered-broker risk). (2) Gambling: prize + chance + consideration; consideration can be non-monetary ("considerable time or effort"), so randomized rewards for gameplay are the highest-risk design; deterministic, fixed-ratio rewards remove "chance" and are the only form Google Play permits for game loyalty programs. NY/FL require registration and bonding above $5,000 (NY 30 days, FL 7 days); RI $500 for retail-only. Eleven-plus states banned dual-currency sweepstakes casinos in 2025-26 (CA AB 831 effective 1/1/2026 with vendor liability), and Washington's "thing of value" cases (Big Fish $155M, DoubleDown $415M) show even non-cashable virtual chips can be gambling. GPT apps (Mistplay, Mode) are 18+, call points "promotional... no cash value", and reserve cancellation rights. (3) Token: the SEC-CFTC joint interpretive release (Rel. 33-11412, effective 3/23/2026) classifies crypto assets into digital commodities, digital collectibles (expressly including "in-game items"), digital tools (memberships, tickets, credentials), stablecoins and digital securities; the first three are not securities but can be sold under an investment contract that ends when purchasers no longer rely on the issuer's essential managerial efforts. Its airdrop interpretation applies only to distributions for which recipients provide no consideration — tokens paid "in exchange for" gameplay or services are expressly outside it. Proposed Regulation Crypto Assets (Rel. 33-11434, FR 8/21/2026, comments due 10/20/2026) adds a $5M/4-yr startup exemption, a $75M/12-mo fundraising exemption and a Subpart D safe harbor. A token redeemable for stock at a fixed rate is a security (custodial receipt) or, if it only tracks price, a security-based swap that cannot be sold to retail non-ECPs (SEC staff 1/28/2026). CLARITY passed the House 7/17/2025, cleared Senate Banking 5/14/2026, is on the Senate calendar with a cloture vote reported for 9/15/2026 and needs 60 votes; GENIUS is law (7/18/2025; effective no later than 1/18/2027; no yield to holders). (4) Money transmission: an "administrator" that issues and redeems convertible value is a money transmitter; SEC/CFTC-regulated persons are exempt; NY BitLicense excludes units usable only inside a game with no outside market and not convertible to fiat/crypto, and rewards-program units. A closed-loop, non-convertible token avoids MSB status; an on-chain transferable token convertible to stock or cash does not. (5) FTC: Endorsement Guides (2023), Consumer Reviews Rule (16 CFR 465, eff. 10/21/2024; incentivized reviews conditioned on sentiment banned), pending Earnings Claim Rule and 2026 earnings-claim actions; CFPB is "monitoring" game currencies. (6) App stores: Apple bars crypto apps from paying currency for downloading apps or social posting (3.1.5(v)), requires loot-box odds, requires sweepstakes be developer-sponsored with in-app rules, and permits incentives for in-app actions; Google requires fixed-ratio, non-chance loyalty rewards in games, licensed crypto exchanges (FinCEN MSB + state MT), and bans paying for a chance at NFTs. (7) Tax: prizes reportable on 1099-MISC box 3 at FMV when >= $2,000/yr (OBBBA, payments after 12/31/2025); rewards for services go on 1099-NEC; COPPA amendments in force with 4/22/2026 compliance date.

## legal-eu (45 findings; skeptic1={"confirmed":22,"partially_correct":19,"unverifiable":4})

Status as of 1 Sept 2026, from primary sources where possible. (1) MiCA: the game's own token is most naturally a "utility token" (Art 3(1)(9): access to a good/service supplied by its issuer). Art 4(3) takes an offer entirely outside Title II if the token is "offered for free", is a utility token for a good/service that "exists or is in operation", or is usable only in a "limited network"; but a token is NOT "free" if holders must hand over personal data or the offeror gets any fee or monetary/non-monetary benefit, so a sign-up-gated reward is unlikely to qualify as free and the "existing utility" limb is the safer hook. Art 4(2) (<150 persons per Member State, <EUR 1m/12 months) only waives the white paper, not Art 7 marketing rules or Art 14 conduct duties. Anyone providing exchange/custody/transfer of the token as a service needs CASP authorisation; the last grandfathering ended 1 July 2026 (ESMA 17 Apr 2026), and non-EU entities may not serve EU clients beyond narrow reverse solicitation. Art 2(4) excludes financial instruments, so the stock tokens sit under MiFID II, not MiCA. (2) MiFID II/Robinhood: Robinhood Europe UAB (Bank of Lithuania; brokerage licence 3 Apr 2025, CASP 29 May 2025, PI 12 Mar 2026) sells "Classic Stock Tokens" that its own KID (1 July 2026) calls an OTC derivative on a US share/ETP, risk class 7/7, no shareholder rights, no compensation-scheme cover, "suitable only for informed retail investors". (3) Gambling: Belgium still treats paid random rewards with monetary-value prizes as illegal gambling (fines to EUR 800k, prison); Austria (OGH 6 Ob 228/24h, 18 Dec 2025) and the Netherlands (Council of State 2022) hold that loot boxes embedded in skill games with no cash-out are not gambling; Germany requires a stake (Entgelt) and a prize of monetary value; UK "gaming" requires chance for "money or money's worth". Free-entry promotional draws are lawful in most states but are regulated (NL code: free entry, EUR 100k cap, once/year; Italy: MIMIT notice 15 days ahead, bond, fiscal rep). (4) Consumer law: UCPD Annex I items 20, 28, 31 and the 2021 guidance on loot boxes; DSA Art 25 dark-pattern ban if the game is an online platform; CPC 2025 virtual-currency principles; Digital Fairness Act proposal expected Q4 2026. Age: brokerage onboarding is adult-only; 18+ gating is required in practice. (5) UK: crypto promotions regime since 8 Oct 2023 bans "free crypto" incentives; a non-transferable redeem-only utility token is outside "qualifying cryptoasset"; tokenised stocks are not offered to UK persons and CFD rules (COBS 22.5) ban inducements; full crypto regime applies 25 Oct 2027. (6) GDPR: EDPB blockchain guidelines v2.0 (7 July 2026) say keep personal data off-chain; AMLR (from 10 July 2027) fixes 5-year KYC retention and deletion, so brokerage KYC cannot be repurposed for the game. (7) Tax: no EU state exempts rewards; Germany allows the giver to pay a 30% flat tax (Sec 37b EStG); NL charges 37.8% gaming tax on chance-based prizes >EUR 449 (organiser withholds); France taxes third-party gifts at 60%; Ireland exempts EUR 3,000/donor/year; Lithuania exempts small prizes (EUR 200) and gifts (EUR 2,500). ## idle-design (34 findings; skeptic1={"confirmed":10,"partially_correct":24})

Idle/incremental economy design has a stable canonical math core that has not changed since Kongregate's Anthony Pecorella documented it in 2015-2017, and the 2025-2026 sources mostly refine benchmarks rather than mechanics. Generators cost cost_base * r^owned with r typically 1.07 (AdVenture Capitalist lemonade) to 1.15 (Cookie Clicker buildings); production is base * owned * multipliers, so exponential costs versus linear production create deliberate 'walls' that act as a natural energy system. Milestone multipliers (x2 profit at 25 and 50 owned in AdCap; 25/50/100 'speed doublers') create 'bumps' of rapid purchases and keep older generators relevant. Prestige converts exponential growth back to a linear ladder; the exponent of the prestige-currency formula sets run length: square-root formulas (AdCap angels = 150*sqrt(lifetime/1e15), Realm Grinder) need ~4x earnings to double, Cookie Clicker's cube root (floor(cbrt(lifetime/1e12)), +1% CpS per level) needs ~8x, and Egg Inc's ~1/7 exponent needs ~128x, which pushes players toward repeated short runs. Layered games (Antimatter Dimensions) gate each new layer at the IEEE-754 double limit 1.8e308 and give roughly x10 (IP) or x5 (EP) per additional 1e308, which is the reason break_infinity.js (mantissa*10^exponent, up to 1e(9e15), 50-600x faster than decimal.js) and break_eternity.js (sign/layer/mag, up to 10^^1e308) exist. Offline caps cluster at 2-24h: Egg Inc ~2h (2017), MergeCiv 12h, Melvor and Tideward 24h (Melvor started at 12h; cap justified by both daily-login retention and on-device replay cost of ~30,000 actions per 24h), Idle Miner Tycoon caps by warehouse value rather than time, Clicker Heroes has no strict cap.

Benchmarks: GameAnalytics' Aug 2026 report gives mobile medians D1 ~22% / D7 ~4% / D30 ~0.75%, ~3.9 sessions/day of ~3.3 min, with no genre cut; other 2026 aggregators put medians at 26/10/3-4 and simulation at 30/8.7/3. Idle developers report much higher D1 (Kolibri 63-81%, AdCap ~60% D1 / ~50% D7 on web in 2015). Kolibri's Idle Miner Tycoon earns ~60% from ads with ~8 rewarded ads per player per day and ~9.5-10 min sessions; Appodeal measured 73 rewarded videos per idle user over Jun 2024-Jan 2025. 2026 US rewarded-video eCPMs are ~$16.5 Android / ~$19.6 iOS (tier-1 casual $12-25, tier-2 $6-14, tier-3 $1-5; Q4 +30-50%), completion ~95%. Payer conversion is 1.8% globally with 2-5% as a common target; historical idle web titles hit 3-10%. Ad-frequency consensus: first interstitial only after 60-90s, 1-2 interstitials per session (hard cap 4-6, 2-3 min apart; >3 per session costs 20-30% D7), 2-6 rewarded views per DAU with 40-70% opt-in.

Monetization patterns that hold up: permanent x3 multipliers early, cash infusions as a % of current rate, instant prestige, rewarded 2x/time-skip boosts, ad-removal subscriptions ($0.99/7 days, $3.99/30 days in Cats & Soup), and $9.99 monthly battle passes tied to event content (Idle Miner Tycoon; critics called it 'tacked on'). Event content is the strongest LiveOps lever (event mines often doubled weekend revenue; AdCap only got events after adding a PlayFab backend post-launch).

Anti-cheat: device wall-clock is user-editable; Android elapsedRealtime (monotonic since boot, counts deep sleep) plus server timestamps are the standard defenses, but Unity explicitly warns that server time alone does not stop modified clients, so state-affecting rewards need server-side sanity checks (affordability, rate limits, plausible progress per elapsed second). No public idle-specific case study of deterministic replay validation was found; the inference from Tideward's ~30k actions/24h is that server-side replay of an input log is cheap enough for idle games.

## p2e-lessons (36 findings; skeptic1={"partially_correct":29,"confirmed":7})

Across every named title, the same failure signature recurs: an uncapped or player-count-scaled reward currency (SLP, GST, BERRY, HMSTR points, NOT taps) whose only demand was new-player inflow or speculative belief in future price. Once inflow slowed, sinks (breeding, sneaker repair, crafting) collapsed faster than emissions because spending required optimism while earning required only presence. Every token in the set now trades 95-99.9% below its all-time high as of 1 Sept 2026: SLP -99%, GST from $7.80 to ~$0.0013, HMSTR -98.3%, NOT -98.4%, CATI -95.9%, PIXEL -99.5%, BIGTIME -99.3%, PRIME -99.2%, ILV -99.9%, GUN -97.4%, SONIC -98.2%, BLUM -99.2%, DOGS -97.5%, X Empire -98.6%. DAU followed price: Axie 2.7M DAU (Nov 2021) to <500K; Hamster Kombat 300M claimed users to 48.5M MAU within five weeks of TGE, losing ~1M users/day; Ronin average DAU fell 47.6% QoQ in Q2 2025 and active addresses fell ~70% across 2025 as Pixels faded from a 1.6M-DAU peak.

Bot/sybil share was consistently a large fraction of reported users: Pixels' CEO estimated 40% of daily active wallets were bots (Nov 2023), an outside CEO estimated 70% (May 2024), Pixels banned 750K accounts in one week; Hamster Kombat banned 2.3M accounts pre-airdrop and Telegram-verified users (155M) were about half the claimed 300M; Sky Mavis halted all SLP ranked emissions on 7 Jan 2026 explicitly because a shrinking player base plus unchanged rewards created bot-farming at scale. Removing bots historically cut DAUW by up to 70% (Splinterlands, Alien Worlds), so headline DAU in reward games should be discounted heavily.

The teams' own stated lessons converge: Notcoin's founders (May 2025) said tap-to-earn is "probably dead because [it is] not sustainable" and that farm-motivated users leave immediately after extracting; Illuvium's CEO admitted being "too heavy on micro-transactions while lacking in basic gameplay"; Sky Mavis reframed SLP as a "reward currency requiring stable value" with a 2% deflation target and a USDC-funded stability fund rather than an appreciating asset. Delphi Digital's 2025-26 work found participation dropped as soon as emissions slowed, that burns are "closer to a symbolic gesture," that performance-gated unlocks work, and that a revenue-weighted token portfolio returned +30.6% (Jan 2025-May 2026) while BTC fell 17.2%.

What survivors did differently: (1) separate the soft currency from the tradeable token and gate withdrawals (Pixels BERRY vs PIXEL with $35 VIP and 20-50% "Farmer Fee"; Sweatcoin points vs SWEAT token; Big Time's non-crypto Time Crystals); (2) tie payouts to revenue rather than to player count (Off The Grid commits 30% of gross revenue to buy back GUN for performance-based player distribution; Parallel funds prize pools from half of a 10% NFT royalty plus battle-pass buybacks and caps PRIME earning at 5 wins/day; Illuvium Staking V3 releases ILV only at 150 ETH revenue milestones; Pixels targets Return-on-Reward-Spend >1.0); (3) hard caps per player per day (Parallel 5 wins, Gods Unchained 10 games, Mistplay 2 hrs/game and ~$550/yr); (4) sell the game first (Gigaverse: ~$10 entry, ~37% paying, $3M in 111 days, no token; Big Time: $100M revenue with zero token sales). Regulation moved in favour of pure utility/collectible tokens: the SEC/CFTC joint interpretive release of 17 March 2026 classes in-game items and utility tokens as non-securities unless marketed with profit expectations from issuer effort. In the Philippines, P2E income has been taxable since Aug 2021 and the BSP VASP moratorium was extended indefinitely in Aug 2025.

For a game that pays out real assets, the best-documented payout benchmark is ZBD's published guidance of a reward budget at 10-20% of ad revenue, enforced with daily withdrawal caps, device attestation, VPN detection and light KYC at first cash-out. Mistplay pays roughly $0.003 per unit, $0.50-$3 per hour, and has paid $150M total over ~10 years against 40M+ MAU. Fraud baselines: AppsFlyer's 2026 report puts ~22% of non-organic installs under fraud signals, casual-game Android at 18-22%, affiliate/rewarded-type sources near 40% versus ~1% for self-reporting networks; offerwall scrub rates run 10-25% in tier-1 geos.

## economics (36 findings; skeptic1={"partially_correct":26,"confirmed":10})

Ad-side economics (Sept 2026 status): rewarded video is the top-yielding format everywhere, but the money is concentrated in a few geos. Broker acquisition economics are the real prize.
